Western Star

Common electronic controller families found across modern Western Star heavy-duty trucks.

VEHICLE

CPC / Vehicle Powertrain Controller

Coordinates vehicle and powertrain information on applicable Detroit-equipped architectures and communicates with other controllers over the vehicle network.

ENGINE

MCM / Engine Controller

Detroit-equipped trucks may use an MCM for engine management, fueling, protection and engine diagnostics. Other engine installations use their own ECM architecture.

EMISSIONS

ACM / Aftertreatment Controller

On applicable Detroit systems, manages aftertreatment functions and related DEF/SCR/DPF inputs, outputs and diagnostics.

TRANSMISSION

TCM / Transmission Controller

Controls automatic or automated transmission operation, shift strategy and network communication when equipped.

CAB / BODY

SAM / sSAM / Multiplex Controllers

Depending on generation, cab and chassis electrical loads may be managed by SAM-family or newer multiplex controller architecture.

NETWORK

Central Gateway

Routes communication between vehicle networks and controller groups on architectures that use a dedicated gateway.

DISPLAY

Instrument / Driver Display

Presents vehicle information, warnings and network-distributed operating data to the driver.

BRAKES

ABS / ESC ECU

Monitors wheel-speed inputs and controls anti-lock braking and stability functions when equipped.

SAFETY

ADAS Radar / Camera Controllers

Supports collision mitigation and other driver-assistance functions depending on model and installed safety package.

CONNECTED

Telematics / Connectivity

Provides connected-vehicle, diagnostic and fleet communication functions on equipped trucks.

Important: Western Star controller names and population vary by model, generation, model year, engine, transmission, vocational equipment and electrical architecture. A 47X, 49X or 57X may not use the same module set as an older truck. Confirm the VIN/build and current OEM service information before replacement or programming.
