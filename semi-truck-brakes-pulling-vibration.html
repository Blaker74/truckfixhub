🔎 PROVE WHY

Pull and vibration tell you braking force is not behaving evenly. They do not tell you which component caused it.

↔️ Pulls left or right

Compare brake response and wheel-end condition side to side. Look for evidence of one brake applying differently, contamination, mechanical or foundation-brake problems, wheel-end concerns, or steering and suspension conditions that become obvious under braking.

📳 Steering wheel shakes

Record whether vibration exists only during braking, changes with speed, or is present while cruising too. Brake-related vibration can overlap with tire, wheel, hub, bearing, steering and suspension symptoms.

🚛 Whole truck or combination shakes

Identify whether the disturbance comes from tractor, trailer, one axle or multiple positions. A combination-wide complaint should not be forced into a single-wheel diagnosis without evidence.

🔧 Started after brake or wheel-end service

Inspect what was disturbed first. Verify assembly, adjustment, wheel-end condition and component movement using the correct procedure for the installed brake system.

Compare positions before replacing parts. Side-to-side differences are strong evidence in a brake pull complaint.

🛑 SAFETY: Pulling, shaking, grabbing, overheating or uneven braking can make a vehicle unsafe to operate. Stop for severe or changing brake behavior. Secure the vehicle before inspection and verify exact brake inspection, adjustment and service limits for the installed components.
