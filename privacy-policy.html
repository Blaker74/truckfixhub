PRIVACY POLICY

Your information should stay understandable.

This page explains the kinds of information that may be processed when you use TruckFixHub and how advertising services such as Google AdSense may use cookies and related technologies.

Effective October 20, 2026

Information you choose to provide

TruckFixHub may process information you voluntarily provide when using account features, forms, community tools, support features, saved vehicle information, customer tools, appointments or other interactive services. The information collected depends on the feature you choose to use.

Private account information is intended to support the feature for which it was provided. Do not submit sensitive information through public community areas.

Technical and usage information

Like most websites, TruckFixHub and service providers used to operate the site may receive technical information such as browser or device information, IP address, page requests, timestamps and general usage or diagnostic information. This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

TruckFixHub uses or intends to use Google AdSense to support free public content. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to TruckFixHub or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Third parties may place and read cookies in your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers as a result of ad serving. Depending on your location, choices and applicable requirements, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can learn about or control personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner-sites information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential site functions, security, preferences, measurement and advertising. Google AdSense pages can contain ad tags that may cause advertising cookies or related technologies to be used. Where consent is required, TruckFixHub will use appropriate consent controls before using technologies that require consent.

Accounts and private features

Some TruckFixHub tools require sign-in because they may contain information intended only for that user, such as saved VIN information, customer information, appointments or other private workspace data. Users are responsible for protecting their login credentials and for choosing what information they enter into these features.

Service providers and sharing

TruckFixHub may use third-party service providers for hosting, authentication, databases, artificial-intelligence features, security, analytics, communications and advertising. Information may be processed by those providers as necessary to deliver their services and subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

TruckFixHub does not intentionally provide personally identifiable information to Google for ad targeting. Public page URLs and public content should not contain private account information.

Your choices

You may choose not to use optional account or community features. Browser settings can also be used to manage or block cookies, although doing so may affect some website functions. Advertising choices may also be available through Google Ads Settings and any consent or privacy message displayed on the site.

Children

TruckFixHub is a heavy-duty trucking information and business utility website and is not directed to children under 13. We do not intentionally design advertising or account features for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TruckFixHub adds or changes features, service providers or legal/privacy requirements. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.
