0️⃣ Trailer does not receive air

First verify the tractor itself has adequate air and normal supply behavior. Then work down the path: tractor supply/control, hose and connection, then trailer-side supply plumbing and controls. Do not jump directly to a trailer valve because the trailer will not charge.

🔴 Trailer supply control will not stay in

Treat this as protection behavior until proven otherwise. Record tractor pressure, whether pressure falls when trailer supply is commanded, whether an audible leak appears, and whether the symptom changes with another trailer. Do not defeat or hold a protection control in place.

🔗 Leak at hose or coupling area

Prove the exact source. Coupling seals, hose assemblies, fittings and nearby plumbing can put the sound in the same area. Secure the combination before hands-on inspection.

🚛 Only one trailer causes the problem

If the same tractor works normally with another known-good trailer, that comparison is strong evidence toward this trailer or its connection. It is not absolute proof of one particular trailer component.

🛑 SAFETY: Trailer air-supply and protection systems are safety-critical. Do not bypass, defeat or mechanically hold protection controls in place. Chock and secure the tractor and trailer before inspection. A combination that cannot properly supply, hold or control trailer air should not be operated until the defect is corrected.
