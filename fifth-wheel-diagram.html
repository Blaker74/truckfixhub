🚛 FIFTH WHEEL

The trailer connection is simple: plate, kingpin, jaws, lock.

1️⃣ TRAILER APPROACH
Trailer height and alignment must allow a proper coupling
↓
2️⃣ FIFTH WHEEL PLATE
Supports the trailer upper coupler
↓
3️⃣ KINGPIN ENTERS THROAT
Kingpin moves into the locking mechanism
↓
4️⃣ JAWS / LOCK CLOSE
Locking parts capture the kingpin
↓
5️⃣ VISUAL CHECK + TUG TEST
Verify the connection before moving the vehicle
🔒 Won’t lock?
Check trailer height/alignment, dirt or damage, jaw position, release mechanism and proper lubrication.
🔓 Won’t release?
Remove trailer load/tension from the lock as appropriate, then inspect release linkage and locking mechanism.
↔️ Too much movement?
Inspect kingpin, jaws, bushings, brackets, mounting hardware and adjustment according to the fifth-wheel manufacturer.
🧴 Dry / hard steering feel?
Inspect plate lubrication and confirm the trailer contact surface and fifth wheel are in serviceable condition.
🧠 Simple rule: never trust the handle position alone. LOOK at the jaws around the kingpin and verify the coupling.
⚠️ An improperly coupled trailer can separate from the tractor. Follow the fifth-wheel and vehicle manufacturer’s coupling/inspection procedure before moving.
