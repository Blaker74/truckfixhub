PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What is private

TruckFixHub provides account-based private organizational tools. Information you save in My Trucks, My Parts, My Notes, My Events, and My Warranties is associated with your signed-in account and is not intended to be displayed publicly.

What is public

Information you intentionally post to the TruckFixHub Community is public. This may include your community nickname, questions, replies, photos, videos, and other information you choose to share. Do not post confidential, personal, customer, vehicle, business, or other sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

Account and service information

TruckFixHub may process account information and technical information needed to sign users in, provide requested features, protect the website, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service. Authentication, database storage, hosting, email, analytics, advertising, or other website functions may be provided by third-party service providers used to operate TruckFixHub.

Cookies and advertising

TruckFixHub may use cookies or similar technologies needed for website functions and may use advertising services such as Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. Visitors can manage cookies through their browser and applicable advertising-provider controls.

Diagnostic searches

TruckFixHub’s public diagnostic search is designed to route visitors to relevant TruckFixHub guides. Visitors should avoid entering confidential or personally identifying information into public search fields or community posts.

Your responsibility

Keep your account credentials secure and sign out when using a shared device. Do not share passwords by email or in the Community.

Questions

For privacy or account questions, contact support@truckfixhub.com.
