Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026. Operator: Blake Pierce / TruckFixHub. Contact: support@truckfixhub.com.

Public guide pages

You can read the public educational guides without an account. The simplified guide pages do not load advertising or analytics scripts and do not ask you to submit information through a website form. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for hosting, security, and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, your address, message, and attachments are processed through our email providers to respond and handle the issue. Do not send sensitive information. We retain correspondence as needed to address the request, maintain relevant records, and meet applicable obligations. Ask us about access, correction, or deletion using the contact address above.

Legacy account and community features

Older bookmarks may still open account tools or community pages outside the simplified guide navigation. These features use Supabase for authentication and storage and may store account/session information in your browser. Private saved records are intended to be associated with your account. Information intentionally posted to community conversations is public; do not post private VINs, customer details, or personal information. See Supabase's privacy information. Contact us to request assistance with an account or removal of your information.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense is a planned funding source. The current simplified public guide pages retain an ownership-verification meta tag but do not load Google advertising code or set advertising cookies. The presence of an ads.txt file does not itself serve ads.

If Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers to serve and measure ads. Google's advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Before enabling advertising, we must configure the applicable consent and privacy choices, including a Google-certified consent platform where required.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Additional participating vendors and controls will be disclosed if introduced. A browser's cookie controls can also affect website functionality.

External links

Manufacturer resources and other external sites operate under their own privacy policies. Following a link sends your browser to that provider. We do not receive access to your manufacturer accounts.

Sharing and changes

Service providers process information needed to host the website and operate requested features. We may disclose information when legally required or needed to protect the service. We do not sell private saved records to advertisers. We will update this policy when the site's practices materially change.
