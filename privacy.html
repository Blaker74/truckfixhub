PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Your private workspace

TruckFixHub provides account-based organizational tools. Information you save in private tools, including VIN records, parts information, call notes, calendar events and warranty information, is associated with your account and is not intended to be publicly displayed.

Public Community

Information you intentionally post to the TruckFixHub Community is public. Do not post confidential, personal or sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

Accounts and service data

TruckFixHub may process account information and technical information needed to provide, secure and improve the service. Authentication and data storage are provided through service providers used to operate the website.

Advertising and cookies

TruckFixHub may use advertising services such as Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertising where permitted. Visitors can manage cookies through their browser and applicable Google advertising controls.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact support@truckfixhub.com.
