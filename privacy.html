PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What is private

TruckFixHub provides account-based private organizational tools. Information you save in My Trucks, My Parts, My Notes, My Events, and My Warranties is associated with your signed-in account and is not intended to be displayed publicly.

What is public

Information you intentionally post to the TruckFixHub Community is public. This may include your community nickname, questions, replies, photos, videos, and other information you choose to share. Do not post confidential, personal, customer, vehicle, business, or other sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

Account and service information

TruckFixHub may process account information and technical information needed to sign users in, provide requested features, protect the website, troubleshoot problems, measure site performance, and improve the service. Authentication, database storage, hosting, email, analytics, advertising, or other website functions may be provided by third-party service providers used to operate TruckFixHub.

Cookies and advertising

TruckFixHub may use cookies and similar technologies for website functions, analytics, security, and advertising. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are used, third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on a visitor's browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers as a result of ad serving on this website.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to TruckFixHub or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Visitors may manage personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and may use privacy or consent controls presented on TruckFixHub where applicable.

Sponsorships and paid placements

TruckFixHub may accept sponsorships, advertisements, products, services, discounts, or other business consideration from companies that serve the heavy-duty trucking industry. Paid placements and material relationships are intended to be identified clearly where they appear. A sponsor does not receive access to private account information merely because it sponsors or advertises on TruckFixHub.

TruckFixHub does not intend to sell a user's private My Trucks, My Parts, My Notes, My Events, or My Warranties content to sponsors for their independent marketing use. If TruckFixHub later introduces a materially different data practice, this policy should be updated before that practice begins.

Consent and privacy choices

Privacy and consent requirements vary by location. TruckFixHub may display consent or privacy messages and provide choices about advertising cookies, personalized advertising, or related data processing where required or appropriate. TruckFixHub intends to use advertising consent technology that meets applicable Google publisher requirements for regions where such consent management is required.

Diagnostic searches

TruckFixHub's public diagnostic search is designed to route visitors to relevant TruckFixHub guides. Visitors should avoid entering confidential or personally identifying information into public search fields or community posts.

Your responsibility

Keep your account credentials secure and sign out when using a shared device. Do not share passwords by email or in the Community.

Questions

For privacy or account questions, contact support@truckfixhub.com.
