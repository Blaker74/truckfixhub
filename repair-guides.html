Important repair notice

TruckFixHub provides educational troubleshooting information and workflow tools. Fault codes can have multiple causes and definitions can vary by engine, OEM and calibration. Do not replace components solely because a code mentions them. Follow OEM diagnostic procedures and use a qualified heavy-duty repair facility when appropriate. Stop operating a vehicle when continued operation may be unsafe or could cause additional damage.
