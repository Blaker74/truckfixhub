What is the cooling system doing?

Choose the strongest symptom. Temperature, coolant level, pressure and contamination each point diagnosis in a different direction.

🔥OverheatingTemperature climbs at idle, under load, on grades or at highway speed.💧Losing CoolantExternal leak, unexplained coolant loss, overflow or repeated low-coolant warning.🎈Building PressureHard hoses, coolant pushed from reservoir or rapid pressure after startup.🌀Fan ProblemFan will not engage, stays engaged, cycles incorrectly or airflow seems weak.🥶Runs Too ColdEngine does not reach expected temperature or cab heat remains weak.🧪Coolant ContaminationOil in coolant, coolant in oil, unusual residue or unexplained fluid mixing.
Diagnostic rule: Overheating is a symptom, not a component. Confirm coolant level and condition, airflow, fan operation, circulation, restrictions, pressure behavior and operating conditions before replacing parts.
Safety: Never remove a pressurized cooling-system cap when hot. Hot coolant and steam can cause severe burns. Follow the vehicle and engine manufacturer's pressure-test and service procedures.
