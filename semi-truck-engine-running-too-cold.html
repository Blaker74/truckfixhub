Semi Truck Engine Running Too Cold?

If coolant temperature stays low or warm-up takes unusually long, compare the symptom with operating conditions before assuming a thermostat failure.

Useful clues

  • Ambient temperature and highway speed
  • Whether temperature rises at idle or under load
  • Cab heater performance
  • Cooling fan behavior
  • Displayed temperature versus diagnostic data

Thermostat operation is one possibility, but fan control, temperature sensing and operating conditions can also affect the result.
