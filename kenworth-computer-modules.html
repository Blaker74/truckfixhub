Kenworth

Common electronic controller families found across modern Kenworth heavy-duty trucks.

CAB / VEHICLE

CECU / Vehicle Electronics

Cab and vehicle multiplex functions may be managed through central electronic control architecture depending on model year and configuration.

ENGINE

Engine ECM

Controls engine fueling, torque, protection and engine-related diagnostics. Exact controller depends on the installed engine.

EMISSIONS

Aftertreatment Controller

Coordinates emissions-system functions such as DEF/SCR and aftertreatment monitoring where the engine architecture uses a separate controller.

TRANSMISSION

TCM / TECU

Controls automated or automatic transmission functions, shifting and network communication when equipped.

CHASSIS

Chassis / Multiplex Controllers

Handles chassis inputs and outputs, lighting and option-dependent multiplex functions. Names and population vary by electrical architecture.

BRAKES

ABS / ESC ECU

Monitors wheel-speed inputs and controls anti-lock braking and stability functions when equipped.

DISPLAY

Instrument / Digital Display

Provides driver information, warning indications and network-distributed vehicle data.

SAFETY

ADAS Radar / Camera Controllers

Supports collision mitigation, lane and other driver-assistance functions depending on installed equipment.

BODY

Body Builder / Remote I/O

Option-dependent controllers and interfaces support vocational body equipment, PTO and auxiliary functions.

CONNECTED

Telematics / Connectivity

Provides connected-vehicle, diagnostic and fleet communication functions on equipped trucks.

Important: There is no single universal Kenworth module list. Controller names and population vary by T680/T880/W990 and other models, model year, engine, transmission, safety package, vocational equipment and electrical architecture. Confirm the VIN/build and current OEM service information before replacement or programming.
