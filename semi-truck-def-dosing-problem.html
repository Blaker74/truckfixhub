💧 DEF DOSING PROBLEM

DEF dosing requires the correct fluid, supply, pressure, temperature conditions, control and doser operation. Start with the exact fault—not the most expensive component.

1️⃣ DEF CONDITION: Verify appropriate DEF quantity and evaluate quality/contamination using manufacturer-approved methods. Do not judge fluid condition by appearance alone.
2️⃣ SUPPLY SYSTEM: Follow the exact application’s tests for tank, pickup, pump, lines, pressure and heating/thawing functions where applicable.
3️⃣ DOSER / DEPOSIT: Inspect the dosing area according to service information. Crystallized deposits may be evidence, but diagnose why correct dosing or decomposition was not occurring.
4️⃣ ELECTRICAL / CONTROL: Check power, grounds, wiring, connectors and command response for the relevant pump, heater and dosing components.
5️⃣ CONDITIONS TO DOSE: The controller may intentionally withhold dosing when required temperatures or operating conditions are not met. Determine command versus actual behavior.
6️⃣ VERIFY SCR OPERATION: After the proven fault is corrected, complete the OEM verification routine and confirm dosing/SCR performance.
DEF QUALITY → SUPPLY / PRESSURE → DOSER → ELECTRICAL → ENABLE CONDITIONS → SCR RESPONSE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Follow manufacturer handling and test procedures for DEF and hot exhaust components. Do not bypass emissions controls.
