💨 AIR & BRAKES

Follow the air from where it is made to where it does the work.

1️⃣ COMPRESSOR
Makes the air
↓
2️⃣ DRYER / GOVERNOR
Controls and cleans the air supply
↓
3️⃣ AIR TANKS
Store the air
↓
4️⃣ VALVES & LINES
Send air where it needs to go
↓
5️⃣ BRAKE CHAMBERS
Turn air pressure into brake movement
🪫 Builds air slowly?
Start upstream: compressor, leaks, dryer, governor.
💨 Loses air?
Listen and soap-test lines, fittings, valves and chambers.
🛑 One brake acts wrong?
Compare that wheel end with the others.
⚠️ Whole system acts wrong?
Think supply pressure and common valves first.
⚠️ Air brakes are safety-critical. Never work under an unsupported vehicle, and never rely on air pressure alone to hold a vehicle.
