👇 Split the complaint first

SUPPLY: Slow/no build, rapid pressure loss and repeated dryer cycling begin upstream with leakage and supply/control testing.
APPLICATION: A leak that appears only when the pedal is applied points toward circuits activated during service braking.
RELEASE: Parking brake that will not release requires sufficient air, a release command, delivery to spring chambers and free foundation brakes.
BRAKING FORCE: Weak brakes, pulling and long stopping distance require side-to-side and tractor/trailer comparison of delivery, chamber movement and foundation brake condition.
STROKE / ADJUSTMENT: Excessive stroke is a symptom to diagnose, not something to hide with repeated manual adjustment.
HOT WHEEL: Separate pneumatic/mechanical brake drag from wheel-end or tire heat.
ABS: Save the complete electronic fault and follow power, wiring, sensor/signal and mechanical wheel-end evidence separately from pneumatic complaints.
