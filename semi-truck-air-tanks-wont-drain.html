Semi Truck Air Tank Won't Drain or Has Water?

Drain problems or unusual water/oil accumulation can provide clues about the reservoir drain system, air dryer and upstream air supply.

Work safely around compressed air

Reservoirs contain stored energy. Follow manufacturer procedures for depressurizing and servicing pneumatic components. Do not remove pressurized fittings or components.

What did you find?

  • Drain valve will not open or flow
  • Excessive water
  • Oily residue
  • Debris at a drain
  • Only one reservoir affected
  • Problem began after dryer/compressor service

Water in the tanks

Some moisture can occur depending on operating conditions, but unusual accumulation may warrant inspection of dryer performance, service condition and the rest of the air system according to manufacturer guidance.

Oil contamination

Oil in the air system can have multiple causes and should be evaluated in context. Do not diagnose the compressor from reservoir residue alone; consider manufacturer limits, operating history and component-specific inspection procedures.
