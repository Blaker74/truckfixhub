Semi Truck Air Dryer Not Purging?

No purge sound can be a dryer symptom, but first determine whether the air system actually reaches the conditions that should command a purge.

Air-system warning

Air dryer, governor and compressor behavior are part of the vehicle's pneumatic system. Diagnose abnormal operation using correct component and vehicle procedures.

Start with the system behavior

  • Does air pressure reach the normal governed range?
  • Does the compressor unload?
  • Is there a purge at all or only a weak/delayed event?
  • Any continuous leakage at the dryer?
  • Recent dryer, governor or air-line service?

Possible diagnostic areas

Depending on the dryer and vehicle configuration, technicians may inspect control signal delivery, governor operation, purge valve/components, supply and control lines, restrictions and whether the system reaches cut-out. A missing purge does not automatically mean the dryer assembly itself is defective.

Compare with continuous purging

A dryer that never purges and one that purges repeatedly are different symptoms. Document pressure behavior and timing so diagnosis starts with the correct condition.
