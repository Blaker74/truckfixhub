👇 Follow the complaint

🔑 WON’T START: First decide whether the engine cranks. No-crank and cranks-but-won’t-start are different diagnostic roads. Use the no-start hub before buying parts.
🐌 LOW POWER: Check for active derate/faults, then inspect the air/boost path, fuel supply, exhaust/aftertreatment and control information before moving into deeper mechanical testing.
🌡️ OVERHEATING: Move into cooling-system diagnosis. Verify coolant condition/level as applicable, airflow, fan operation, circulation and the exact operating condition that creates the complaint.
🐢 DERATE: Treat derate as a control strategy/result until the root cause is identified. Save the complete fault picture and determine which system caused the limitation.
🌫️ SMOKE: Smoke color and operating condition are clues, not component verdicts. Combine them with fault data, air/fuel checks and mechanical evidence.
🟠 CHECK ENGINE: Record the complete SPN/FMI or OEM fault information before clearing codes. A code reports a detected condition; it does not automatically prove the named part failed.

🔎 The engine diagnostic chain

1️⃣ Identify the exact engine, serial information, model year/emissions generation and truck application.
2️⃣ Define the complaint precisely and reproduce it safely when practical.
3️⃣ Save active/inactive faults and operating data before clearing anything.
4️⃣ Check the basics and follow the system capable of producing the symptom.
5️⃣ Perform directed tests using the correct manufacturer procedure and specifications.
6️⃣ Prove the root cause, make the repair, then recreate the original condition to verify it.
