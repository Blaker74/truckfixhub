👇 Follow the complaint

🔑 WON’T START: First decide whether the engine cranks. No-crank and cranks-but-won’t-start are different roads.
🐌 LOW POWER: Check active derate/faults, then air/boost, fuel supply, exhaust/aftertreatment and control information before deeper mechanical testing.
🌡️ OVERHEATING: Verify coolant condition/level as applicable, airflow, fan operation, circulation and the operating condition that creates the complaint.
🐢 DERATE: Treat derate as a control strategy/result until the root cause is identified.
💨 SMOKE: Color and operating condition are clues, not component verdicts. Combine them with air/fuel/fluid and mechanical evidence.
🛢️ OIL: Separate actual pressure from sensor information and actual consumption from leaks before moving toward internal engine repair.

🔎 The engine diagnostic chain

1️⃣ Identify exact engine, serial information, model year/emissions generation and truck application.
2️⃣ Define the complaint precisely and reproduce it safely when practical.
3️⃣ Save active/inactive faults and operating data before clearing anything.
4️⃣ Check basics and follow the system capable of producing the symptom.
5️⃣ Perform directed tests using correct manufacturer procedures/specifications.
6️⃣ Prove root cause, repair, then recreate the original condition to verify it.
