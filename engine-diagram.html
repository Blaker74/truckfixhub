👇 Follow the complaint

🔑 WON’T START: First decide whether the engine cranks. No-crank and cranks-but-won’t-start are different roads.
🐌 LOW POWER: Check active derate/faults, then air/boost, fuel supply, exhaust/aftertreatment and control information before deeper mechanical testing.
🌡️ OVERHEATING: Verify coolant condition/level as applicable, airflow, fan operation, circulation and the operating condition that creates the complaint.
🐢 DERATE: Treat derate as a control strategy/result until the root cause is identified.
💨 SMOKE: Color and operating condition are clues, not component verdicts. Combine them with air/fuel/fluid and mechanical evidence.
🛢️ OIL: Separate actual pressure from sensor information and actual consumption from leaks before moving toward internal engine repair.

🔎 The engine diagnostic chain

1️⃣ Identify exact engine, serial information, model year/emissions generation and truck application.
2️⃣ Define the complaint precisely and reproduce it safely when practical.
3️⃣ Save active/inactive faults and operating data before clearing anything.
4️⃣ Check basics and follow the system capable of producing the symptom.
5️⃣ Perform directed tests using correct manufacturer procedures/specifications.
6️⃣ Prove root cause, repair, then recreate the original condition to verify it.

⛰️ ENGINE BRAKE NOT WORKING?

Quick answer

First use diagnostic data to confirm the engine controller sees the engine-brake ON/level switch and that operating conditions allow engine braking. Check throttle position, clutch and brake inputs, vehicle/engine speed, active faults and OEM inhibit conditions. If the controller requests engine braking but braking does not occur, follow the engine manufacturer's electrical and mechanical test procedure downstream.

Diagnostic order

  1. Verify engine-brake master and level switch inputs.
  2. Check accelerator/throttle returns to the expected released state.
  3. Verify clutch and service-brake input status.
  4. Read active/inactive engine faults.
  5. Confirm engine speed and other enable conditions are in range.
  6. Compare engine-brake requested/commanded state with actual operation.
  7. If commanded but inactive, follow OEM wiring, actuator/solenoid and engine-brake mechanical diagnostics.

Pattern clues

No switch input: diagnose switch, wiring or controller input before opening the engine.
Works only sometimes: watch clutch, throttle and brake states during the failure for an intermittent inhibit.
Only one braking level seems weak: compare commanded stages and follow engine-specific diagnostics.
Stopped after engine or harness work: recheck disturbed connectors, harness routing and programming/configuration.
Stopped after cylinder-head, camshaft, rocker or top-end work: recent mechanical work is evidence. Verify the applicable engine-brake hardware, rocker/valvetrain installation, adjustments and timing using the exact engine manufacturer's procedure before replacing unrelated electrical parts.

🔑 DIESEL HARD START

It starts—but takes too long. Separate cranking speed, fuel delivery, control inputs and mechanical condition.

👇 Capture the pattern

1️⃣ COLD OR HOT? A cold-only complaint and a hot-soak complaint may have different causes. Record coolant/ambient temperature and soak time.
2️⃣ CRANKING SPEED: Listen and measure where supported. Low battery state, cable voltage drop, starter condition or mechanical load can reduce cranking performance.
3️⃣ FUEL SUPPLY: Verify fuel level/quality, filters, restriction, priming and possible air entry or drain-back where applicable before condemning high-pressure components.
4️⃣ SAVE FAULTS / DATA: Record OEM/SPN/FMI information and watch engine speed, pressure and temperature inputs required by the exact engine during crank.
5️⃣ COMBUSTION / MECHANICAL: If cranking and fuel/control evidence are correct, follow manufacturer-directed cylinder, compression, valve-train or timing tests.

🔎 Useful clues

AFTER SITTING: Investigate fuel drain-back/air entry and electrical state before replacing injection parts.
ONLY HOT: Compare hot cranking speed and required fuel/control data against the OEM diagnostic procedure.
WHITE SMOKE WHILE CRANKING: Fuel may be reaching cylinders but combustion quality may be poor. Use temperature, cylinder and mechanical evidence to narrow it.
NO SMOKE / NO START ATTEMPT: Verify required fuel delivery and control permission/inputs rather than guessing from exhaust appearance alone.
STARTED HARD AFTER TOP-END WORK: Recent repair history matters. If the complaint appeared immediately after cylinder-head, camshaft, injector or valvetrain work, verify what was disturbed—especially assembly, adjustments and timing—before chasing unrelated parts.

🛢️ LOW OIL PRESSURE

Treat a genuine low-oil-pressure warning as serious until proven otherwise.

🛑 First decision

Abnormal engine noise + low pressure warning: shut down safely and follow manufacturer guidance. Do not keep running the engine just to see whether the warning clears.

👇 Diagnostic path

1️⃣ VERIFY LEVEL / CONDITION. Check oil correctly for the exact engine and inspect for dilution, contamination, wrong viscosity or recent service issues.
2️⃣ SAVE FAULTS / DATA. Record pressure readings, oil temperature, RPM, active/inactive codes and when the complaint occurs—cold, hot idle, cruise or load.
3️⃣ SENSOR OR REAL PRESSURE? Inspect the sensor circuit and verify actual pressure using the manufacturer-approved method/mechanical gauge where specified.
4️⃣ IF PRESSURE IS TRULY LOW: Follow the OEM lubrication-system path: filter/bypass condition, pickup/suction path, pump/regulator and internal clearances as applicable.
5️⃣ RECENT SERVICE? Recheck oil/filter application, fill level and anything disturbed during repair before assuming an internal failure.

🔎 Pattern clues

LOW ONLY HOT AT IDLE: Verify actual pressure at the specified oil temperature/RPM before drawing conclusions.
READING DROPS OUT / JUMPS: Electrical/sensor evidence matters, but do not dismiss a warning without verifying actual pressure.
LOW AFTER OIL CHANGE: Confirm correct oil, level, filter application and installation immediately.
