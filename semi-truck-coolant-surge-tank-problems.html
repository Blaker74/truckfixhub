Coolant Surge Tank or Pressure Cap Problems?

Residue around the tank or cap can be an important clue, but coolant pushed from this area can have more than one cause.

Never open hot

A hot cooling system can release pressurized coolant and steam. Allow the system to cool and follow the manufacturer's service procedure.

Inspection clues

  • Cracks, staining or seepage at tank seams
  • Leakage around hose or sensor connections
  • Coolant residue around the pressure cap
  • Coolant expelled during high temperature or load
  • Repeated low-coolant warnings

A cap or tank can be faulty, but abnormal system pressure, overheating or another cooling-system problem may also force coolant out. Diagnose the reason before replacing parts.
