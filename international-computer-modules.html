International Truck Computer Module & ECU Identification

A technician-first repository for electronic controllers used on International LT, RH, HX, HV and MV trucks, with legacy Diamond Logic architecture included where applicable.

Configuration matters: International module content changes with model year, engine, transmission, brake system, ADAS and body equipment. This catalog identifies controller families and functions; VIN/build-specific OEM information remains the authority for exact presence, location, connectors and programming.

BCM / BC — Body Control Module

Diamond LogicCab / chassis
📷 International electrical integration documentation contains Body Control Module system diagrams and connector illustrations. OEM imagery remains linked at the source.

What it does: Central Diamond Logic vehicle/body controller. It monitors switches and sensors, controls configured outputs and exchanges information over vehicle and body-builder datalinks.

Diagnostic importance: Multiple lighting, PTO, body, switch or chassis complaints can share BCM power, ground, I/O or network causes. Diagnose circuits before replacing the controller.

International TEM / Body Builder Resources

International ECM / Engine ECM

Engine
📷 Physical controller varies with International S13/A26 or installed Cummins engine family.

What it does: Controls engine combustion, fueling, torque and engine-mounted sensors/actuators and communicates with the truck over the powertrain network.

Diagnostic importance: Always identify the engine family before applying connector, calibration or pinout information.

International Engines and Powertrains

PIM — Powertrain Interface Module

S13 powertrainInterface
📷 Current International electrical integration documentation provides PIM system block diagrams and body-builder connector signals.

What it does: On applicable S13-equipped architecture, the PIM provides powertrain/body interface functions for signals such as remote accelerator, PTO, engine-running output, tachometer and vehicle-speed output.

International Electrical Systems Integration Guides

TCU / TCM — Transmission Control Unit

Transmission
📷 Controller appearance depends on installed International T14, Eaton, Allison or other transmission configuration.

What it does: Controls automated/automatic transmission functions and exchanges torque, speed and shift information with engine and vehicle controllers.

Diagnostic importance: Verify transmission make/model before applying module identification or wiring information.

ABS / ESC ECU

BrakesStability
📷 Hardware depends on installed brake/stability system.

What it does: Processes wheel-speed and brake-system information and controls antilock braking and applicable stability functions.

Diagnostic importance: Sensor, wiring, valve, power/ground and network faults should be separated from controller failure.

Instrument Cluster / Instrument Panel Controller

Driver information
📷 Cluster appearance varies across LT, RH, HX, HV, MV and legacy International generations.

What it does: Displays gauges, warning lamps, messages and vehicle status received from electronic controllers.

Diagnostic importance: Missing gauge information may originate in the source ECU or datalink rather than the cluster.

Body Builder J1939 Engine Speed Control Module

VocationalPTO
📷 International's integration guide shows this module connected to the private Body Builder J1939 network and BCM.

What it does: Customer/body-equipment interface capable of requesting configured engine-speed/PTO functions through the Diamond Logic architecture.

International TEM / Body Builder Resources

Remote Power Modules / I/O Modules

Body equipment
📷 Legacy International electrical documentation identifies remote power modules as part of body/electrical architecture.

What it does: Provides additional configured input/output capability for vehicle or body equipment.

Diagnostic importance: Vocational body wiring and OEM chassis wiring must be separated during diagnosis.

ADAS Radar / Camera Controllers

SafetyDriver assistance
📷 Installed radar/camera hardware varies by International model, year and safety package.

What it does: Supports equipped collision mitigation, lane and other advanced driver-assistance functions.

Diagnostic importance: Verify mounting, alignment/calibration, wiring and network communication before replacing sensors/controllers.

HVAC Electronic Controller

CabHVAC
📷 Control module and panel configuration varies by cab/model generation.

What it does: Manages configured heating, ventilation and air-conditioning requests, actuators and related cab climate functions.

Telematics / Connected Vehicle Module

Telematics
📷 Hardware varies with connected-services specification and model year.

What it does: Provides equipped vehicle connectivity, diagnostic/operational data communications and fleet-service functions.

Legacy PAM / Auxiliary Modules

Legacy
📷 Older International electrical/service documentation identifies PAM and other auxiliary electronic modules on specific configurations.

What it does: Configuration-specific auxiliary control functions on earlier International platforms.

Diagnostic importance: Legacy module names must be matched to the exact chassis/year rather than applied to current LT/HX/HV architecture.

International network architecture
Powertrain J1939Connects major powertrain controllers such as engine, transmission and vehicle/body control functions.
Body Builder J1939Provides a dedicated network for configured body equipment and vocational control integration.
Diamond LogicInternational's programmable electrical architecture integrates chassis and body equipment through electronic control rather than extensive standalone relay logic.
