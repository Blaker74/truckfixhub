Coolant Temperature Rising and Falling?

Record when the gauge changes: climbing a grade, idling, highway speed, fan engagement or immediately after a load change.

Do not ignore a true overheat

If temperature enters a dangerous range or a stop-engine warning appears, follow OEM shutdown instructions. Do not remove a hot cooling-system cap.

Diagnostic clues

Fluctuation can involve coolant level or air in the system, thermostat operation, fan control, coolant circulation, temperature-sensor or wiring issues, radiator airflow and other conditions. Comparing the displayed value with appropriate diagnostic data can help distinguish an actual temperature swing from an indication problem.
