🚛 SEMI TRUCK WON’T START?

Before buying anything, answer one question.

DOES THE ENGINE CRANK?

❌ NO — IT DOESN’T CRANK

The starter does not rotate the engine normally when START is requested. Begin with battery capability, connections, power and ground paths, start command, relays/interlocks and starter-circuit diagnosis.

🔋 OPEN WON’T-CRANK GUIDE
⚡ STARTING / CHARGING SYSTEM

✅ YES — IT CRANKS

The starter turns the engine, but the engine does not run. Move away from a basic starter-circuit diagnosis and determine which required engine condition is missing.

🔑 OPEN CRANK / NO-START GUIDE
⛽ FUEL SYSTEM
🐌 IT CRANKS, BUT VERY SLOWLY: Treat slow cranking as an important clue. Verify battery capability and connections, test the positive and ground paths under load, and follow the manufacturer’s starting-system procedure before condemning the starter. If electrical delivery is proven, investigate abnormal starter or mechanical load as applicable.

🧠 Save the evidence first

1️⃣ Record all active and inactive fault codes before clearing anything.
2️⃣ Note exactly what happens during START: nothing, click, slow crank, normal crank, starts then dies, hot-only, cold-only or intermittent.
3️⃣ Record recent repairs, battery work, fuel-filter service, programming or anything disturbed before the complaint began.
4️⃣ Use the exact truck, engine, transmission, model year and engine serial information when selecting the OEM procedure.

NO CRANK and CRANKS-BUT-WON’T-START are two different diagnostic roads. Choose the road before choosing a part.
⚠️ Follow manufacturer starting and electrical safety procedures. Secure the vehicle as required, use the correct transmission/neutral or park procedure, and never bypass safety interlocks to force a crank. High-current starting circuits and high-pressure diesel fuel systems can cause serious injury.
