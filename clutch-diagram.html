🦶 CLUTCH DIAGNOSTICS

First decide what job the clutch is failing to do: RELEASE, HOLD TORQUE, ENGAGE SMOOTHLY, or RESPOND TO A COMMAND.

Pedal input or automated clutch request

2️⃣ ACTUATION
Linkage, hydraulic, air-assisted or electronic/AMT actuator as equipped
↓
3️⃣ RELEASE SYSTEM
Release bearing, fork/cross-shaft and related mechanism
↓
4️⃣ CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Disc, pressure plate, flywheel and clutch brake where applicable

5️⃣ TRANSMISSION INPUT
Torque enters—or must stop entering—the transmission

👇 Pick the failure

⚙️ WON’T DISENGAGE: If the transmission input remains driven with release commanded, inspect pedal/command, actuation, release travel, adjustment and release components before blaming the transmission.
🔥 SLIPS UNDER LOAD: True slip is engine RPM increasing without matching road speed while torque is applied. Record gear, load and temperature. Check adjustment/actuation and contamination or heat evidence before condemning the clutch assembly.
🫨 CHATTER / GRAB: Note hot versus cold, forward versus reverse, loaded versus empty. Friction surfaces, contamination, flywheel/pressure-plate condition, mounts and driveline movement can overlap.
🔊 NOISE: Pedal position matters. Record whether noise occurs pedal up, light contact, fully depressed or during engagement. That timing is stronger evidence than “clutch noise.”
🤖 AMT CLUTCH FAULT: An automated manual transmission may use an actuator and electronic control instead of a conventional driver-operated release path. Save all faults first. Verify power, grounds, air supply if used, actuator operation and required learn/calibration procedures using the exact manufacturer system.

🧠 Before adjusting or replacing anything

1️⃣ IDENTIFY THE SYSTEM. Manual clutch, hydraulic release, air-assisted release and AMT clutch systems do not share one universal adjustment procedure.
2️⃣ WATCH THE INPUT AND OUTPUT. Does the pedal or controller request release? Does the release mechanism actually move far enough?
3️⃣ SEPARATE RELEASE FROM TRANSMISSION. Hard gear engagement can be caused by a clutch that never fully releases. Prove release before opening the transmission.
4️⃣ CHECK THE CLUTCH BRAKE WHERE APPLICABLE. A clutch brake has a specific job and operating range. Diagnose and adjust it only by the exact clutch/transmission procedure.
5️⃣ VERIFY AFTER REPAIR. Slip must be retested under the original safe load; release complaints must be retested under the original stopped/shift condition.
