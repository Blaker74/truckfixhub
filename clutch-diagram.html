🦶 CLUTCH

Follow the pedal request to the transmission input.

1️⃣ CLUTCH PEDAL
The driver requests clutch release or engagement
↓
2️⃣ LINKAGE / HYDRAULIC OR AIR ASSIST
Transfers pedal movement to the clutch release system as equipped
↓
3️⃣ RELEASE MECHANISM
Moves the release bearing and related clutch components
↓
4️⃣ CLUTCH DISC + PRESSURE PLATE
Connect or disconnect engine torque from the transmission
↓
5️⃣ TRANSMISSION INPUT
Receives engine torque when the clutch is engaged
🔥 Slips under load?
Confirm engine RPM rises without a matching increase in vehicle speed. Check adjustment where applicable, clutch wear, contamination and related components before condemning one part.
⚙️ Hard to get into gear?
First determine whether the clutch fully releases. Inspect pedal travel, linkage or assist system and manufacturer-specified adjustment before blaming the transmission.
🔊 Noise with pedal movement?
Write down exactly when the noise occurs: pedal released, pedal depressed or while moving through engagement. Timing helps separate release-system, clutch and transmission-input concerns.
🫨 Chatter / grabbing?
Inspect mounts, flywheel/clutch surfaces, contamination, driveline movement and engagement-system condition. Chatter does not automatically identify the clutch disc as the cause.

Start with what the clutch is failing to do

A clutch has two basic jobs: connect engine torque to the transmission and release that connection when commanded. If it will not release, follow the pedal and release path. If it slips, focus on torque-holding ability and correct adjustment. If it chatters or makes noise, record exactly when the symptom occurs. Always use the clutch manufacturer’s inspection and adjustment specifications.
