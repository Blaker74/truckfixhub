🦶 CLUTCH

Follow the driver’s pedal movement to the transmission input.

1️⃣ CLUTCH PEDAL
Driver requests release or engagement
↓
2️⃣ LINKAGE / HYDRAULIC OR AIR ASSIST
Transfers pedal movement
↓
3️⃣ RELEASE MECHANISM
Moves the release bearing and clutch mechanism
↓
4️⃣ CLUTCH DISC + PRESSURE PLATE
Connect or disconnect engine torque
↓
5️⃣ TRANSMISSION INPUT
Receives engine power when the clutch is engaged
🔥 Slips under load?
Confirm engine RPM rises without matching road-speed increase. Check adjustment where applicable, wear, contamination and clutch condition.
⚙️ Hard to get into gear?
Check whether the clutch fully releases before blaming the transmission. Inspect pedal travel, linkage/assist and adjustment where applicable.
🔊 Noise with pedal movement?
Note whether noise appears pedal up, pedal down or during engagement. That timing helps narrow the source.
🫨 Chatter / grabbing?
Check mounts, clutch/flywheel condition, contamination, driveline movement and engagement system.
🧠 Simple rule: first decide whether the clutch problem is RELEASE, ENGAGEMENT, SLIP or NOISE.
⚠️ Clutch assemblies are heavy and contain high spring forces. Use proper service procedures and support equipment.
