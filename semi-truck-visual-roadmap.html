First: is it safe to keep going?

Stop safely for brake or steering trouble, loose wheel hardware, severe overheating, a red stop-engine warning or an uncertain trailer coupling. Arrange qualified help. These maps explain what to investigate; they do not certify a truck safe to drive.

Won’t start? Take the right branch.
