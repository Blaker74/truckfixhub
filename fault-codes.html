Before buying a part

Capture the complete SPN, FMI and source module. Check whether the fault is active or stored. Look for related codes. Verify power, grounds, connectors and wiring where relevant. Compare live data with what the truck is actually doing. Then repair, retest and prove the fault stays gone.
