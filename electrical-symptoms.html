What is the electrical system doing?

Start with the symptom, then prove power, ground and circuit integrity under load before replacing modules or components.

🔋Dead / Weak BatteriesRepeated dead battery, slow electrical wake-up or batteries discharge while parked.⚡No Charge / Low VoltageCharging warning, low system voltage or batteries do not recover while running.🧯Fuse Keeps BlowingFuse opens repeatedly, circuit shorts or component loses power after fuse replacement.💡Intermittent PowerLights, dash, accessories or modules cut in and out, reset or behave unpredictably.🔗J1939 / No CommunicationModules disappear, multiple communication faults appear or scan tool cannot communicate.🧠Module / Controller ConcernECM, CPC, ABS, transmission or another controller is suspected of losing power or communication.
Diagnostic rule: Static voltage alone does not prove a circuit. Check power and ground at the load, use voltage-drop testing when the circuit is working, inspect connectors and network integrity, and prove the circuit before condemning an electronic module.
Safety: Protect circuits from accidental shorts. Use correctly rated test equipment, follow OEM procedures, and disconnect or isolate power when the service procedure requires it. Never bypass circuit protection as a repair.
