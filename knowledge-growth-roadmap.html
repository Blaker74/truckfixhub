How The Library Keeps Growing

TruckFixHub should get bigger underneath without getting harder on top. New knowledge gets broken into tiny useful pieces and connected to the drawers where a technician will actually look for it.

THE GROWTH LOOP

❓1. FIND A GAPWhat are truck people asking?
📚2. GATHERCollect dependable information and field evidence.
🔎3. CHECKSeparate proven facts from clues and opinions.
🧒4. SIMPLIFYTurn it into plain language and pictures.
🔗5. CONNECTLink it to symptoms, codes, systems and tests.

EVERY TOPIC SHOULD GROW THESE BRANCHES

👀 WHAT YOU NOTICENormal-language symptoms, sounds, smells, leaks, warnings and driver complaints.
🧩 WHAT SYSTEM IT BELONGS TOThe smallest useful explanation of what the system is supposed to do.
🗺️ WHAT TO CHECK NEXTVisual yes/no diagnostic paths that move from easy evidence to harder evidence.
🧪 HOW TO TEST ITVoltage, pressure, temperature, movement, restriction, command and response.
⚠️ CODES THAT MAY CONNECTSPN/FMI clues and shared-cause relationships without turning codes into parts guesses.
🔧 WHAT FIXED ITField lessons from real repairs, clearly labeled until independently verified.
✅ HOW TO PROVE THE REPAIRWhat should change after the repair and what must be rechecked.
📄 EXACT INFORMATIONPoint users to vehicle/component-specific manufacturer information when exact values matter.

THE EXPANSION RULE

One new fact should never live on one lonely page. When useful knowledge is added, it should be connected anywhere it helps: symptom page, system page, diagnostic picture, fault-code path, beginner lesson and community follow-up. That is how one lesson can strengthen the whole library.

WHAT GETS BUILT FIRST

  1. High-frequency breakdowns: no-start, low power, overheating, electrical, air/brakes, aftertreatment, driveline and wheel-end complaints.
  2. Missing diagnostic branches: places where a user reaches a dead end instead of a clear next test.
  3. Repeated community questions: recurring confusion becomes a candidate for a new simple drawer or visual.
  4. Fault-code families: organize related codes by system and shared causes instead of building disconnected code pages.
  5. Beginner translations: turn technician language into plain language without removing the technical term.

THE GOAL

More knowledge underneath. Fewer choices in front of the user.

TruckFixHub does not need to look enormous. It needs to feel obvious.
