🔋 STARTING & CHARGING

Start with the batteries. Follow the power.

1️⃣ BATTERIES
Store the electrical power
↓
2️⃣ CABLES + CONNECTIONS
Carry high current to and from the batteries
↓
3️⃣ START CONTROL
Key / start signal, relay and control circuit tell the starter to work
↓
4️⃣ STARTER
Uses battery power to crank the engine
↓
5️⃣ ALTERNATOR
Recharges the batteries and supplies the electrical system while running
🔇 No crank?
Check battery state, connections, start signal, relay/control and starter feed.
🐌 Cranks slowly?
Check batteries under load, cable voltage drop, grounds, connections and starter condition.
🪫 Batteries keep dying?
Check charging output, belt/drive, connections and unwanted key-off electrical draw.
⚡ Charging warning?
Verify actual system voltage before condemning the alternator.
🧠 Simple rule: a cable can LOOK good and still fail under load. Voltage-drop testing helps find resistance in power and ground paths.
