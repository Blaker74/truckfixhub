Dash may stay normal or go dim.👆Click — But No CrankClicking proves activity, not that batteries, cables or starter can carry the load.🐢Cranks Very SlowlyPrioritize battery condition, cable/ground voltage drop, starter load and mechanical drag.🎲Starts SometimesIntermittent no-crank: connections, control circuit, interlocks, relay and starter deserve evidence-based testing.🔄Cranks Normally — Won't StartThe starter system is turning the engine. Move diagnosis toward engine start requirements.⚡Dash / Electrical Acts StrangeModules reset, gauges drop, lights flicker or communication faults appear during the complaint.
Diagnostic rule: Do not condemn the starter because it clicks. Do not condemn batteries from static voltage alone. Test the circuit while it is being asked to work.
Safety: Secure the vehicle before starting-system tests. Starting circuits carry very high current. Follow OEM procedures and do not bypass neutral, clutch or other safety interlocks as a routine shortcut.
