OEM-document rule: TruckFixHub does not host or present copied manufacturer wiring diagrams as its own. Exact OEM schematics, connector views, pinouts, service specifications and repair procedures should be obtained from the vehicle or component manufacturer's authorized/current service-information source and matched to the exact VIN, model year, engine, options and harness configuration.

Why this page changed

TruckFixHub's value is the diagnostic structure we build—not republishing somebody else's service documents. Our diagrams are simplified educational troubleshooting aids designed to help identify what should be tested next. They are not substitutes for exact OEM wiring information.
