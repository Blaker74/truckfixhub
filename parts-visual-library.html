Air Dryer

PHOTO SLOT — U-JOINT

U-Joint

What it does: Transfers torque between driveline sections while allowing operating-angle movement.

When to look here: Driveline clunk, vibration, rust dust, looseness, binding or repeat joint failure.

Related parts: Driveshaft • Yokes • Carrier bearing • Slip joint

PHOTO SLOT — NOx SENSOR

NOx Sensor

What it does: Measures nitrogen-oxide levels in the exhaust so the aftertreatment system can monitor and control emissions performance.

When to look here: NOx-related faults, SCR efficiency complaints, warning lights or derate diagnosis.

Related parts: DEF doser • SCR catalyst • Exhaust temperature sensors • DPF

PHOTO SLOT — STARTER

Starter Motor

What it does: Uses battery power to turn the engine fast enough for it to start.

When to look here: No crank, click/no crank, intermittent crank or slow-crank diagnosis.

Related parts: Batteries • Cables • Solenoid • Starter control circuit

PHOTO SLOT — CARRIER BEARING

Carrier Bearing

What it does: Supports a multi-piece driveshaft and helps keep the driveline positioned correctly.

When to look here: Vibration, support movement, damaged rubber, bearing noise or driveline alignment concerns.

Related parts: Driveshaft • U-joints • Slip joint • Ride height

Wheel Seal

What it does: Keeps wheel-end lubricant inside the hub while sealing around the rotating spindle/hub interface.

When to look here: Lubricant around the hub, wheel, brake area or repeated seal leakage.

Related parts: Hub • Bearings • Spindle • Vent • Brake components

PHOTO SLOT — HEIGHT CONTROL VALVE

Height Control Valve

What it does: Adds or exhausts suspension air to maintain the specified chassis ride height.

When to look here: Truck sits too high/low, one side low, slow leveling or incorrect ride height.

Related parts: Air springs • Linkage • Supply air • Dump valve
