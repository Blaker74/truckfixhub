🐌 ENGINE CRANKS SLOW

Slow cranking means the starter system is turning the engine, but not at the expected speed. Prove electrical delivery before replacing the starter.

1️⃣ BATTERY BANK: Verify state of charge and battery capability using the manufacturer-approved test. One weak battery can affect the complete bank.
2️⃣ CONNECTIONS: Inspect battery terminals, interconnects, disconnects, grounds and starter connections for looseness, corrosion, heat or damage.
3️⃣ VOLTAGE DROP UNDER CRANK: Test positive and ground paths while current is actually flowing. An unloaded continuity check can miss a high-resistance connection.
4️⃣ STARTER PERFORMANCE: If battery capability and cable delivery are proven, evaluate starter current/performance using the exact application procedure.
5️⃣ MECHANICAL LOAD: If electrical and starter operation are proven, consider abnormal engine/accessory mechanical drag using safe OEM procedures.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Recheck crank speed and voltage under the same temperature and operating conditions that caused the complaint.
BATTERY CAPABILITY → CONNECTIONS → LOADED VOLTAGE DROP → STARTER → MECHANICAL LOAD → VERIFY.
⚠️ Heavy-duty starter circuits carry very high current. Secure the vehicle and follow OEM electrical safety procedures.
