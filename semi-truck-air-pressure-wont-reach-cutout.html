Semi Truck Air Pressure Won't Reach Cut-Out?

Pressure that rises but levels off below the vehicle's normal governed range is a different clue from a truck that produces no air.

Safety first

Air-brake pressure and warning systems are safety-critical. Do not operate the truck if required pressure, warning or brake-release conditions are not satisfied.

What to observe

  • Where pressure stops rising
  • Whether primary and secondary gauges behave alike
  • Whether an air leak becomes louder as pressure increases
  • Air-dryer purge behavior
  • Compressor loading/unloading behavior when properly tested
  • Recent repairs or plumbing changes

Potential diagnostic areas

Depending on configuration, technicians may investigate leakage, compressor delivery, governor/control circuits, air dryer operation, discharge plumbing, reservoir/check-valve behavior and gauge/sensor accuracy. Normal governed pressures and test procedures vary by vehicle and component manufacturer.

Do not adjust blindly

Do not change governor settings or bypass pressure controls simply to make the gauge read higher. Confirm specifications and diagnose the underlying condition using approved procedures.
