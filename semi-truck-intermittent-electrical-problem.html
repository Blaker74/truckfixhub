👻 INTERMITTENT ELECTRICAL PROBLEM

Intermittent faults are easier when you treat when it fails as diagnostic evidence.

1️⃣ BUILD A FAILURE PROFILE: Hot or cold? Wet or dry? Moving or parked? Bumps? Engine vibration? Key cycle? Certain accessories?
2️⃣ FIND WHAT FAILS TOGETHER: Multiple symptoms can share power, ground, splice, connector, network or module supply.
3️⃣ CAPTURE DATA: Record fault codes and operating conditions before clearing anything. History can reveal voltage or communication events.
4️⃣ TEST WHILE FAILED: A circuit that works in the shop may hide the problem. When practical, measure power, command, loaded voltage drop and return while the symptom is present.
5️⃣ STRESS THE SUSPECT PATH SAFELY: Follow approved procedures for harness movement, connector inspection, temperature/moisture correlation and terminal checks. Do not damage connectors by probing them incorrectly.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Recreate the original trigger after repair. One successful key cycle is not enough when the complaint was intermittent.
WHEN? → WHAT FAILS TOGETHER? → CAPTURE DATA → TEST DURING FAILURE → PROVE SHARED PATH → REPAIR → RECREATE TRIGGER.
⚠️ Avoid random module replacement. Intermittent power, ground, terminal tension and harness faults can imitate failed electronics.
