← AIR SUSPENSION

↘️ ONE SIDE / ONE CORNER LOW

When only one area is low, start local before blaming the vehicle’s entire air system.

1️⃣ DEFINE THE AREA: One bag, one corner, one side or one axle group? Note loaded/unloaded condition and whether height changes after driving.
2️⃣ AIR SPRING: Inspect the local air spring for leakage, damage, rubbing, incorrect seating or structural concerns using approved methods.
3️⃣ LOCAL PLUMBING: Inspect nearby line, fitting, tee and connection points. Trace backward only after the local branch is checked.
4️⃣ CONTROL / LINKAGE: Inspect height-control linkage and valve arrangement for damage, incorrect mounting or lost motion. Some systems control groups rather than individual corners.
5️⃣ MECHANICAL SUSPENSION: A bent/broken mount, spring component, bushing, torque rod or axle-location problem can create an apparent ride-height issue even when the bag holds air.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Restore the exact application’s specified ride height and confirm it remains correct through suspension movement and the original operating condition.
IDENTIFY LOW AREA → LOCAL BAG → LOCAL PLUMBING → CONTROL / LINKAGE → MECHANICAL LOCATION → SPECIFIED HEIGHT → VERIFY.
⚠️ Never work under a chassis supported only by air springs. Mechanically support the vehicle before inspection or repair.
