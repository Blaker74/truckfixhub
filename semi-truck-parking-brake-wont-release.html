Parking brakes won't release

Do not force the truck to move. Start with the air supply, then find out which brakes are still applied.

1️⃣ FIRST: Does the truck have enough air?

If pressure is low, builds slowly or will not build, solve that problem before blaming the brake hardware.

Find which step did not happen. Do not jump straight to the chamber.

🚚 Several tractor brakes stay applied

When multiple tractor brakes fail to release together, look for evidence in the shared release/control path before treating each wheel as a separate failure. Exact plumbing and valve arrangements vary by vehicle.

👂 Air leaks while trying to release

Record where the sound is strongest and whether it changes with the parking control. A wheel-area leak does not automatically prove the chamber failed.

❄️ Problem appeared in freezing weather

Cold conditions can expose moisture or icing problems in an air system. Preserve that timing as evidence and review dryer/system condition. Do not use improvised heat sources or unapproved chemicals around brake components.

⚠️ Spring brake stored energy

Never open, cut or disassemble a spring brake chamber. Do not improvise a caging or release method. Use the correct manufacturer procedure and qualified heavy-duty brake practices.

SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

Low air, a shared control problem and one mechanically dragging wheel can all produce “parking brake won't release.” The pattern chooses the next test.

🛑 SAFETY: Do not drive against an applied parking or spring brake. A dragging brake can rapidly overheat and damage brake, tire and wheel-end components. Never crawl beneath an unsecured vehicle or bypass brake-protection systems. Verify exact pressure, release and service procedures for the exact vehicle and components.
