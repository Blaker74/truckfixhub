🔒 PARKING BRAKE WON’T RELEASE

Spring brakes require adequate release pressure and a working control/delivery path. Separate no release air from mechanical brake binding.

1️⃣ SYSTEM PRESSURE: Verify the vehicle has sufficient air pressure for normal release according to its specifications.
2️⃣ CONTROL: Verify parking-brake control operation and whether release air is being commanded/delivered.
3️⃣ DELIVERY PATH: Inspect/test applicable valves, hoses, fittings and chamber supply. A restriction or leak can prevent full release.
4️⃣ SPRING BRAKE CHAMBER: Follow chamber manufacturer procedures. Never disassemble a spring-brake chamber containing a powerful compressed spring.
5️⃣ FOUNDATION BRAKE: If chamber movement/release is correct but wheel remains held, inspect mechanical brake components, adjustment and seizure/binding.
AIR PRESSURE → RELEASE COMMAND → DELIVERY → CHAMBER MOVEMENT → FOUNDATION BRAKE → PROVE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Spring-brake chambers can contain lethal stored energy. Never disassemble them unless specifically designed/serviceable and you are following the manufacturer procedure.
