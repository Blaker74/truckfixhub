Clear sponsorship. Independent technical guidance.

Paid placements will be labeled clearly as “Sponsored” or “Advertisement” where they appear. A financial relationship will not be hidden inside fine print or used to make an advertisement look like independent technical guidance.

Sponsors cannot purchase a favorable diagnosis, recommendation, review, ranking or technical conclusion. Any endorsement must reflect an honest opinion or actual experience, and TruckFixHub will not publish product-performance claims that we cannot reasonably support.

If a company supplies money, products, services, discounts or another material benefit connected to content, that relationship will be disclosed where users can see it.

Our rule: usefulness comes first.

TruckFixHub is built to help people solve truck problems. Advertising and sponsorship should help support that mission without taking control of it.

Availability, placement and pricing are discussed individually. TruckFixHub may decline partnerships that are not a good fit for the heavy-duty trucking audience.
