🎵 TURBO WHISTLE / HISS

A new whistle or hiss is a clue—not a turbo diagnosis.

1️⃣ WHEN? Idle, acceleration, engine brake, only under heavy load, hot only? Reproduce the noise safely.
2️⃣ CHARGE-AIR SIDE: Inspect boots, clamps, pipes, cooler and intake connections. Escaping boosted air often becomes much more obvious as load rises.
3️⃣ EXHAUST SIDE: Inspect manufacturer-approved areas for pre-turbo exhaust leakage. Soot tracks can help locate a leak that changes turbo response.
4️⃣ INLET: Check filter and inlet plumbing for restriction, collapse, loose joints or damage that changes compressor sound.
5️⃣ TURBO HARDWARE: Only after external air/exhaust paths are checked should turbo condition, wheel contact, bearing concerns or control hardware move higher on the list. Use OEM inspection limits.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Repeat the same load/RPM condition after repair and confirm noise plus performance return to normal.
WHEN DOES IT WHISTLE? → CHARGE AIR → EXHAUST → INLET → TURBO INSPECTION → PROVE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Never reach into turbo inlet/outlet areas with the engine running. Turbo and exhaust components can be extremely hot and rotate at very high speed.
