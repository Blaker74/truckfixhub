Verified Engine Oil Specs

PACCAR MX-13 — current operator-manual configuration

Total system capacity, oil pan + new filters: 42 U.S. qt (40.0 L).

Manufacturer source: PACCAR MX-13 Operator's Manual Y53-1100-1F1. PACCAR publishes operator manuals by engine/generation; verify the engine and manual revision before service.
Mack MP7 / MP8 — heavy-duty published specifications

Published engine oil capacity: 44 qt (42 L).
MP7 steel-pan oil-change volume: 38 qt (36 L).
MP8 service quantity varies with oil-filter configuration; identify the installed arrangement before filling.

Manufacturer source: Mack Trucks Heavy-Duty Body Builder, Section 1 — Oil and Filters. Mack separates total capacity, pan volume, filter volume and oil-change volume.
Mack MP10 — heavy-duty published specifications

Published engine oil capacity: 55 qt (52 L).

Manufacturer source: Mack Trucks Heavy-Duty Body Builder, Section 1 — Oil and Filters. Verify model year and configuration.
Caterpillar C15 — on-highway deep-standard sump

Approximate lubrication-system refill: 38 L (40 qt), including standard factory-installed oil filters.
Optional Caterpillar bypass filter: add approximately 2.5 L (2.6 qt).

Source: Caterpillar Operation and Maintenance literature SEBU7695. Auxiliary filtration changes total capacity; engine serial prefix and sump must be identified.
Caterpillar C11 / C13 — on-highway

Deep-standard sump: 40 L (42 qt).
Shallow sump: 34 L (36 qt).
Values include standard factory-installed oil filters.

Source: Caterpillar Operation and Maintenance literature SEBU7695. Verify sump and engine serial prefix before service.
Detroit DD13 — Gen 5 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 38.0 L (40.0 qt). Pan high: 35.0 L (37.0 qt). Pan low: 30.0 L (32.0 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0217.
Detroit DD15 — Gen 5 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 43.0 L (45.0 qt). Pan high: 40.0 L (42.0 qt). Pan low: 35.0 L (37.0 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0217.
Detroit DD16 — GHG17 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 43.0 L (45.0 qt). Pan high: 40.0 L (42.0 qt). Pan low: 35.0 L (37.0 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Heavy Duty Platform Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0189.
Detroit Series 60 — on-highway truck pan

LOW without filters: 26 qt. FULL without filters: 32 qt. Total at FULL with filters/galleries charged: 40 qt.

Manufacturer source: Detroit Series 60 On-Highway Operator's Guide DDC-SVC-MAN-0039.
Cummins X15 — EPA 2017 on-highway

Oil: 15W-40 or 10W-30 meeting Cummins CES 20086 / API CK-4.
Stamped steel pan: 43.5 L (11.5 gal). Aluminum wedge pan: 45.4 L (12 gal). Aluminum rear-center sump: 41.6 L (11 gal). Standard filter: 2.2 L (0.58 gal).

Manufacturer source: Cummins X15 EPA 2017 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, Bulletin 5410641.
Volvo D13 — EPA 2027

Specification: Volvo VDS-5.0. Viscosity: SAE 5W-30. Total lube capacity: 35 qt (33 L).

Manufacturer source: Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications.

Research queue: PACCAR MX-11 and older MX generations; CAT 3406E/C12 and additional C15 serial families; Cummins ISX/ISM/N14; International/Navistar; Mack/Volvo historical generations.

Verified Transmission Specs

Eaton Cummins Endurant HD

Recommended lubricant: Eaton PS-386. Nominal oil capacity: 16 pints (7.57 L).

Manufacturer source: Eaton Cummins Endurant HD service/product literature.
Volvo I-Shift — EPA 2027 published configuration

Approximate oil capacity: 18 qt (17 L) or 20.8 qt (19.68 L), depending on transmission configuration.

Manufacturer source: Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications.

Drive Axles

Dana/Spicer, Meritor/Rockwell, PACCAR, Detroit, Mack and Volvo axle families are being indexed by exact model. Housing, installation angle and axle configuration can change lubricant quantity. Fill-hole level and the current manufacturer procedure remain the final service reference.

Coolant & Other Fluids

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017 coolant guidance

Cummins specifies fully formulated antifreeze/coolant with the required additive package and references TMC RP 329 for ethylene-glycol coolant or RP 330 for propylene-glycol coolant.

Manufacturer source: Cummins Bulletin 5410641. Chassis cooling capacity varies by installation.
Accuracy standard: TruckFixHub distinguishes configuration-specific manufacturer data from general guidance. Confirm component tag, engine serial number, sump/filter configuration, vehicle configuration and current OEM service information before filling. Never assume one capacity applies to every version of an engine or drivetrain family.
