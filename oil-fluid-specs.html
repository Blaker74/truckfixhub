Verified Engine Oil Specs

Detroit DD13 — Gen 5 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 38.0 L (40.0 qt).
Oil pan high limit: 35.0 L (37.0 qt).
Oil pan low limit: 30.0 L (32.0 qt).
Dipstick add-to-full range: approximately 5.0 L (5.2 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual, DDC-SVC-MAN-0217, revised 2025-08-26. Detroit warns that overfilling can cause engine damage.
Detroit DD15 — Gen 5 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 43.0 L (45.0 qt).
Oil pan high limit: 40.0 L (42.0 qt).
Oil pan low limit: 35.0 L (37.0 qt).
Dipstick add-to-full range: approximately 5.0 L (5.2 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual, DDC-SVC-MAN-0217, revised 2025-08-26.
Detroit DD16 — GHG17 truck

Service fill, oil + filter: 43.0 L (45.0 qt).
Oil pan high limit: 40.0 L (42.0 qt).
Oil pan low limit: 35.0 L (37.0 qt).

Manufacturer source: Detroit DD Heavy Duty Platform Operator Manual, DDC-SVC-MAN-0189, GHG17 capacity table. Confirm engine generation before applying this value.
Detroit Series 60 — on-highway truck oil pan

Oil pan LOW mark, without filters: 26 qt.
Oil pan FULL mark, without filters: 32 qt.
Total engine oil capacity at FULL: 40 qt with filters installed and filters/oil galleries charged.

Manufacturer source: Detroit Series 60 On-Highway Engine Operator's Guide, DDC-SVC-MAN-0039 (Rev. 06/06). Detroit states front-sump and rear-sump Series 60 truck pans covered by this guide use the same quantity.
Detroit engine-oil specification families

DFS 93K223: API FA-4 approved-oil list for applicable Gen 5/GHG17/GHG14/EPA10 engines.
DFS 93K222: API CK-4 approved-oil list for applicable Gen 5/GHG17/GHG14/EPA10/EPA07 engines.
DFS 93K214: API CI-4 PLUS approved-oil list for applicable pre-2007 engines.
DFS 93K215: API CH-4 approved-oil list for applicable non-EGR engines.

Manufacturer source: Daimler Truck North America / Detroit Lubricants, Fuels & Coolants service information. Exact oil selection and drain interval must match engine generation and Detroit requirements.
Cummins X15 — EPA 2017 on-highway configuration

Recommended oil: high-quality 15W-40 or 10W-30 heavy-duty engine oil meeting Cummins CES 20086 / API CK-4.
Stamped-steel pan oil change fill: 43.5 L (11.5 gal).
Aluminum wedge-type pan oil change fill: 45.4 L (12 gal).
Aluminum rear-center-sump oil change fill: 41.6 L (11 gal).
Standard filter capacity: 2.2 L (0.58 gal).

Manufacturer source: Cummins X15 EPA 2017 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, Bulletin 5410641. Capacity varies by oil-pan configuration.
Volvo D13 — EPA 2027 powertrain

Oil specification: Volvo VDS-5.0.
Viscosity: SAE 5W-30.
Total lube oil capacity: 35 qt (33 L).
Normal-service oil drain interval: 90,000 miles (144,840 km), subject to Volvo's stated service conditions.

Manufacturer source: Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications. Applies to the published EPA 2027 D13 configuration, not every historical D13.

Research queue: PACCAR MX-11/MX-13; Mack MP7/MP8; CAT 3406/C12/C13/C15; Cummins ISX/ISM/N14; International/Navistar; additional Detroit generations and configurations.

Verified Transmission Specs

Eaton Cummins Endurant HD

Recommended lubricant: Eaton PS-386.
Nominal oil capacity: 16 pints (7.57 L).

Source: Eaton Cummins Endurant HD official product/service literature. Exact model and application must still be verified.
Volvo I-Shift — EPA 2027 published configuration

Approximate oil capacity: 18 qt (17 L) or 20.8 qt (19.68 L), depending on transmission configuration.

Manufacturer source: Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications. Exact I-Shift configuration must be identified before service.

Additional transmission families are being researched from manufacturer service literature before publication.

Drive Axles

Dana/Spicer, Meritor/Rockwell, Detroit, Mack and Volvo axle families are being indexed by exact axle model. Capacity will not be published as a generic truck-model number because housing, configuration, installation angle and axle family can change it. The fill-hole level and current manufacturer procedure remain the final service reference.

Coolant & Other Fluids

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017 coolant guidance

Cummins specifies fully formulated antifreeze/coolant with the required additive package and references TMC RP 329 for ethylene-glycol coolant or RP 330 for propylene-glycol coolant in its X15 EPA 2017 quick-reference literature.

Manufacturer source: Cummins Bulletin 5410641. Chassis cooling-system capacity varies by installation and is not represented here as one universal X15 capacity.
Accuracy standard: TruckFixHub distinguishes configuration-specific manufacturer data from general guidance. Always confirm the component identification tag, engine serial number, vehicle configuration and current OEM service information before filling or servicing a component. Never assume a capacity applies to every version of an engine or drivetrain family.
