Coolant & Other Fluids

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017 coolant guidance

Cummins specifies fully formulated antifreeze/coolant with the required additive package and references TMC RP 329 for ethylene-glycol coolant or RP 330 for propylene-glycol coolant.

Cummins Bulletin 5410641. Chassis cooling-system capacity varies by installation.
Accuracy standard: TruckFixHub distinguishes verified manufacturer data from general guidance. Confirm component identification tag, serial number, model year/emissions generation, sump/filter/housing configuration and current OEM information before service. We will leave a specification blank rather than invent a number.
