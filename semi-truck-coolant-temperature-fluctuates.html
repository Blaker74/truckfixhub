🌡️ TEMPERATURE GOES UP & DOWN

First decide whether coolant temperature is truly changing or the displayed signal is changing.

1️⃣ COMPARE DATA: Compare dash indication with scan/OEM temperature data and, where appropriate, independent temperature evidence.
2️⃣ MATCH THE EVENT: Does temperature rise on hills, at idle, during regeneration, with A/C, after thermostat opening or when the fan cycles?
3️⃣ COOLANT LEVEL / AIR: Verify correct cold level and investigate leaks or improper filling/air removal if service was recently performed.
4️⃣ THERMOSTAT / CIRCULATION: Use the engine-specific procedure to evaluate thermostat behavior and coolant circulation. Do not condemn a thermostat from gauge movement alone.
5️⃣ FAN / AIRFLOW: Compare fan command with actual fan operation and inspect radiator/CAC airflow when temperature rises under load or low vehicle speed.
6️⃣ SENSOR / WIRING: Sudden impossible jumps, dropouts or disagreement between data sources can indicate an electrical/input issue rather than true thermal change.
VERIFY REAL TEMP → MATCH OPERATING CONDITION → LEVEL/AIR → THERMOSTAT/CIRCULATION → FAN/AIRFLOW → SENSOR/SIGNAL → PROVE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Do not open a hot cooling system. Use exact OEM temperature ranges, thermostat tests and fan-control procedures for the engine/vehicle.
