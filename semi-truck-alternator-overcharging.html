⚡ CHARGING VOLTAGE TOO HIGH

Overcharging can damage batteries and electronics. Verify the voltage and understand how the exact vehicle controls charging.

1️⃣ VERIFY WITH A TRUSTWORTHY METER: Compare actual system voltage at appropriate test points and conditions. Do not rely only on a dash gauge.
2️⃣ BATTERY / CONNECTION CONDITION: Inspect battery bank, grounds and charging connections. Sense-path errors can affect regulated output on some systems.
3️⃣ CONTROL STRATEGY: Determine whether regulation is internal, externally controlled or network-managed for the exact application.
4️⃣ SENSE / CONTROL CIRCUITS: Use the OEM wiring diagram and diagnostic procedure to test applicable sense, ignition, field/control, power and ground circuits.
5️⃣ ALTERNATOR / REGULATOR: Condemn the charging unit only after its inputs and control strategy are proven correct.
6️⃣ VERIFY + CHECK DAMAGE: Confirm normal charging behavior and inspect batteries/electrical system for consequences of sustained overvoltage as required.
VERIFY VOLTAGE → BATTERIES / CONNECTIONS → CONTROL TYPE → SENSE / CONTROL CIRCUITS → REGULATOR / ALTERNATOR → VERIFY.
⚠️ Sustained overvoltage can damage batteries and electronic modules. Address confirmed overcharging promptly.
