🧰 Six Aftertreatment Drawers

1 · DOC MAKES THE CONDITIONSThe diesel oxidation catalyst helps create the heat and chemistry needed downstream. Exhaust temperature and engine condition matter.
2 · DPF CATCHES SOOTThe DPF traps particulate matter. Restriction and soot loading matter, but sensor or tube trouble can give misleading pressure information.
3 · REGEN BURNS SOOTRegeneration raises exhaust temperature so soot can be oxidized. A failed regen is a symptom that needs a reason.
4 · DEF GOES INThe DEF system must store, supply and meter suitable fluid. Pressure, temperature, quality and dosing all matter.
5 · SCR REDUCES NOxSCR uses ammonia derived from DEF to reduce nitrogen oxides. Efficiency depends on more than the catalyst itself.
6 · SENSORS REPORT WHAT HAPPENEDNOx, temperature, differential-pressure and other inputs help the controller judge operation. A sensor code does not automatically mean a bad sensor.

🔍 Let The Symptom Pick The Drawer

🚫 WON'T REGENLook for inhibit conditions, active faults, temperature capability, soot status and whether regeneration is actually requested.
🔁 KEEPS ASKING FOR REGENAsk why soot is accumulating or why the controller believes it is accumulating.
💧 DEF WARNINGSeparate fluid level, quality, temperature, supply pressure, dosing and electrical/sensor problems.
♻️ SCR EFFICIENCY FAULTVerify DEF delivery, exhaust temperature, NOx plausibility and upstream condition before condemning the catalyst.
🐢 DERATEA derate is often the controller's response to another problem. Find the initiating fault.
⚠️ MANY CODESLook for shared causes such as power, ground, communication, exhaust temperature, DEF supply or a common harness.

🔧 The Diagnostic Drawer Never Changes

SEE ITRecord active and inactive faults, warnings, derate state, soot information and the conditions when the complaint occurs.
NAME ITRoute the evidence toward temperature, soot/DPF, DEF supply/dosing, NOx/SCR, electrical or communication.
TEST ITCompare commanded values with actual response. Inspect wiring, tubes, fluid and physical condition.
PROVE ITResolve the supported cause, operate the system under the required conditions and verify the fault does not return.

⚠️ A CODE NAMES A CONDITION — NOT NECESSARILY A BAD PART

Do not replace NOx sensors, DPFs, SCR catalysts, DEF pumps or dosing components solely because their system appears in a fault description. Exact thresholds, regeneration procedures, temperatures, pressures and diagnostic routines are engine/manufacturer specific and must be verified with current service information.
