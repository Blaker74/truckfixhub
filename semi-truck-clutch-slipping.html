🔥 CLUTCH SLIPPING

Prove true clutch slip first. The strongest road symptom is engine RPM rising without a matching increase in vehicle speed while torque is applied.

1️⃣ RECORD THE CONDITION: Note gear, road speed, engine RPM, load, grade, temperature and whether the complaint appears only after the clutch is hot.
2️⃣ VERIFY FULL ENGAGEMENT: Confirm the pedal, linkage, hydraulic/air system or automated actuator is allowing the clutch to fully engage. A release system that remains partially applied can create slip.
3️⃣ VERIFY ADJUSTMENT: Use the exact clutch manufacturer's procedure. Do not apply a generic free-play or clutch-brake adjustment to every clutch system.
4️⃣ LOOK FOR CONTAMINATION: Engine rear-main, transmission input or other leakage may contaminate friction surfaces. Find the source before replacing friction parts.
5️⃣ LOOK FOR HEAT / WEAR EVIDENCE: Odor, discoloration, glazing and friction-material condition can support a slip diagnosis, but determine why the heat occurred.
6️⃣ CHECK APPLICATION / TORQUE: Confirm the clutch assembly is correct for the engine, transmission and vehicle application when repeat or early failure is involved.
7️⃣ VERIFY THE REPAIR: Retest under the original safe load and gear condition. Do not call it fixed from an unloaded idle test.
PROVE RPM FLARE → FULL ENGAGEMENT → EXACT ADJUSTMENT → CONTAMINATION → HEAT / WEAR → APPLICATION → VERIFY UNDER LOAD.
⚠️ Do not intentionally overload or abuse the clutch to reproduce slip. Follow current clutch and vehicle manufacturer safety/service procedures.
