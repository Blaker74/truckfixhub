Follow the fuel. Do not jump straight to the pump or injectors.

The engine needs the right fuel, delivered in the right amount and condition, at the right time. A weak engine under load does not automatically prove a bad fuel pump.

⛰️ Weak mostly under load

Heavy load increases fuel demand. Preserve the pattern: does power fall only on hills or acceleration, does it recover when load is removed, and are fuel-related pressure or command values changing at the same time? Compare the complaint with air, boost and exhaust behavior before declaring the fuel system guilty.

🧰 Filter or supply restriction

Start upstream. Check service history, correct filter application and installation, visible fuel condition, supply-line condition and any manufacturer-provided restriction or supply test. A recently installed filter can still be wrong, restricted or installed incorrectly.

🫧 Air entering the fuel path

Air can enter a low-pressure supply path without leaving an obvious external fuel leak. Inspect connections and disturbed service areas and use the correct manufacturer test method. Do not assume every visible bubble proves the same failure on every fuel system.

📉 Fuel pressure concern

Use the correct test point, operating condition and specification for the exact engine. Ask whether pressure is low at idle, only under load, during cranking or all the time. Then work backward through supply, restriction and control before condemning a pressure-producing component.

Injectors come later

Rough running, smoke or contribution faults can move diagnosis toward individual cylinders or injection components, but first prove the shared fuel supply is healthy. One shared supply problem can make several cylinders look bad at once.

Prove the fuel path can supply what the engine needs before buying expensive fuel-system parts.

⚠️ SAFETY: High-pressure diesel fuel can penetrate skin and cause severe injury. Never use hands to search for high-pressure leaks. Do not loosen high-pressure fuel connections on a running system unless the exact approved service procedure specifically requires it. Verify pressure limits and procedures for the exact engine.
