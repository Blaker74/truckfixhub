💡 LOAD → GROUND
1️⃣ POWER — Is the correct voltage actually getting there?
2️⃣ FUSE / RELAY — Is the circuit being allowed to turn on?
3️⃣ WIRE / CONNECTOR — Look for opens, shorts, corrosion, loose pins, rub-through or poor connections.
4️⃣ GROUND — A good power feed with a bad ground can still make the component fail.
⚠️ Don’t replace the component until you know it has good POWER and good GROUND.
