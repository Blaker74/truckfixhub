TRUCKFIXHUB FAULT CODE GUIDE

SPN 1569 FMI 31

Engine protection torque derate condition.

What does it mean?

SPN 1569 FMI 31 commonly appears when the engine control system is intentionally limiting available torque because another condition has triggered an engine-protection or emissions-related derate. It is often a consequence code rather than the root cause.

What should be checked?

  • Look for other active fault codes first
  • Record warning lamps and dashboard messages
  • Check whether an emissions/aftertreatment inducement is active
  • Check engine temperature, fluid levels and other protection conditions
  • Determine when the derate began and what fault appeared before it

Diagnosing only SPN 1569 can send a repair in the wrong direction. The companion fault usually provides the better starting point.

Do not ignore a derate

A derate is the control system limiting operation for a reason. If the truck displays stop-engine warnings, unsafe operating conditions, or severe loss of power, follow manufacturer guidance and obtain qualified repair assistance.
