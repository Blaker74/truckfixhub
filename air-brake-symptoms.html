What is the air or brake system doing?

Choose the complaint you can verify. Air supply problems and foundation-brake problems require different diagnostic paths.

📉Won't Build AirPressure rises too slowly, stops climbing or cannot reach normal operating range.💨Losing Air PressurePressure drops, an audible leak is present or the system will not hold air.🔒Brakes Won't ReleaseOne or more brakes remain applied after the release command.🔥Brake Drag / Hot WheelWheel end runs hot, vehicle feels held back or a brake fails to fully release.↔️Pulls While BrakingUneven braking, pull, grab or noticeably different braking response side to side.💧Air Dryer / MoistureExcess water or oil in tanks, frequent purge, no purge or dryer-related complaint.
Diagnostic rule: Separate air generation, air storage/distribution, control valves and foundation brakes before replacing components. Verify the complaint and isolate the affected section of the system.
Safety: Brake and compressed-air faults can make a vehicle unsafe to move. Chock wheels, support the vehicle correctly, never rely on air suspension or a jack alone, and follow current OEM/service procedures. Do not operate a vehicle with an unresolved unsafe brake condition.
