Truck Knowledge Map

TruckFixHub can hold a huge amount of knowledge without making the technician stare at a huge amount of information. Start with one simple question: What is the truck doing?

Our rule for growing TruckFixHub:
Deep knowledge underneath. Childlike-simple choices on top. A new technician should never need to know the right technical word before the site can help.

2 · Every system grows the same way

A · SymptomsWords technicians actually use: won't start, hot hub, low air, no power, leaking, shaking.
B · System knowledgeWhat the components are, what they do and how the system is supposed to work.
C · Visual diagnostic pathsSimple yes/no evidence paths that keep the diagnosis moving.
D · TestsVoltage drop, pressure, temperature, movement, restriction, command, response and other measurable evidence.
E · Failure patternsWhat different failures look, sound, smell and behave like—without turning patterns into guesses.
F · Exact informationWhen a specification or procedure depends on the exact truck, engine, axle or component, route to current manufacturer information.

⚠️ Simple does not mean careless

TruckFixHub will simplify the route to the answer, not invent specifications. Safety-critical torque, adjustment, programming, wiring, fluid and application-specific information must be verified for the exact equipment using current manufacturer information.
