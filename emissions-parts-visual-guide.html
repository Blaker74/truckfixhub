♻️ EMISSIONS PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Recognize the major parts in the diesel aftertreatment path.

The same-looking sensor can serve a different location or function. Before ordering, identify the exact engine, emissions generation, sensor position and application. Fault codes are starting clues—not automatic parts orders.

DOC — Diesel Oxidation Catalyst

Look for: Metal aftertreatment canister upstream of the DPF on many systems.

Job: Oxidizes exhaust constituents and helps create heat/conditions used by downstream aftertreatment.

SEE EMISSIONS SYSTEM →

DPF — Diesel Particulate Filter

Look for: Large exhaust filter canister in the aftertreatment assembly.

Job: Traps particulate matter/soot so it can be managed through regeneration and service.

DIAGNOSE DPF →

SCR Catalyst

Look for: Downstream catalyst section after DEF is introduced into the exhaust stream.

Job: Uses ammonia derived from DEF to reduce NOx emissions.

SEE SCR SYSTEM →

DEF Doser / Injector

Look for: Small injector/dosing unit mounted at the exhaust mixing section with fluid/electrical connections.

Job: Meters DEF into the exhaust when commanded.

DIAGNOSE DOSING →

NOx Sensor

Look for: Threaded exhaust probe with cable leading to a small electronic control module.

Job: Measures NOx concentration; systems commonly use upstream and downstream positions.

DIAGNOSE NOx SENSOR →

Exhaust Temperature Sensor

Look for: Threaded probe in exhaust/aftertreatment with a wire lead and connector.

Job: Reports exhaust temperature used for protection, dosing and regeneration strategies.

DIAGNOSE →

DPF Differential Pressure Sensor

Look for: Small sensor/module with two pressure hose ports connected across the DPF.

Job: Measures pressure difference used to evaluate exhaust restriction/filter loading.

DIAGNOSE →

EGR Cooler

Look for: Metal heat-exchanger body with exhaust and coolant connections.

Job: Removes heat from recirculated exhaust before it returns to the intake path.

SEE EGR SYSTEM →

EGR Valve

Look for: Exhaust-flow valve/actuator assembly between exhaust and intake/EGR passages.

Job: Controls the amount of exhaust gas recirculated into the engine.

SEE EGR DIAGNOSTICS →

DEF Tank / Header Assembly

Look for: DEF reservoir with cap and top/header module containing level/temperature and fluid connections depending on design.

Job: Stores DEF and supplies monitored fluid to the dosing system.
