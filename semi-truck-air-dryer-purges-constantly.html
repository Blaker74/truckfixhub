💨 AIR DRYER PURGES CONSTANTLY?

Frequent purging is often a symptom of the air system cycling too often — not proof that the dryer itself is bad.

Quick answer

If a semi truck air dryer purges repeatedly, watch the air gauges and determine whether the compressor is repeatedly loading and unloading. A substantial downstream air leak or excessive air use can make the compressor cycle frequently, causing normal purge events to happen too often. If system pressure is stable but the dryer still exhausts abnormally, inspect the governor/control circuit, purge-control signal, purge valve and dryer plumbing according to the compressor and dryer manufacturer's procedure.

Start here — in this order

1️⃣ Watch system pressure during the complaint

Record pressure at compressor cut-in and cut-out and whether pressure falls rapidly between purge events. Do not diagnose by sound alone.

2️⃣ Check the truck and trailer for air leaks

A leak can force repeated compressor cycles. Inspect the service, parking, suspension and accessory air systems using approved leak-testing procedures.

3️⃣ Determine whether compressor cycling matches each purge

A purge associated with each normal compressor unload event points you toward why the compressor is cycling so frequently. Purging that does not match expected cycling requires a different path.

4️⃣ Inspect governor and control plumbing

Follow the correct schematic for the compressor/dryer system. Check control lines for leakage, restriction, damage or incorrect routing and verify governor/control operation to manufacturer specifications.

5️⃣ Inspect the purge valve and dryer

If the system is commanding correctly but the purge exhaust behaves abnormally, inspect the purge valve, seals, heater/electrical components where applicable and dryer condition using the dryer's service information.

6️⃣ Verify the complete cycle after repair

Confirm normal pressure build, cut-out, purge, compressor unloaded period and cut-in. The goal is a correct air-system cycle, not simply making the purge noise disappear.

What the pattern can tell you

📉 Pressure drops quickly after each purge

Look hard for downstream leakage or abnormal air consumption. The dryer may simply be responding to frequent compressor cycles.

🔁 Compressor loads and unloads constantly

Diagnose why system pressure is crossing the control thresholds so frequently. Check leakage, governor/control operation and system plumbing.

💨 Air keeps exhausting from the dryer

A continuous exhaust is different from repeated normal purge blasts. Inspect purge-valve/control behavior and verify the compressor's loaded/unloaded state.

🚛 Problem changes when trailer is connected

Include trailer air leakage and trailer supply/control circuits in the diagnosis. Isolate tractor from trailer to see which side changes the cycling.

🌧️ Water or contamination is present

Check dryer maintenance condition and the complete compressor/dryer system. Follow manufacturer guidance for cartridge/service intervals and contamination diagnosis.

🔧 Started after dryer, governor or compressor work

Recheck line routing, fittings, control connections and installed components against the correct schematic before replacing anything else.

Information to collect before buying parts

  • Truck year, make, model and engine
  • Air compressor and dryer manufacturer/model when known
  • Observed cut-in and cut-out pressure
  • Time between purge events
  • Whether compressor cycles with each purge
  • System pressure-loss/leak-test results
  • Tractor-only versus trailer-connected behavior
  • Governor/control-line inspection results
  • Recent compressor, governor or dryer repairs
🧠 Parts-cannon rule: frequent dryer purge does not automatically equal a bad air dryer. First prove whether the compressor is being forced to cycle because the system is losing or using air.
⚠️ Compressed-air systems store dangerous energy. Chock and support the vehicle correctly and exhaust pressure from the affected circuit before disconnecting air lines or servicing components.
