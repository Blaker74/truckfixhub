TRUCKFIXHUB REPAIR WORKSPACE

One truck. One repair. One record.

The driver, mechanic and parts professional work from the same repair information instead of repeating it by phone, text and paper.

The repair triangle

🚛DriverReports the truck, location, symptoms, codes and photos.
🔧Mechanic / ShopReviews the intake, diagnoses the problem and requests parts.
📦PartsUses VIN/ESN, builds the parts list and returns availability or notes.

Repair Job workflow

1️⃣
Problem ReportedDriver creates the Repair Intake once.
2️⃣
Shop ContactedChoose a nearby shop or a known/preferred shop and share the intake.
3️⃣
DiagnosingMechanic records inspection findings and confirmed diagnosis.
4️⃣
Parts RequestedVIN, ESN, diagnosis and requested parts stay attached to the repair.
5️⃣
RepairingParts and repair information come back to the mechanic.
✅
Completed / Back in ServiceWhat Fixed It? becomes part of the truck's permanent repair history.

Information follows the repair

Driver → Mechanic → Parts → Mechanic → Driver/Fleet. Truck information should only be entered once. Sharing is deliberate: TruckFixHub does not automatically send a driver's private repair information to nearby businesses.
