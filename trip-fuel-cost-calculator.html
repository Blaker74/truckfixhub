Trip Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate fuel costs for a car, pickup, RV or truck. Use this free calculator for a vacation, commute or work route. No login required.

Enter your trip details, then calculate.

How the calculation works

Gallons needed = total miles ÷ miles per gallon. Fuel cost = gallons needed × price per gallon.

Example: a 200-mile trip at 25 mpg uses 8 gallons. At $3.50 per gallon, estimated fuel cost is $28. If 200 miles is the one-way distance, the round-trip estimate is $56.

The starting values are examples, not current fuel prices. Enter the price you expect to pay. This estimates fuel only; tolls, parking, lodging, maintenance and other costs are separate. Actual consumption changes with load, speed, weather, idling and terrain.

Questions about trip fuel costs

Does this work for diesel trucks?

Yes. Enter your truck’s measured mpg and diesel price. Use your own operating records instead of assuming a passenger-car value fits a loaded truck.

How do I calculate my mpg?

Divide miles driven between comparable fill-ups by gallons added at the next fill-up. Repeated fill-ups give a more useful picture than one unusually easy or difficult trip.

Can I estimate a weekly commute?

Enter the total miles for all journeys that week and choose “The entire trip.”
