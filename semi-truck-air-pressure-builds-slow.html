Air builds too slowly

Do not start by blaming the compressor. First find out where the air is going.

⚙️ MAKE AIR
➡️ MOVE AIR
🛢️ STORE AIR
💨 HOLD AIR

1 · Watch both gauges

Do primary and secondary pressure rise together? Does one lag behind? Does pressure rise steadily, stall, or rise and then fall?

Different gauge behavior is evidence.

2 · Listen for air escaping

A leak can consume air as fast as the compressor is trying to make it. Listen safely around tanks, lines, fittings, dryer, valves, chambers and connected air-operated systems.

If pressure builds and then falls, move toward the air-loss path.

3 · If it holds air, test the supply side

If a large downstream leak is not supported by the evidence, investigate compressor delivery, drive condition, compressor control/governor operation, discharge plumbing, dryer behavior and restrictions using the correct procedure for the truck.

Slow build alone does not prove a weak compressor.

4 · Ask whether temperature changes it

A problem that becomes much worse in freezing weather can point toward moisture, icing or a temperature-sensitive restriction. Do not use improvised heating or unapproved chemicals.

TruckFixHub rule: A slow gauge is the symptom. Prove whether air is being made too slowly, restricted on the way to the tanks, or escaping before replacing anything.
