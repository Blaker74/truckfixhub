Using TruckFixHub

TruckFixHub provides simple educational diagnostic guides, private organizational tools, search/navigation tools, and a public community for people in trucking. Users are responsible for the accuracy of information they save or post and for maintaining the security of their account.

Diagnostic guides

TruckFixHub diagnostic diagrams and troubleshooting information are general educational starting points. They are not vehicle-specific repair instructions and are not a substitute for manufacturer service information, proper diagnostic testing, required tools, trained technicians, or established safety procedures.

Truck and component designs vary. Before repairing, adjusting, disconnecting, lifting, supporting, pressurizing, depressurizing, or operating a vehicle or component, users should follow the applicable manufacturer procedures and safety requirements.

Community information

Community content may come from other users and may be incomplete or incorrect. Users should independently verify community advice before relying on it for repairs, safety, warranties, purchases, or business decisions.

Community members should communicate respectfully and must not post unlawful content, spam, impersonation, private information belonging to others, or material they do not have the right to share.

Private tools

TruckFixHub provides tools for users to save truck/VIN information, parts, notes, events, and warranty records. These tools are for organization and recordkeeping. Users remain responsible for maintaining any records required by their employer, customer, manufacturer, insurer, government agency, or other party.

No guarantee of diagnosis

TruckFixHub does not guarantee that a diagnostic guide, search result, community answer, or saved record will identify a failure or produce a correct repair. When safety or significant property damage may be involved, stop and use qualified professional and manufacturer guidance.
