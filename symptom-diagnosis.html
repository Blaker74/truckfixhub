TruckFixHub diagnostic rule

Symptoms point to systems. Tests prove causes. A part is not the diagnosis. Use the path to narrow the system, gather evidence, test the likely cause, make the repair, then verify the complaint is gone.

Safety: Stop operation for unsafe brake or steering conditions, fire or fuel leaks, severe overheating, low oil pressure, or any condition that makes the vehicle unsafe. Confirm procedures and specifications with current service information for the exact vehicle.
