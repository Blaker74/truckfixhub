## Editorial rules

- Publish original explanations and diagnostic organization; do not copy proprietary service manuals or databases.
- Do not invent torque values, timing values, valve lash, pressures, limits, calibration information, part fitment, or other application-specific technical specifications.
- Exact repair specifications belong to current manufacturer information for the exact application.
- A symptom or fault code is not proof that a named part failed.
- Prefer useful depth and connected diagnostic paths over thin page count.
- Keep public navigation simple enough for a first-time visitor.
- Keep advertising and sponsorship clearly separate from independent technical conclusions.

## Indexing rules

Public educational pages may be indexed. Private account and user-data tools should remain `noindex` and/or excluded from crawling where appropriate. The public sitemap should contain only pages intended for search discovery.

## Monetization rules

Primary near-term monetization is Google AdSense plus clearly labeled, relevant heavy-duty industry sponsorships. Future affiliate links or paid placements must be disclosed and must not control diagnostic conclusions.

## Owner-side steps that cannot be completed from the repository

1. In the AdSense account, confirm `truckfixhub.com` is added as the site and submit/request review when Google makes that action available.
2. Configure Google's required privacy/consent message or another Google-certified CMP for regions where Google requires one.
3. Complete AdSense identity, address, tax, and payment verification when Google requests them.
4. Keep Search Console sitemap/indexing monitoring active and allow Google time to crawl new or changed pages.
5. Review any policy notices shown inside AdSense after the site is crawled and address the specific notice rather than making speculative site changes.

## Completion principle

No website can guarantee AdSense approval. Google makes the final decision after its own crawl and policy review. TruckFixHub should be submitted as a useful website first and an advertising property second.
