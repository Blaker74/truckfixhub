DEF, SCR & NOx Sensor Troubleshooting

Understanding common heavy-duty diesel aftertreatment complaints and derates.

How the pieces relate

The SCR system uses diesel exhaust fluid as part of the process for reducing NOx emissions. Sensors and controls monitor system performance. A fault in one area—or an engine/exhaust condition affecting the system—can create warnings elsewhere, which is why complete fault information matters.

What to capture before diagnosis

  • All active SPN/FMI codes, not only the dashboard description
  • DEF warning and level indications
  • Check-engine, stop-engine or emissions lamps
  • Whether the truck is currently derated
  • Recent NOx sensor, DEF, exhaust or engine work
  • Visible wiring, connector, exhaust or fluid concerns

A NOx code doesn't always mean “buy a NOx sensor”

Diagnostic procedures may require checking electrical supply, wiring, connectors, plausibility and other system conditions. Capturing the complete code set before replacing parts helps avoid unnecessary expense.

Derate warnings

A derate can be a consequence of an unresolved emissions fault rather than the original cause. Record the earlier or accompanying faults whenever possible and follow the OEM diagnostic process for the specific engine and calibration.

Important

TruckFixHub is an educational and workflow resource, not a substitute for OEM service information or professional diagnosis. If warning indicators or vehicle behavior suggest continued operation is unsafe or may damage the truck, stop and seek qualified repair assistance.
