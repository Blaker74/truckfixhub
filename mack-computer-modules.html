Mack Computer Modules

Module names and installed equipment vary by model, year and options. Confirm the VIN and Mack service information before replacement or programming.

VECU5 / VECU4 — Vehicle Electronic Control Unit

📷 OEM module/location imagery: Mack Body Builder electrical documentation

Central vehicle electronic controller used for vehicle functions, multiplexing, PTO/body integration and communication with other controllers. Mack introduced VECU5 for 2022 model-year architecture; earlier vehicles use VECU4 architecture.

EMS / ECM — Engine Control Module

📷 Appearance depends on installed Mack/Volvo-family engine

Controls engine fueling, torque, combustion and engine sensors/actuators while communicating with the vehicle network.

ACM — Aftertreatment Control Module

📷 Aftertreatment controller identification varies by emissions generation

Coordinates aftertreatment functions including DEF/SCR and exhaust-system control on applicable powertrains.

TECU / TCM — Transmission Electronic Control Unit

📷 Module varies with mDRIVE or installed vendor transmission

Controls automated/automatic transmission operation and exchanges torque, speed and shift information with the engine and vehicle controllers.

ABS / ESC ECU

📷 Brake controller varies with installed brake/stability package

Processes wheel-speed and braking information and controls ABS and applicable electronic stability functions.

Instrument Cluster / Driver Information Controller

📷 Cab electronics vary by Anthem, Pinnacle, Granite, LR and generation

Displays vehicle gauges, warnings and status information received from networked controllers.

ADAS Radar / Camera Controllers

📷 Safety-system hardware varies by model and option package

Supports equipped collision mitigation and driver-assistance functions. Calibration, mounting, wiring and network integrity must be verified before replacement.

Cab / Door / HVAC Controllers

📷 Configuration-dependent cab modules

Electronic controllers used for equipped cab, door and climate-control functions. Exact module count and naming varies by cab architecture.

Body / PTO Integration

📷 Mack VECU body-builder architecture

Mack documentation states that Mack trucks do not use a separate external BBM in the documented VECU5 architecture; body-builder functions are managed by the VECU and transmitted through the SAE J1939 body interface.
