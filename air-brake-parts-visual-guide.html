🛑 AIR BRAKE PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Recognizable shapes for common heavy-duty air brake components.

Air brake work is safety-critical. These visuals help identify components; they do not replace manufacturer procedures, brake inspection requirements or proper training.

Air Dryer

Look for: Large vertical black canister on a metal valve/base with several air lines.

Job: Removes moisture and contamination from compressed air.

SEE AIR SYSTEM →

Air Compressor

Look for: Engine-driven metal pump with air, coolant and oil connections.

Job: Produces compressed air for the truck.

DIAGNOSE →

Spring Brake Chamber

Look for: Double round metal cans at a drive/trailer brake with a pushrod exiting one side.

Job: Applies service braking and contains the spring parking/emergency brake section.

DIAGNOSE →

Service Brake Chamber

Look for: Single round metal can, common on steer axle brakes, with a pushrod.

Job: Converts service air pressure into mechanical brake force.

SEE BRAKES →

Automatic Slack Adjuster

Look for: Heavy lever joining the brake chamber pushrod to the S-cam shaft.

Job: Transfers chamber movement and maintains brake running clearance within its design.

SEE FOUNDATION BRAKES →

Relay Valve

Look for: Compact valve body near rear axle/trailer brakes with multiple air ports.

Job: Uses a control signal to quickly apply/release nearby brake chambers.

SEE AIR FLOW →

ABS Modulator Valve

Look for: Air valve near an axle with air lines plus an electrical connector/wiring.

Job: Modulates brake pressure during an ABS event.

DIAGNOSE ABS →

ABS Wheel Speed Sensor

Look for: Small cylindrical sensor with a wire positioned at the wheel-end tone ring.

Job: Reports wheel speed to the ABS controller.

DIAGNOSE ABS →

ABS Tone Ring

Look for: Toothed or slotted ring rotating with the hub/wheel end beside the ABS sensor.

Job: Provides the repeating target the wheel-speed sensor reads.

DIAGNOSE ABS →

Air Reservoir / Tank

Look for: Long cylindrical metal tank mounted to frame with air lines and a drain.

Job: Stores compressed air for brake and accessory systems.
