FUTURE TRUCK TECHNICIANS

Your future could
keep us moving.

Trucking needs people who can diagnose a problem, learn a system, and care about getting the job done. This is a place to explore that work—and meet people willing to share what they know.

WHY BLAKE IS BUILDING THIS

We need to help the next generation get started.

Finding young people who want to work in this industry is one of the challenges behind TruckFixHub. Bringing drivers, owners, mechanics, and parts people together gives newcomers a place to ask their first questions.

01 · EXPLORE

Curious about truck repair?

Start with what interests you: electrical troubleshooting, engines, brakes, parts, or the technology inside a modern truck. Ask a technician what a normal day actually looks like.

02 · TAKE A STEP

Look for a way to learn.

Contact a local technical school or truck shop about training and entry-level pathways. Ask about cost, paid training, supervision, tools, and the requirements for your age and location.

03 · PASS IT ON

Know the trade? Share it.

Tell a beginner how you got started. Share a lesson, explain a diagnostic approach, or describe the habits that make a good technician. Experience has more value when it reaches the next person.

Share your experience →

Your first conversation with a shop or school

  • What does a beginner do here, and who supervises the work?
  • What training is provided, and what does it cost?
  • Is this a paid position? What are the hours and entry requirements?
  • Which tools and protective equipment are provided?
  • Can I visit, meet the team, and see the work before deciding?

Use community conversations to make connections. Confirm opportunities and terms directly with the shop or school. TruckFixHub does not currently list verified apprenticeships or operate a placement program.
