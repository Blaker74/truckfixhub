👇 Follow the pattern

⬇️ WHOLE SUSPENSION WON’T AIR UP: Confirm vehicle air pressure first. Check dump command/control, height-control linkage/valve and shared supply plumbing before condemning multiple air bags.
↘️ ONE SIDE / ONE CORNER LOW: Think local first. Inspect the air spring, nearby lines/fittings, linkage, mounts and mechanical damage, then trace upstream if local components test good.
💨 LEAKS DOWN WHILE PARKED: Record whether one corner, one axle or the entire suspension drops—and how quickly. Use an approved leak-detection method, repair the proven leak, then repeat the same parked test.
↕️ RIDE HEIGHT WRONG: Do not adjust the height-control valve simply to hide a fault. Inspect linkage, mounting, air supply, bags and axle-locating components first, then use the exact application’s ride-height procedure.
🔨 CLUNK / TRACKING PROBLEM: Air bags support the chassis, but mechanical components locate the axle. Inspect torque rods, bushings, mounts and related suspension parts when the system holds air but geometry or movement is wrong.

🧠 Local or shared?

1️⃣ One corner low: start at that corner.
2️⃣ One axle/group low: look for plumbing or control shared by that group.
3️⃣ Everything low: verify vehicle air supply and common dump/height-control paths.
4️⃣ Holds air but rides wrong: inspect mechanical axle location and verify ride height by specification.
