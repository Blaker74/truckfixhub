👇 Follow the pattern

⬇️ WHOLE SUSPENSION WON’T AIR UP: Verify vehicle air pressure and common dump/height-control paths before condemning multiple bags.
↘️ ONE SIDE / CORNER LOW: Start local—bag, line, fitting, linkage and nearby mechanical condition—then trace upstream if local parts prove good.
📉 LEAKS DOWN PARKED: Map what drops and how fast. One corner suggests local; multiple bags together point toward shared plumbing/control.
📏 RIDE HEIGHT WRONG: Do not adjust the valve to hide worn/bent parts. Verify the exact spec, air/dump state, linkage, bags and axle-location components first.
🎈 REPEAT AIR-BAG FAILURE: Read the failure pattern. Check rubbing, clearance, ride height, travel control, mounts and axle location before installing another bag.
⬇️ DUMP CONTROL: Separate missing command from a valve that receives the correct command but does not respond. Then prove the pneumatic path.

🧠 Local or shared?

1️⃣ One corner: start at that local branch.
2️⃣ One axle/group: identify plumbing/control shared by that group.
3️⃣ Everything: verify common vehicle air supply and dump/height-control functions.
4️⃣ Holds air but geometry is wrong: inspect mechanical axle location and measure ride height by exact specification.
