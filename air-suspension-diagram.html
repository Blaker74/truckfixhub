🎈 AIR SUSPENSION

Follow the air from the supply to the air bags.

1️⃣ AIR SUPPLY
Vehicle air system provides pressure
↓
2️⃣ HEIGHT CONTROL VALVE
Senses ride height and adds or exhausts air
↓
3️⃣ AIR LINES + FITTINGS
Carry air to the suspension
↓
4️⃣ AIR SPRINGS / BAGS
Support the vehicle load
↓
5️⃣ AXLE LOCATION COMPONENTS
Torque rods, bushings, shocks and mounts control axle movement
⬇️ One side low?
Check that side’s air bag, line/fittings, mounting and mechanical suspension components.
⬇️ Whole suspension low?
Check air supply, height-control valve operation, dump controls and shared plumbing.
💨 Leaks down parked?
Listen and use approved leak-detection methods at bags, fittings, lines and valves.
↕️ Ride height wrong?
Inspect height-control linkage and suspension condition, then verify adjustment using manufacturer specifications.
🧠 Simple rule: ONE SIDE low usually points local. BOTH SIDES low usually points toward something shared.
⚠️ Never work beneath a vehicle supported only by air suspension. Air bags can deflate suddenly. Properly support the vehicle first.
