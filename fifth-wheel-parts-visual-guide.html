🔒 FIFTH WHEEL PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Know what actually locks the tractor to the trailer.

A release handle position does not prove a safe coupling. Identify and inspect the actual lock, kingpin, mounting and slider components using the exact fifth-wheel manufacturer procedure.

Fifth Wheel Top Plate

Look for: Large greased horseshoe-shaped plate mounted behind the tractor cab.

Job: Supports the trailer upper coupler and houses the kingpin locking mechanism.

SEE COUPLING SYSTEM →

Throat

Look for: V-shaped/open channel at rear center of fifth-wheel plate.

Job: Guides the trailer kingpin into the locking mechanism during coupling.

WON'T LOCK →

Locking Jaws / Lock

Look for: Heavy steel locking pieces underneath the plate surrounding/capturing the kingpin.

Job: Mechanically captures the kingpin when correctly coupled.

DIAGNOSE LOCK →

Locking Bar / Wedge

Look for: Heavy sliding lock component underneath the fifth-wheel plate; design varies.

Job: Holds/adjusts the locking mechanism around the kingpin on applicable designs.

EXCESS PLAY →

Release Handle / Linkage

Look for: Long pull handle extending from side of fifth wheel with linkage underneath.

Job: Commands the locking mechanism to release when the coupling is properly unloaded.

WON'T RELEASE →

Trailer Kingpin

Look for: Vertical steel pin projecting downward from trailer upper coupler plate.

Job: Enters the fifth-wheel throat and is captured by the lock.

SEE TRAILER SIDE →

Pivots / Mounting Brackets

Look for: Heavy brackets and pivot pins supporting the fifth-wheel plate on the tractor.

Job: Secure the assembly while allowing designed fore/aft plate articulation.

CHECK MOVEMENT →

Slider Rails

Look for: Parallel toothed/holed rails under a sliding fifth wheel.

Job: Allow fore/aft fifth-wheel position adjustment while providing locking locations.

SEE SLIDER SYSTEM →

Slider Lock Pins

Look for: Heavy pins that extend into slider rail locking positions.

Job: Prevent the fifth wheel from sliding after adjustment.

CHECK SLIDER LOCK →

Trailer Upper Coupler Plate

Look for: Flat steel plate on trailer underside surrounding the kingpin.

Job: Rests on the fifth-wheel top plate and provides the trailer-side mating surface.
