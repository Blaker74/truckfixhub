If you hear the engine repeatedly turning while you hold the key/start button, choose YES. If it clicks, stays quiet, or the engine itself does not turn, choose NO.

Secure the vehicle before starting-system tests. Do not bypass neutral, clutch or other safety interlocks as a routine diagnostic shortcut.
