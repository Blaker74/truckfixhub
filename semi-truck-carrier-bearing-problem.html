⭕ CARRIER / SUPPORT BEARING

A damaged carrier bearing may be the victim of geometry, mounting or another driveline problem. Find out why it failed.

1️⃣ COMPLAINT: Record vibration/noise speed, load and whether the complaint changes on acceleration versus coast.
2️⃣ RUBBER SUPPORT: Inspect the support cushion for tearing, separation, distortion or evidence that the shaft is not centered normally.
3️⃣ BEARING: With the driveline safely secured, inspect for roughness, looseness and damage using the manufacturer's method.
4️⃣ BRACKET / MOUNT: Inspect mounting hardware, bracket condition and evidence of movement or misalignment.
5️⃣ SHAFT ALIGNMENT: Inspect adjoining shaft sections, U-joints, slip components and phasing. A support bearing cannot correct a driveline assembled or positioned incorrectly.
6️⃣ RIDE HEIGHT / ANGLES: Suspension ride height changes driveline geometry. Verify the vehicle sits at the correct specified height before final angle conclusions.
7️⃣ VERIFY: Correct the cause, not only the damaged bearing, then road-test through the original condition.
COMPLAINT → SUPPORT RUBBER → BEARING → MOUNT → SHAFT ALIGNMENT → RIDE HEIGHT / ANGLES → VERIFY.
