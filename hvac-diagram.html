❄️ HVAC

For A/C, follow the refrigerant loop. For heat, follow hot coolant and airflow.

1️⃣ COMPRESSOR
Moves refrigerant through the A/C system
↓
2️⃣ CONDENSER
Releases heat outside the cab
↓
3️⃣ METERING DEVICE
Controls refrigerant entering the evaporator
↓
4️⃣ EVAPORATOR
Absorbs heat from cab air
↓
5️⃣ BLOWER + DOORS
Move and direct conditioned air into the cab
🥵 A/C not cold?
Check airflow first, then compressor operation, condenser airflow and system pressures with proper equipment.
🌬️ Little or no airflow?
Check blower operation, fuse/power, cabin filter or restriction, and air-door operation.
🥶 No heat?
Check engine temperature, coolant level/flow, heater hoses, heater core and temperature-door operation.
↔️ Air from wrong vents?
Look at mode-door controls, actuators and related electrical or air-control circuits.
🧠 Simple rule: AIRFLOW and TEMPERATURE are two different problems. First ask: “Is air moving?” Then ask: “Is it the right temperature?”
⚠️ Refrigerant systems are pressurized. Use proper service equipment and never intentionally vent refrigerant.
