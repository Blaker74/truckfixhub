❄️ HVAC

First ask two questions: Is air moving? Is it the right temperature?

1️⃣ COMPRESSOR
Circulates refrigerant through the A/C system
↓
2️⃣ CONDENSER
Rejects heat from the refrigerant to outside air
↓
3️⃣ METERING DEVICE
Controls refrigerant flow into the evaporator
↓
4️⃣ EVAPORATOR
Absorbs heat from air moving toward the cab
↓
5️⃣ BLOWER + AIR DOORS
Move, mix and direct conditioned air through the cab vents
🥵 A/C not cold?
Make sure airflow is normal first. Then inspect compressor command/operation, condenser airflow and refrigerant-system performance with the proper service equipment.
🌬️ Little or no airflow?
Focus on the blower circuit, filters or restrictions, evaporator condition and air doors before chasing refrigerant problems.
🥶 No heat?
Verify engine operating temperature and coolant level, then check coolant flow through the heater circuit and temperature-door operation.
↔️ Air from wrong vents?
Focus on mode doors, actuators and their electrical, pneumatic or control circuits as equipped.

Airflow and temperature are separate diagnostic paths

A cab can have perfectly cold A/C components and still feel warm if not enough air moves through the evaporator. It can also have strong airflow but poor cooling because the refrigerant system is not transferring heat correctly. Separate airflow, temperature and air-direction complaints first; then test only the system responsible for that symptom.
