# TruckFixHub Sitewide Safety & Accuracy Audit

## Governing standard

**SAFETY → INTEGRITY → PEOPLE → ACCURACY → ORGANIZATION → USEFULNESS → GROWTH → REVENUE**

When safety and getting the truck moving conflict, safety wins.

**AUDIT-ONLY MODE:** No SEO expansion, new repair-guide topics, feature expansion or growth additions until this audit is complete. Changes during the audit are limited to safety, factual accuracy, privacy, integrity, broken routing and necessary corrections.

## Publication rules

Every technical page must satisfy these rules before it is considered audit-passed:
- No guessed specifications, torque values, pressures, temperatures, electrical values, fluid requirements or adjustment values.
- No symptom, warning or fault code may be presented as proof that a component failed.
- No unsafe continuation-of-operation advice.
- No bypassing warnings, protection logic, emissions equipment, brake/air safeguards, required lighting or electrical protection.
- No improvised procedures for safety-critical systems.
- No generic reset, forced-regeneration or adjustment procedure where application-specific OEM instructions control.
- Safety-critical symptoms must put stop-operation / professional-service guidance near the top when appropriate.
- Current manufacturer procedures and applicable inspection requirements control over generic web guidance.
- Community and What Fixed It outcomes are troubleshooting leads, not diagnoses.
- AI-assisted information never outranks diagnostic evidence, OEM information or qualified professional judgment.
- No fabricated repair counts, verification claims, testimonials or certainty.
- Internal links must resolve to real, intended pages.
- Page title, description and copy must accurately describe what the page can establish.

### Regulatory baseline confirmed for remaining vehicle-control pages
FMCSA safety guidance states a CMV should not be driven unless required safety-related parts/accessories are in good working order, including service brakes/trailer brake connections, parking brake, steering, lighting/reflectors, tires, wheels/rims and coupling devices. FMCSA also identifies unsafe operation, brake-system pressure loss, hub leaks and related defects as maintenance/safety concerns. Remaining pages in these categories will be judged against that baseline plus applicable OEM guidance.

## Audit completion definition
The audit is complete only when every public technical page has been reviewed; safety-critical pages put appropriate stop/escalation language near the top; manufacturer-specific claims requiring authoritative support are verified or removed; fault-code pages separate code meaning from root-cause diagnosis; no page encourages bypasses, unsafe continuation or blind parts replacement; intended internal links resolve; legacy routes are intentionally handled; sitemap/navigation match the final trusted library; and user/community tools cannot turn unverified information into apparent diagnostic certainty.

Pages are not marked passed merely because they existed previously or were SEO-optimized.
