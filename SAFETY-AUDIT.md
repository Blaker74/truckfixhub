# TruckFixHub Sitewide Safety & Accuracy Audit

## Governing standard

**SAFETY → INTEGRITY → PEOPLE → ACCURACY → ORGANIZATION → USEFULNESS → GROWTH → REVENUE**

When safety and getting the truck moving conflict, safety wins.

This audit is intentionally sitewide. Technical pages are reviewed as one library rather than being treated as isolated SEO articles.

## Publication rules

Every technical page must satisfy these rules before it is considered audit-passed:

- No guessed specifications, torque values, pressures, temperatures, electrical values, fluid requirements or adjustment values.
- No symptom, warning or fault code may be presented as proof that a component failed.
- No unsafe continuation-of-operation advice.
- No bypassing warnings, protection logic, emissions equipment, brake/air safeguards, required lighting or electrical protection.
- No improvised procedures for safety-critical systems.
- No generic reset, forced-regeneration or adjustment procedure where application-specific OEM instructions control.
- Safety-critical symptoms must put stop-operation / professional-service guidance near the top when appropriate.
- Current manufacturer procedures and applicable inspection requirements control over generic web guidance.
- Community and What Fixed It outcomes are troubleshooting leads, not diagnoses.
- AI-assisted information never outranks diagnostic evidence, OEM information or qualified professional judgment.
- No fabricated repair counts, verification claims, testimonials or certainty.
- Internal links must resolve to real, intended pages.
- Page title, description and copy must accurately describe what the page can establish.

## Audit completion definition

The sitewide audit is complete only when:

- every public technical page above has been reviewed against the publication rules;
- safety-critical pages have appropriate stop/escalation language near the top;
- manufacturer-specific facts that require authoritative sourcing have been verified or removed;
- fault-code pages clearly separate code meaning from root-cause diagnosis;
- no page encourages bypasses, unsafe continuation or blind parts replacement;
- all intended internal links resolve;
- legacy/duplicate routes are intentionally redirected, retained or removed;
- sitemap and navigation match the final trusted library;
- the user-facing tools and community surfaces cannot turn unverified information into apparent diagnostic certainty.

This file is the audit ledger. Pages are not marked passed merely because they existed previously or were SEO-optimized.
