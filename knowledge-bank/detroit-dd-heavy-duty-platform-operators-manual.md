## Engine identification notes
The manual identifies DD Platform components for multiple emissions generations, including EPA07, EPA10, GHG14 and GHG17 configurations. Component location and configuration can differ by engine model and generation.

The manual states that the fourteen-digit engine model and manufacturing serial number is etched on a pad at the left front of the engine cylinder block. Its example explains that the model digits identify the engine family and application and that the remaining digits include assembly-plant and serial-number information.

Examples shown in the manual include DD13 12.8 L, DD15 14.8 L and DD16 15.6 L applications across Freightliner, Western Star and other applications depending on emissions generation.

## Safety anchors
Preserve Detroit warnings whenever knowledge from this source is converted into simplified diagnostics. Important examples include:
- Do not use or read a diagnostic tool while the vehicle is moving.
- Park outside for parked regeneration because exhaust gases become extremely hot.
- Do not use ether or starting fluid on engines equipped with a manifold/grid heater.
- Disconnect or disable the starting system before service where accidental startup is possible.
- Never remove the cooling-system pressure cap at operating temperature.
- High-pressure fuel can penetrate skin; the manual directs shutting the engine down for a minimum of 10 minutes before servicing the high-pressure circuit.
- Follow battery, electrical, rotating-component, hot-surface, fuel-fire and aftertreatment safety precautions.
