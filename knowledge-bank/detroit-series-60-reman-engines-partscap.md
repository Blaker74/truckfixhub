## Detroit Reman Series 60 configuration types
### Complete Engine
A remanufactured complete engine intended as the most turn-key repair option. PartsCap describes these engines as remanufactured to original power-unit specifications.

### Three Quarter Engine
A more flexible remanufactured assembly. PartsCap states that electronic controls, manifolds, fuel pumps and fuel lines are added to complete the installation.

### Long Block Plus
A remanufactured long-block configuration designed to provide additional repair flexibility. PartsCap states rocker arms, injectors and the EGR cooler are added at service time.

### Long Block Basic
The basic remanufactured configuration. PartsCap identifies the included core components as the block, crankshaft, cylinder kits and cylinder head, allowing usable components from the customer takeout to be transferred as appropriate.

## Warranty information shown by PartsCap
### Complete & Three Quarter Engines
- Standard: 1 year / 100,000 miles
- PartsCap lists a 2 year / 200,000 mile option with StepUP registration
- PartsCap lists a 3 year / 300,000 mile option with StepUP registration and a fee

### Long Blocks
- Standard: 1 year / 100,000 miles

Warranty information can change. Verify current Detroit Reman warranty terms before representing coverage to a customer.
