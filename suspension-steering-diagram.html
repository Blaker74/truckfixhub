👇 Follow the complaint

↔️ PULLS TO ONE SIDE: First identify whether it pulls all the time, only while braking, under acceleration, or on certain road crowns. Check tires and pressures, brake drag, wheel-end condition and mechanical looseness before treating it as alignment alone.
〰️ STEERING WHEEL SHAKES: Record the speed and condition. Inspect steer tires/wheels and wheel ends, then steering/suspension looseness. If vibration is vehicle-speed related but not isolated to the steer axle, continue into the broader drivetrain vibration path.
↔️ WANDERS / FEELS LOOSE: Follow steering free play from wheel to road wheel one connection at a time. Inspect linkage, kingpins, wheel ends and axle-locating suspension parts before alignment.
💪 HARD STEERING: Separate loss of steering assist from mechanical binding. Check assist supply/operation as equipped and inspect linkage, kingpins and related joints before condemning the steering gear.
🛞 UNEVEN TIRE WEAR: Treat the wear pattern as evidence, not a diagnosis. Pressure, wheel-end movement, alignment, suspension condition, balance and operating conditions can create overlapping patterns.

🧠 Alignment comes after mechanical condition

1️⃣ Verify tires, pressure and wheel condition.
2️⃣ Find steering or wheel-end looseness and mechanical binding.
3️⃣ Verify suspension condition, axle location and ride height where applicable.
4️⃣ Measure alignment only after the parts controlling those measurements are mechanically sound.
