🛞 SUSPENSION & STEERING

Follow the driver’s steering input all the way to the tires.

1️⃣ STEERING WHEEL / COLUMN
Driver input starts here
↓
2️⃣ STEERING GEAR + LINKAGE
Transfers movement toward the wheels
↓
3️⃣ KINGPINS / KNUCKLES / TIE ROD
Allow and control wheel direction
↓
4️⃣ SPRINGS / AIR BAGS / TORQUE RODS
Support and locate the axle
↓
5️⃣ WHEELS + TIRES
Where looseness and alignment problems often show themselves
↔️ Wanders?
Check tire condition/pressure, steering free play, linkage, suspension movement and alignment.
🛞 Uneven tire wear?
Inspect alignment, loose/worn components, wheel ends and tire pressure before blaming the tire.
🔨 Clunk / knock?
Inspect joints, bushings, spring hardware, torque rods, shocks and mounting points.
💪 Hard steering?
Check steering fluid/system assist, linkage binding, front axle components and tire condition.
🧠 Simple rule: looseness anywhere between the steering wheel, axle and tire can change how the truck tracks.
⚠️ Steering and suspension are safety-critical. Support the vehicle correctly before inspecting components underneath it.
