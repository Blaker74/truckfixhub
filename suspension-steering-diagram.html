🛞 SUSPENSION & STEERING

Follow the driver’s steering input all the way to the tires.

1️⃣ STEERING WHEEL / COLUMN
Driver input starts here
↓
2️⃣ STEERING GEAR + LINKAGE
Convert and transfer steering movement toward the steer wheels
↓
3️⃣ KINGPINS / KNUCKLES / TIE ROD
Pivot and connect the steer wheels so they can change direction together
↓
4️⃣ SPRINGS / AIR SPRINGS / TORQUE RODS
Support, locate and control axle movement
↓
5️⃣ WHEELS + TIRES
Where alignment, looseness and suspension problems often leave visible clues
↔️ Wanders / loose steering?
Start with tire condition and pressure, then inspect steering free play, linkage, kingpins, suspension movement and alignment using the correct procedure.
🛞 Uneven tire wear?
Read the wear pattern as a clue. Check inflation, alignment, wheel ends and loose or worn steering/suspension components before blaming the tire.
🔨 Clunk / knock?
Inspect joints, bushings, spring hardware, torque rods, shocks and mounting points. Find when the noise occurs: steering, braking, bumps, acceleration or axle movement.
💪 Hard steering?
Check the steering-assist system, fluid where applicable, belt/drive where applicable, linkage binding, axle components and tires before condemning the steering gear.

Steering complaints can start outside the steering gear

A truck that wanders, pulls, feels loose or wears tires unevenly can have causes in the tires, wheel ends, steering linkage, kingpins, axle location, suspension or alignment. Inspect the whole path and measure free play or alignment to the manufacturer’s specifications instead of adjusting or replacing parts by feel alone.

🧠 Simple rule: the tire follows the axle, and the axle follows the steering and suspension. Looseness or movement anywhere in that chain can change how the truck tracks.
⚠️ Steering and suspension are safety-critical. Secure and properly support the vehicle before inspection and follow manufacturer inspection, torque and adjustment procedures.
