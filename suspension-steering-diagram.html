👇 Follow the complaint

↔️ PULL: Determine whether it pulls constantly, during braking, under acceleration or mainly on certain road crowns. Check tires, brake drag and mechanical condition before alignment.
🛣️ WANDER / LOOSE: Follow free play from steering wheel to road wheel one connection at a time. Several small loose points can stack into a large road complaint.
💪 HARD STEERING: Separate loss of hydraulic/electric assist as equipped from mechanical binding in linkage, kingpins or joints.
〰️ SHAKE: Record exact road speed and condition. Start with steer tires/wheels and wheel ends, then steering/suspension looseness. Continue into driveline diagnosis if the vibration is not isolated to the steer axle.
🛞 UNEVEN WEAR: Treat the pattern as evidence. Pressure, wheel-end movement, steering looseness, suspension, alignment, balance and service conditions can overlap.
🔨 CLUNK: Identify what movement causes the noise—bump, brake, steering, acceleration or chassis twist—then look for witness marks and movement in the corresponding parts.
🔩 KINGPIN PLAY: Movement at the tire is not enough. Separate kingpin/knuckle movement from wheel-bearing and linkage movement using the axle manufacturer’s procedure.

🧠 Alignment comes last

1️⃣ Verify tires, inflation and wheel condition.
2️⃣ Find steering, kingpin or wheel-end looseness and mechanical binding.
3️⃣ Verify suspension condition, axle location and ride height where applicable.
4️⃣ Measure alignment only after the components controlling those measurements are mechanically sound.
