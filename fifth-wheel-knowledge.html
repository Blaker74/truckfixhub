SAF-HOLLAND

HOLLAND

Common North American families include FW35 and related FW31/FW33 service literature, FW17 and lightweight FWAL products. The FW35 owner's literature covers identification, coupling/uncoupling, sliding fifth-wheel positioning, maintenance, lubrication, adjustment, bracket inspection and troubleshooting.

Useful identification: capture the model/serial information from the fifth wheel before selecting rebuild parts. Do not assume two HOLLAND top plates use the same lock or rebuild components because they look similar.

JOST INTERNATIONAL

JOST

Major North American product families include JSK37U, JSK36N, lightweight JSK3CN and terminal-tractor JSK37Y. JOST also publishes replacement-top-plate families for selected HOLLAND and Fontaine applications.

Coupling check: JOST operating literature requires a visual inspection after coupling; a pull test alone is not enough. Confirm no gap at the trailer/fifth-wheel interface, the kingpin is inside the lock and the release handle is in the correct locked position for the model.

Coupling: never trust sound alone

A fifth wheel can appear to couple and still be unsafe. After coupling, perform the manufacturer-required visual inspection and coupling verification. Look for proper trailer contact, correct kingpin position in the lock and the correct release-handle/lock indication for that exact model.

Incorrect trailer height can cause a high-hook or improper coupling. Manufacturer operating instructions should control the exact procedure.

Kingpin & upper coupler matter too

Do not diagnose every coupling complaint as a fifth-wheel failure. Inspect the trailer kingpin and upper coupler condition using the applicable limits and gauges. A damaged or out-of-spec mating component can create difficult coupling, excess movement or unsafe lock engagement.

Lubrication

Lubrication requirements depend on the fifth-wheel design. Conventional lubricated wheels require the specified lubrication on lock/mechanism and trailer-contact areas; low-lube designs have different service requirements. JOST maintenance literature specifies lithium-based grease with an EP additive on the trailer-contact surface for applicable models. HOLLAND and Fontaine publish model-specific lubrication/maintenance instructions.

Rule: identify conventional vs low-lube configuration before applying a generic grease procedure.

Lock adjustment is model-specific

Free play at the kingpin must be evaluated with the manufacturer's inspection and adjustment procedure. Do not turn an adjustment screw merely because a fifth wheel feels loose. Determine whether wear is in the lock components, kingpin, brackets, bushings or another part of the coupling system.

Some designs are factory-adjusted or use procedures/tools that differ significantly by family.

Slider will not release

Separate the top-plate lock from the slider mechanism. Check air supply/control where applicable, release-cylinder operation, plungers, engagement rack/track condition, contamination, binding and damaged/missing parts. JOST publishes separate inboard/outboard slider troubleshooting and winterization guidance; HOLLAND and Fontaine also publish model-specific slider service information.

Hard to couple or release

First verify trailer height and alignment, correct lubrication, kingpin/upper-coupler condition, lock cleanliness and complete mechanism movement. Inspect for bent, worn, broken or contaminated components. Do not force a release mechanism or grind/modify lock parts to make a coupling complaint disappear.

Before ordering a rebuild kit

  • Brand and exact fifth-wheel family/model.
  • Serial number or production identification where available.
  • Top plate vs complete assembly.
  • Stationary vs sliding mounting.
  • Inboard/outboard/no-tilt bracket style where applicable.
  • Manual, air, retractable or other release configuration.
  • Standard-lube vs low-lube configuration.
  • Actual failed/worn components confirmed by inspection.

Replacement top plates are not universal

JOST publishes replacement options for selected HOLLAND and Fontaine models, but compatibility has qualifications. For example, JOST's HOLLAND replacement information lists selected FW8, FW17, FW35, FW2000/2500/2535, FW3501 and FWAL applications and notes an installed-height change. Its Fontaine replacement literature likewise lists selected families and exclusions.

Never cross a top plate from brand/model appearance alone. Verify the current manufacturer's compatibility document, bracket/application and installed-height effect.

TruckFixHub fifth-wheel diagnostic order

Identification → trailer height/alignment → kingpin & upper coupler → lock engagement → release mechanism → bracket/mounting → slider system → lubrication/adjustment → final coupling verification.

This order keeps a coupling complaint from becoming an unverified parts swap.

Safety boundary

The fifth wheel is a safety-critical coupling. Do not operate a fifth wheel that does not couple, lock, release or slide correctly. Never substitute TruckFixHub summaries for the current manufacturer inspection, adjustment, rebuild, installation or coupling procedure. Support the trailer/tractor correctly before service and use qualified personnel for repairs.
