About

Truck knowledge that starts with the problem.

TruckFixHub is an independent educational website operated by Blake Pierce, an outside sales professional working in truck parts.

Why this site exists

A useful parts conversation needs more than a truck badge and a guess. The truck, engine, component build, previous repairs, and actual symptom all matter. TruckFixHub organizes those details into plain-language guides for drivers, owners, shop staff, and parts professionals.

What you will find

The library covers parts identification, starting and charging concerns, air systems, cooling, aftertreatment, and other heavy-duty systems. Its purpose is to help you describe a complaint and understand the next diagnostic question. It does not certify a vehicle as safe or establish fitment for a particular VIN.

Independent guidance

TruckFixHub is not an OEM service department and is not affiliated with the manufacturers linked in the resource directory. Brand names identify relevant equipment and sources. Manufacturer publications remain the authority for application-specific specifications and procedures.

Corrections and responsibility

Blake Pierce is responsible for the site. If an explanation is unclear or wrong, send the page address, the issue, and any relevant manufacturer reference to support@truckfixhub.com. Do not email passwords or confidential customer records.
