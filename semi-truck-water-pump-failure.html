Semi Truck Water Pump Failure Symptoms

A coolant leak near the pump is one clue, but overheating or noise alone does not prove the water pump has failed.

Work safely

Never open a hot pressurized cooling system. Keep hands and tools clear of belts, pulleys and fans. Follow OEM procedures before inspection or disassembly.

Possible clues

  • Coolant residue or active leakage near the pump
  • Bearing or pulley noise
  • Abnormal pulley movement where applicable
  • Overheating that requires circulation testing
  • Repeated coolant loss

Other cooling-system faults can produce similar symptoms, so verify the cause before replacing the pump.
