👇 Follow the complaint

🚫 WON’T GO INTO GEAR: Identify manual, automated manual or automatic first. Save faults. Verify clutch release or required input conditions, then power/air/control inputs and requested versus actual gear before moving toward internal transmission diagnosis.
↕️ ROUGH / LATE / MISSED SHIFT: Record the exact gear or transition, load, temperature, road speed and throttle condition. One bad shift provides different evidence than every shift being wrong.
🔊 NOISE: Determine whether the noise follows engine RPM or road speed and whether it changes by gear, acceleration, coast or neutral. Separate transmission noise from clutch, driveshaft, axle and wheel-end sources.
💧 LEAK: Clean and trace the fluid to its true source. A wet seal area does not prove the seal is the original cause; check lubricant level, venting and operating condition as applicable.
⚠️ FAULT CODE: Save the complete code and operating conditions. Electronically controlled transmissions may depend on power, grounds, network information, sensors, actuators and pneumatic/electromechanical systems.

🧠 Four questions before major transmission work

1️⃣ Can torque enter correctly? Check clutch release/condition or the appropriate converter/input system.
2️⃣ Is the correct gear being commanded? Compare driver request, control inputs and requested/actual gear where supported.
3️⃣ Is the problem truly internal? Prove input and control systems before disassembly.
4️⃣ Could the complaint be after the transmission? Road-speed noise, vibration or harshness can originate in driveshaft, axle or wheel-end components.
