⚙️ TRANSMISSION

First decide: is the problem getting power IN, changing gears, or sending power OUT?

1️⃣ ENGINE / CLUTCH OR TORQUE CONVERTER
Power enters the transmission
↓
2️⃣ CONTROLS / SHIFT COMMAND
Driver input and electronic or pneumatic controls request the gear
↓
3️⃣ TRANSMISSION
Internal gears and components change ratio
↓
4️⃣ OUTPUT
Power leaves through the output shaft
↓
5️⃣ DRIVESHAFT → DRIVE AXLE
Power continues toward the wheels
🚫 Won’t go into gear?
Check clutch/release or converter-related conditions, shift controls, air/electrical supply and active transmission faults.
↕️ Shifts badly?
Note exactly which shift fails and under what load. Check controls, sensors, fluid where applicable and fault codes.
🔊 Noise?
Determine whether noise follows engine RPM, transmission gear, road speed, load or coast.
💧 Fluid / oil leak?
Clean the area and identify the true source before replacing a seal or gasket.
🧠 Simple rule: “Transmission problem” is too broad. Identify INPUT, SHIFT/CONTROL, INTERNAL, or OUTPUT first.
⚠️ Secure the vehicle before inspection. Never crawl under a vehicle that is supported only by air suspension or a jack.
