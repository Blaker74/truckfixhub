🔩 DRIVETRAIN & VIBRATION DIAGNOSTICS

First question: does the complaint follow engine RPM or road speed?

〰️ Shakes at Highway Speed🛞 Wheel / Tire Vibration⚙️ Transmission / Output🔩 Axle / Differential
1️⃣ ENGINE / INPUT
Creates and transfers torque
↓
2️⃣ TRANSMISSION OUTPUT
Sends torque into the driveline
↓
3️⃣ DRIVESHAFT / U-JOINTS
Carry rotating torque toward the axle
↓
4️⃣ DIFFERENTIAL / AXLES
Change direction and distribute torque
↓
5️⃣ WHEELS / TIRES
Put torque to the road and rotate at vehicle speed

👇 Follow when it happens

〰️ HIGHWAY-SPEED VIBRATION: Separate engine-speed from road-speed vibration first. If it follows road speed, inspect wheels/tires as well as driveshafts, U-joints, support bearings, runout, balance, angles and axle/wheel-end sources.
🔊 CLUNK ON TAKEOFF / PULL-TO-COAST: Record exactly when torque changes direction. Inspect driveline connections, U-joints, slip joints and mounts, then move toward transmission output or axle/differential backlash when the evidence leads there.
⚙️ ENGINE REVS BUT TRUCK DOESN’T PULL: Follow torque from clutch/converter through transmission output, driveshaft and axle until you find the first point where power stops transferring correctly.
💧 GEAR-OIL LEAK: Clean and trace the leak to its true source. Airflow and rotating components can move lubricant away from the original leak. Check venting and level using the correct procedure.

🧠 Four clues that narrow vibration fast

1️⃣ SPEED: Engine RPM or vehicle speed?
2️⃣ LOAD: Acceleration, steady pull, coast or all the time?
3️⃣ GEAR: One gear or the same road speed in multiple gears?
4️⃣ LOCATION: Steering wheel, seat/floor or whole vehicle is useful evidence—but not proof of the source.
