🔩 DRIVETRAIN

Follow the power from the engine to the drive tires.

1️⃣ ENGINE
Produces torque
↓
2️⃣ CLUTCH / TORQUE CONVERTER
Transfers engine torque into the transmission
↓
3️⃣ TRANSMISSION
Changes ratio and sends torque to the output
↓
4️⃣ DRIVESHAFT + U-JOINTS
Carry rotating torque between the transmission and drive axle
↓
5️⃣ DIFFERENTIAL + AXLES → TIRES
Distribute torque to the drive wheels
〰️ Vibration?
First decide whether it follows engine RPM or road speed. Then inspect the related driveline, tires/wheels and wheel-end components using the correct procedures.
🔊 Clunk?
Check for excessive movement or wear in U-joints, slip joints, mounts, driveline connections and axle/differential components.
⚙️ Won’t move / slips?
Determine whether torque is being lost at the clutch/converter, transmission, driveshaft or drive axle before replacing parts.
💧 Gear oil leak?
Clean and identify the actual leak source. Check lubricant level and related venting or component condition before assuming the seal alone is the cause.

When the symptom happens helps locate it

A drivetrain vibration that changes with road speed points in a different direction than one that follows engine RPM. A clunk only during acceleration or coast can give a different clue than one heard while turning or shifting. Record when the complaint starts, changes and disappears, then inspect the matching section of the power path.

🧠 Simple rule: ask WHEN — engine RPM, road speed, acceleration, coast, turning or shifting. Timing helps narrow the drivetrain section.
⚠️ Never inspect or work near a rotating driveshaft. Secure and properly support the vehicle and follow manufacturer driveline service procedures.
