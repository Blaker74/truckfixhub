🔩 DRIVETRAIN

Follow the power from the engine to the drive tires.

1️⃣ ENGINE
Produces torque
↓
2️⃣ CLUTCH / TORQUE CONVERTER
Transfers engine torque into the transmission
↓
3️⃣ TRANSMISSION
Changes ratio and sends torque to the output
↓
4️⃣ DRIVESHAFT + U-JOINTS
Carry rotating torque between the transmission and drive axle
↓
5️⃣ DIFFERENTIAL + AXLES → TIRES
Distribute torque to the drive wheels

👇 PICK THE COMPLAINT

〰️ It vibrates going down the road

  1. Decide whether the vibration follows engine RPM or vehicle speed.
  2. If it follows road speed, inspect tires/wheels and wheel-related causes as well as the driveline.
  3. Inspect driveshafts, U-joints, slip joints, support bearings and driveline connections with the vehicle safely secured.
  4. Consider driveline angle, runout, balance or damaged components when basic inspection does not reveal the cause.
  5. Do not assume every road-speed vibration is a driveshaft problem.

🔊 It clunks when taking off or changing from pull to coast

  1. Note exactly when the clunk occurs: takeoff, shift, acceleration, coast, reverse or turning.
  2. With the vehicle secured, inspect driveline connections and U-joints for abnormal wear or movement using approved procedures.
  3. Inspect slip joints, mounts and related connections.
  4. If driveline components are sound, continue toward transmission output and differential / axle backlash or wear diagnosis.
  5. Repair the proven source of excessive movement instead of replacing parts based only on noise location.

⚙️ Engine revs but the truck will not pull correctly

  1. Determine whether the complaint began suddenly or has been getting worse.
  2. On a manual system, check the clutch / engagement path; on an automatic or automated system, check transmission faults and operating data.
  3. Verify the transmission output is actually transferring torque into the driveline.
  4. Inspect driveshaft and axle connections for a mechanical disconnect or failure.
  5. Follow the power path until you find the first point where torque no longer transfers correctly.

When the symptom happens helps locate it

A drivetrain vibration that changes with road speed points in a different direction than one that follows engine RPM. A clunk only during acceleration or coast can give a different clue than one heard while turning or shifting. Record when the complaint starts, changes and disappears, then inspect the matching section of the power path.
