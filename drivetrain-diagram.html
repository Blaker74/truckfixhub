🔩 DRIVETRAIN

Follow the power from the engine to the tires.

1️⃣ ENGINE
Makes the power
↓
2️⃣ CLUTCH / TORQUE CONVERTER
Connects engine power to the transmission
↓
3️⃣ TRANSMISSION
Changes gear ratio and sends power rearward
↓
4️⃣ DRIVESHAFT + U-JOINTS
Carry power to the drive axle
↓
5️⃣ DIFFERENTIAL + AXLES → TIRES
Turn driveshaft rotation into wheel rotation
〰️ Vibration?
Look at speed/RPM pattern, driveshaft condition, U-joints, slip joints, angles, tires and wheel ends.
🔊 Clunk?
Check driveline free play, U-joints, slip joints, mounts and differential-related movement.
⚙️ Won’t move / slips?
Determine whether power is being lost before, inside or after the transmission.
💧 Gear oil leak?
Find the exact source before assuming the seal itself caused the failure.
🧠 Simple rule: identify WHEN the problem happens — engine RPM, road speed, acceleration, coast, turning or shifting. That helps locate the section of the drivetrain.
⚠️ Never inspect a rotating driveshaft underneath a running vehicle. Secure the vehicle before inspection.
