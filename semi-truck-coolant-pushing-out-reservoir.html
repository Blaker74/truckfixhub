🌊 COOLANT PUSHING OUT

Reservoir overflow is a symptom. Determine whether the system is overheating, overfilled, unable to control pressure, or being pressurized abnormally.

1️⃣ WHEN DOES IT HAPPEN? Cold start, idle, heavy pull, after shutdown, regeneration, or only when fully hot?
2️⃣ VERIFY TRUE TEMPERATURE: Determine whether the engine is actually overheating. Follow fan, airflow, coolant circulation and restriction paths if temperature is high.
3️⃣ LEVEL / CAP / RESERVOIR: Verify correct cold fill level and inspect cap, neck, reservoir and return/vent routing per the vehicle procedure.
4️⃣ PRESSURE BEHAVIOR: Abnormally rapid pressure rise from cold can be important evidence. Use the OEM cooling-system pressure/combustion-gas diagnostic method rather than judging bubbles alone.
5️⃣ ENGINE-SPECIFIC SOURCES: Depending on engine design, abnormal cooling-system pressurization can involve combustion sealing, cylinder head/liner areas, compressor interfaces or other coolant passages. Test them in the manufacturer-directed order.
6️⃣ VERIFY UNDER ORIGINAL LOAD: After repair, recreate the condition that caused coolant loss and confirm temperature, pressure behavior and level remain normal.
WHEN → TRUE TEMP → FILL/CAP/VENT → PRESSURE BEHAVIOR → OEM SOURCE TESTING → PROVE → VERIFY UNDER ORIGINAL CONDITION.
⚠️ Never remove a pressure cap from a hot cooling system. Coolant can cause severe burns. Abnormal pressurization requires engine-specific service information.
