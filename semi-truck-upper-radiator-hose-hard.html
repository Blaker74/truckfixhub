🧱 UPPER RADIATOR HOSE HARD

A firm hose on a hot pressurized system can be normal. The diagnostic question is whether pressure builds at the wrong time, exceeds the expected behavior, or comes with coolant loss/overheating.

HOT ENGINE: Cooling systems normally build pressure as temperature rises. Hose feel alone cannot diagnose a head gasket.
COLD / RAPID PRESSURE: Pressure appearing unusually quickly from a truly cold start deserves proper pressure-behavior and engine-specific testing.
CHECK TEMPERATURE: If overheating accompanies the complaint, diagnose fan, airflow, coolant circulation, restriction and coolant condition/level.
CHECK CAP / SYSTEM: Verify the correct pressure cap and reservoir/vent configuration. A wrong or faulty component can change pressure behavior.
PROVE ABNORMAL PRESSURIZATION: Use the current OEM test procedure for combustion gas or other engine-specific pressure sources. Do not diagnose by squeezing a hose or watching bubbles alone.
COLD OR HOT? → TEMPERATURE → CAP/SYSTEM → MEASURE PRESSURE BEHAVIOR → OEM SOURCE TEST → PROVE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Never squeeze, disconnect or open hot pressurized cooling-system components in a way that exposes you to coolant. Use proper test equipment and procedures.
