Diagram system
1 · Start with the evidenceA code or symptom identifies where to begin; it does not automatically condemn a component.
2 · Follow the test pathEach branch asks what can be observed or proven next.
3 · Verify the fixThe original operating condition should be recreated whenever practical before the job is considered complete.
Safety: Diagnostic diagrams are educational starting points, not replacements for OEM procedures, specifications, wiring information or qualified repair judgment. Safety-critical systems require proper procedures and equipment.
