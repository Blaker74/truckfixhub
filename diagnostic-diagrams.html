👣 How To Use A Picture Path

Enter with the symptom or code you actually have. Move one decision at a time. When a branch calls for evidence, test before advancing. If the evidence does not support that branch, return to the decision point instead of forcing the diagnosis toward a suspected part.

When several codes or symptoms appear together, investigate what they share—power, ground, network, air, fluid supply or mechanical system—before assuming several unrelated components failed at once.

🧠 Why These Diagrams Are Different

TruckFixHub diagrams are original diagnostic organization tools. They simplify the reasoning path without pretending to replace manufacturer service information. The goal is to help a technician decide what evidence is needed next—not copy proprietary manuals or invent exact specifications we cannot verify.
