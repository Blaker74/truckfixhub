Where moisture appears helps tell you which part of the path needs attention.

🛢️ Water drains from a reservoir

Record which reservoir, how much liquid is present, how often it returns, weather conditions and dryer service history. A small observation and a recurring moisture problem are not the same diagnosis.

❄️ Problem appears in freezing weather

Preserve the temperature pattern. Note whether the symptom is slow air build, no air delivery to a circuit, repeated dryer behavior, brake release trouble or another specific change. Cold weather can expose moisture or restriction, but temperature alone does not name the failed component.

🛢️ Oily residue is present

Describe what was actually found instead of calling every dark residue compressor oil. Location, amount, recurrence and system service history matter. If oil carryover is suspected, the compressor and air-treatment path require application-specific inspection.

Water, oil and freezing are clues. Record the location and pattern, then prove where air treatment or delivery stopped working correctly.

🛑 SAFETY: Moisture or freezing that interferes with air-pressure build, brake application or brake release can make the vehicle unsafe to operate. Do not use improvised heat, flame or unapproved chemicals on brake or air-system components. Secure the vehicle and follow the correct manufacturer cold-weather and air-system service procedures.
