What changed in the way it runs?

Choose the strongest symptom first. One complaint can have several causes, so prove the system before buying parts.

🐌Low PowerWeak under load, slow acceleration or cannot pull like it normally does.🌀Low BoostBoost is low or slow to build. Check the air path before condemning the turbocharger.🌫️SmokeBlack, white or blue smoke. Smoke color and operating condition help narrow the path.〰️Rough / MissRough idle, stumble, cylinder miss, surge or unstable engine speed.⚠️Derate / Limited SpeedPower is electronically limited. Capture active fault codes before clearing anything.⛽Fuel Use IncreasedFuel economy dropped without an obvious change in route, load or driving conditions.
Diagnostic rule: Low power does not automatically mean turbo failure. Air restriction, charge-air leaks, fuel delivery, sensors, exhaust restriction, aftertreatment and commanded derates can all produce similar complaints.
Safety: Use appropriate precautions around hot exhaust components, rotating machinery and pressurized intake/fuel systems. Confirm procedures and limits using current service information for the exact engine and vehicle.
