Low power is not a turbo diagnosis

Air supply → charge-air integrity → fuel delivery → combustion → exhaust flow → electronic command.

A restricted intake, charge-air leak, fuel-delivery problem, inaccurate input, exhaust restriction, aftertreatment condition or commanded derate can all feel like a weak turbo. Test the system before buying the expensive part.

Weak only under load

Record boost behavior, smoke, engine speed, road load, gear, related faults and whether the complaint becomes worse as demand rises. Problems that appear only at high demand often expose restrictions, leaks or supply limitations that may not be obvious at idle.

Weak all the time

Compare commanded operation, active faults, basic air/fuel supply and whether the engine is electronically limited. A constant complaint needs a different path than a truck that performs normally until a grade or heavy pull.

Smoke is evidence

  • Black smoke: consider the relationship between available air, commanded fuel, boost/charge-air integrity and combustion.
  • White smoke: document temperature and operating condition and investigate incomplete combustion or fluid entry with proper testing.
  • Blue smoke: investigate oil entering the combustion/exhaust path rather than treating color alone as proof of a specific component.

Smoke color narrows the investigation; it does not identify a failed part by itself.

Evidence worth capturing

Record whether the complaint occurs at idle, acceleration, sustained load or all conditions; complete active/inactive fault information; boost behavior; visible smoke and when it appears; recent repairs; fluid consumption; and whether a derate or warning is active. Exact pressure, temperature, flow and performance specifications vary by engine and vehicle and must be verified with current service information.

Engine diagnostic safety

Use appropriate precautions around hot exhaust components, rotating machinery, high-current starting systems and pressurized intake/fuel systems. Do not perform unsafe running tests or defeat engine/aftertreatment protections. Confirm procedures and limits using current service information for the exact engine and vehicle.
