↔️ STEERING & SUSPENSION — SEE IT & NAME IT

Recognize the parts that steer, support and locate the truck.

Steering looseness, suspension damage and incorrect ride height are safety-critical. Identify where movement actually occurs before replacing parts, and use the exact manufacturer inspection limits and procedures.

Tie Rod / Tie-Rod Ends

Look for: Long steering link spanning between steer knuckles with ball-joint ends.

Job: Keeps steer wheels linked so they turn together.

SEE STEERING →

Drag Link

Look for: Steering link with ball/socket ends connecting steering arm/pitman arm to the steer axle linkage.

Job: Transfers steering gear movement to the steer wheels.

DIAGNOSE WANDER →

Steering Gear

Look for: Heavy gearbox mounted to frame with steering input shaft, hydraulic hoses and pitman arm/output.

Job: Converts steering-wheel input into force that moves the steering linkage.

DIAGNOSE HARD STEERING →

Kingpin / Steer Knuckle Area

Look for: Vertical pivot through the steer axle end/knuckle area.

Job: Allows the steer knuckle and wheel to pivot for steering while carrying load.

DIAGNOSE KINGPIN PLAY →

Leaf Spring

Look for: Stacked curved steel spring leaves clamped together above/below an axle.

Job: Supports vehicle weight and helps locate/control axle movement.

SEE SUSPENSION →

Air Spring / Air Bag

Look for: Large rubber bellows between axle/suspension and frame.

Job: Supports load with compressed air and provides suspension compliance.

SEE AIR SUSPENSION →

Shock Absorber

Look for: Cylindrical damper mounted between suspension/axle and frame.

Job: Controls suspension oscillation and bounce.

SEE SUSPENSION →

Torque Rod / Radius Rod

Look for: Heavy straight link with large rubber-bushed eyes between axle and frame/suspension structure.

Job: Controls axle position and reaction to drive/brake forces.

SEE AXLE LOCATION →

Height Control Valve

Look for: Small air valve mounted to frame with linkage arm connected to axle/suspension.

Job: Adds or exhausts air to maintain designed ride height.

DIAGNOSE RIDE HEIGHT →

Axle / Suspension Mounting Area

Look for: Axle beam/housing with spring seats, U-bolts, hangers, bushings or air-suspension attachment points.

Job: Carries wheel loads while suspension hardware controls axle position relative to the frame.
