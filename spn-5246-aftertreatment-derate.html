TRUCKFIXHUB FAULT CODE GUIDE

SPN 5246

Aftertreatment inducement / operator warning and derate context.

What SPN 5246 tells you

SPN 5246 is commonly encountered in the context of an aftertreatment inducement or derate condition. It should be interpreted together with its FMI, the other active faults, dashboard warnings, engine family and OEM service information. It is usually not useful to treat SPN 5246 as a stand-alone component diagnosis.

Record these first

  • The complete SPN and FMI combination
  • Every other active aftertreatment or engine fault
  • DEF and DPF warning-lamp status
  • Current derate message or operating restriction
  • Recent regen history
  • Any recent DEF, sensor, doser or aftertreatment repairs

Why related faults matter

An inducement code can be the consequence of an unresolved emissions-system fault. Diagnosing the underlying fault rather than guessing at the inducement code is the important next step.

Do not ignore a derate

Follow the vehicle and engine manufacturer's warnings. If continued operation is restricted or unsafe, arrange qualified repair assistance rather than attempting to defeat or bypass the emissions system.
