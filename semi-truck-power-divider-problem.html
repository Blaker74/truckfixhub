🚛 POWER DIVIDER / INTER-AXLE PROBLEM

Tandem drives have an additional torque path. First determine whether the complaint is control, engagement or internal torque transfer.

1️⃣ IDENTIFY THE AXLE SYSTEM: Record axle manufacturer/model and the exact inter-axle differential/power-divider arrangement before testing.
2️⃣ DEFINE THE COMPLAINT: Won’t lock, won’t unlock, no drive, warning indicator, noise, bind, or failure only on low-traction surfaces?
3️⃣ COMMAND / CONTROL: Depending on design, verify switch request, air supply/lines, solenoid or electrical command and indicator logic before condemning internal hardware.
4️⃣ ACTUATION: Determine whether the locking mechanism actually moves/responds using the axle manufacturer’s procedure. A valid command does not prove mechanical engagement.
5️⃣ TORQUE PATH: If no-drive or abnormal transfer remains, follow torque through forward axle, inter-axle shaft and rear axle to isolate where transfer stops.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Confirm normal lock/unlock indication and torque distribution only under the operating conditions permitted by the manufacturer.
IDENTIFY SYSTEM → COMPLAINT → COMMAND → AIR / ELECTRICAL CONTROL → ACTUATION → TORQUE PATH → INTERNAL ONLY WHEN PROVEN → VERIFY.
⚠️ Improper differential-lock operation can cause driveline windup or component damage. Follow the vehicle/axle manufacturer’s engagement and road-use instructions.
