📏 BRAKE CHAMBER STROKE OUT OF RANGE

Excessive pushrod travel is evidence of a brake-system problem. Do not make repeated manual slack-adjuster corrections and call it fixed.

1️⃣ IDENTIFY COMPONENT: Determine chamber type/size, brake type and the applicable stroke limit from current specifications.
2️⃣ MEASURE CORRECTLY: Follow the prescribed pressure/application and measurement procedure. Incorrect test pressure or reference points can create bad conclusions.
3️⃣ COMPARE POSITIONS: Compare axle mates and look for one brake that behaves differently.
4️⃣ FOUNDATION BRAKE: Inspect friction wear, drum/rotor, cam or caliper mechanism, bushings/hardware and mechanical movement as applicable.
5️⃣ SLACK ADJUSTER / LINKAGE: Inspect installation, geometry and automatic adjuster operation using manufacturer procedures. Repeated loss of adjustment means the underlying fault remains.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Re-measure stroke after repair and complete the required brake inspection.
IDENTIFY CHAMBER → CORRECT TEST → MEASURE → FOUNDATION BRAKE → ADJUSTER/LINKAGE → REPAIR → RE-MEASURE.
⚠️ Brake stroke limits are component-specific and regulated. Use the current chamber/brake manufacturer specifications and applicable inspection rules.
