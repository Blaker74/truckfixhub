↔️ STEERING WANDERS / FEELS LOOSE

Wandering is often a stack-up of small movements. Follow free play from the steering wheel all the way to the road wheels.

1️⃣ DEFINE IT: Constant wander, road-crown sensitivity, crosswind only, loaded/unloaded, or after hitting bumps?
2️⃣ TIRES FIRST: Verify steer-tire pressure, condition, size/match and obvious irregular wear before adjusting steering parts.
3️⃣ TRACE FREE PLAY: With approved inspection methods, watch column/shaft, steering gear output, drag link, steering arms, tie rod, kingpins and wheel ends. Find where input stops becoming wheel movement.
4️⃣ AXLE LOCATION: Inspect springs, bushings, torque rods and other axle-locating parts as equipped. The axle must stay where alignment measurements assume it is.
5️⃣ RIDE HEIGHT / ALIGNMENT: Verify mechanical condition and ride height before using alignment to compensate for worn or loose components.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Road-test safely under the original load/speed/road condition.
DEFINE WANDER → TIRES → TRACE FREE PLAY → AXLE LOCATION → RIDE HEIGHT → ALIGNMENT → VERIFY.
⚠️ Steering looseness is safety-critical. Do not return the truck to service until unsafe movement is corrected to manufacturer limits.
