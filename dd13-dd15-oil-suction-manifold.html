DD13 / DD15 Low Oil Pressure: Oil Suction Manifold Issues

Manufacturer documents and a public repair discussion describe different oil suction manifold concerns. Here is what they establish—and what remains unconfirmed.

TruckFixHub editorial overview · Sources reviewed October 11, 2026

Low oil pressure needs prompt attention. Follow your truck’s warning and shutdown instructions. A similar online story is not a diagnosis for your engine.

Two manufacturer documents, different scopes

Manufacturer campaign

Certain 2014 engines: D18M3 REV2

Detroit’s campaign covers specified model-year 2014 DD13, DD15 and DD16 engines built March 1–April 30, 2014 with the previous manifold installed. It says replacing that manifold may prevent its failure, low oil pressure fault codes and engine damage. Some engines had already been repaired and were excluded.

The prescribed correction is manifold replacement using Detroit’s service procedures. This is limited campaign coverage, not evidence that every DD13 or DD15 needs the same repair. The document’s no-charge deadline was July 31, 2020; it does not establish present-day free repair eligibility.

Manufacturer field information

Weld and end-cap failures: CX Advocate, April 2026

A Detroit CX Advocate slide identifies failures at the manifold end-cap weld and drain-back valve cover weld, plus holes found in the bridge. It associates these concerns with a check-engine light and reduced oil pressure on DD13, DD15 and DD16 engines.

The slide reports weld failures on components built since May 2016 and says some manifolds made in 2018–2019 may have a hole. It notes declining failures following a supplier machine change in December 2020. These are component production observations, not blanket truck-model-year eligibility.

The field solution shown is manifold replacement. A cap-retaining bracket was described as being tested; this slide does not establish an approved DIY repair. It also describes a new design under development, not a confirmed release or fitment.

A reported shop experience

Public anecdote · Outcome not confirmed

DD13 low-pressure warning persisted after parts replacement

In a November 29, 2023 r/DieselTechs thread, the original poster reported continued low-pressure codes and a shutdown warning after replacing the pressure sensor and oil pump. Commenter HourOk2135 described a separate similar case in which a cracked side piece on the pickup manifold detached during inspection after the pump and sensor had been replaced.

Other replies suggested pickup-tube seals and manifold checks. Those suggestions are not confirmed findings for the original truck. The visible thread does not provide a verified final repair or before-and-after pressure readings.

Manifold damage and seal concerns are different findings

The manufacturer slide documents structural damage. The discussion also raises seals as possible causes, but does not prove they caused the original poster’s problem. Treat a damaged manifold, a leaking seal and a sensor concern as separate possibilities requiring vehicle-specific evaluation.

Information to bring to your technician

  • Engine serial number, truck year, engine model and oil-pan configuration.
  • Exact warning text and full fault codes, with when the warning occurs.
  • Recent oil service or parts replacements and the actual test results.
  • For a 2014 engine, campaign completion history checked with a Detroit service facility.

Ask the shop to identify the confirmed cause and document the result after repair. Match parts and procedures to the engine serial number; this overview is not a parts-ordering or disassembly guide.
