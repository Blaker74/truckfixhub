🧰 Six Air-Brake Drawers

1 · COMPRESSOR MAKES AIRIf pressure builds slowly, prove whether the problem is supply, leakage, control or restriction before condemning the compressor.
2 · DRYER MANAGES MOISTUREThe air dryer removes moisture and works with the governor/control system. Purge behavior is evidence, not a diagnosis by itself.
3 · TANKS STORE AIRReservoirs provide stored energy. Drains, check valves, protection valves and leaks affect what each circuit can hold.
4 · VALVES CONTROL AIRFoot, relay, quick-release, protection and other valves route or exhaust air. Know what the valve is being asked to do.
5 · CHAMBERS MAKE MOVEMENTBrake chambers turn pressure into pushrod force. Stroke, adjustment and mechanical condition matter.
6 · FOUNDATION BRAKES MAKE FRICTIONDrums/discs, shoes/pads, adjusters, cams, calipers and hardware create braking force at the wheel.

🔍 Let The Symptom Pick The Drawer

🐌 BUILDS AIR SLOWLYCheck leakage first, then compressor supply/control, dryer behavior and restrictions. Measure instead of guessing.
💨 LOSES AIR PARKEDListen and isolate safely. Determine which circuit and component is actually leaking.
🔒 BRAKE WON'T RELEASESeparate trapped air/control trouble from chamber, adjuster, cam, caliper or foundation-brake binding.
🔥 BRAKE DRAGS / WHEEL HOTDo not assume bearing or brake immediately. Identify where the heat is being generated.
↔️ PULLS WHEN BRAKINGCompare left/right application, friction condition, adjustment, contamination and mechanical movement.
⚠️ ABS LIGHT ONABS electrical diagnosis and foundation-brake diagnosis overlap but are not the same. Start with the actual fault.

🔧 The Diagnostic Drawer Never Changes

⚠️ BRAKE WORK IS SAFETY-CRITICAL

Stored air, spring brakes, raised vehicles and moving brake components can cause severe injury. Chock and support the vehicle correctly and follow current manufacturer and regulatory procedures. Never enter a danger zone around a brake chamber or release spring-brake force without the proper procedure. Exact pressure limits, stroke limits, adjustment methods and inspection criteria depend on the system and application.
