Air & Brake Knowledge Center

Air brakes can be understood as a chain. Make air, store air, control air, move the brake, create friction.

Simple air-brake rule:
MAKE AIR → DRY AIR → STORE AIR → CONTROL AIR → APPLY BRAKE → STOP TRUCK

Six parts of the story

1 · Compressor makes airThe compressor supplies the system. If pressure builds slowly, prove whether the problem is supply, leakage, control or restriction before condemning it.
2 · Dryer manages moistureThe air dryer removes moisture and works with the governor/control system. Purge behavior is evidence, not a diagnosis by itself.
3 · Tanks store airReservoirs give the system stored energy. Drains, check valves, protection valves and leaks affect what each circuit can hold.
4 · Valves control where air goesFoot, relay, quick-release, protection and other valves route or exhaust air. Identify what a valve is being asked to do before replacing it.
5 · Chambers turn air into movementBrake chambers convert pressure into pushrod force. Stroke, adjustment and mechanical condition matter.
6 · Foundation brakes create frictionDrums/discs, shoes/pads, adjusters, cams, calipers and hardware finally create the braking force at the wheel.

What is the truck doing?

Builds air slowlyCheck leakage first, then compressor supply/control, dryer behavior and restrictions. Measure instead of guessing.
Loses air parkedListen and isolate safely. Determine which circuit and component is actually leaking.
Brake won't releaseSeparate trapped air/control problems from chamber, adjuster, cam, caliper or foundation-brake binding.
Brake drags / wheel gets hotDo not assume wheel bearing or brake immediately. Identify where the heat is being generated.
Pulls when brakingCompare left/right brake application, friction condition, adjustment, contamination and mechanical movement.
ABS light is onABS electrical diagnosis and foundation-brake diagnosis overlap but are not the same thing. Start with the actual fault information.

Keep diagnosis simple

SEE ITConfirm the complaint safely: pressure behavior, leak, warning, brake response, heat or wheel-end symptom.
NAME ITRoute it toward air supply, storage, control, chamber/application, foundation brake or ABS/electrical.
TEST ITMeasure pressure, leakage, build behavior, application/release response, stroke or electrical evidence using approved procedures.
PROVE ITRepair the supported cause and perform the required operational and safety verification before returning the truck to service.

⚠️ Brake work is safety-critical

Stored air, spring brakes, raised vehicles and moving brake components can cause severe injury. Chock and support the vehicle correctly and follow current manufacturer and regulatory procedures. Never enter a danger zone around a brake chamber or release spring-brake force without the proper procedure. Exact pressure limits, stroke limits, adjustment methods and inspection criteria depend on the system and application.
