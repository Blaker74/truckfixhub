WHY TRUCKFIXHUB EXISTS

Help people get from a truck problem to the right next step.

TruckFixHub is being built around a simple idea: when a truck has a problem, the person dealing with it should not have to fight through a maze of unrelated information.

Built from heavy-duty experience

TruckFixHub was founded by Blake Pierce, whose background includes more than 30 years working around heavy-duty truck parts and logistics. The goal is not to replace technicians, OEM service information or professional repair facilities. It is to make useful heavy-duty information easier to find, organize and understand.

TruckFixHub is an independent information resource and is not an official website of Detroit, Cummins, Freightliner or other manufacturers referenced in educational content.

Start with what the truck is doing

Our troubleshooting library is organized around real symptoms and vehicle systems. A low-air warning belongs with air-system problems. A hot wheel belongs with brake and wheel-end problems. A failed regen belongs with aftertreatment. The structure is designed to help users narrow the problem before guessing at a part.

Our content standards

Safety firstWe do not recommend bypassing safety systems or operating a vehicle that is unsafe.
Integrity alwaysWe separate symptoms, possible causes and confirmed repair outcomes instead of presenting guesses as facts.
Optimize relentlesslyUseful information should be fast to find, easy to understand and practical on a phone beside a truck.
No blind parts swappingA symptom can have multiple causes. Guides narrow a diagnostic path rather than declare a component guilty without testing.
Useful innovationTechnology belongs here when it solves a real trucking problem, reduces friction or improves access to useful information.
Sustainable growthTrust and usefulness come before traffic or revenue. Growth should be earned by helping people.

How technology is used

TruckFixHub uses modern software and AI-assisted workflows to help research, organize, draft, structure and improve its growing information library. Technology is a tool for scaling useful work; it is not a substitute for sound diagnostic judgment, manufacturer procedures, qualified service or safety requirements.

Advertising and sustainability

TruckFixHub may use advertising and paid AI features to help cover the cost of operating and improving the service. Advertising does not determine our troubleshooting conclusions, and paid access does not turn educational information into a guaranteed diagnosis.

Our goal is to keep a substantial public repair library freely available while building sustainable tools that can continue helping drivers, technicians, parts professionals and fleets.

What TruckFixHub is not

TruckFixHub is an informational and organizational resource. It does not replace OEM service information, professional diagnosis, inspection requirements or qualified repair. Truck systems vary by manufacturer, model, configuration and application.
