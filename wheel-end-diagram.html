🛞 WHEEL ENDS

Think from the axle outward.

1️⃣ SPINDLE / AXLE
The foundation
↓
2️⃣ INNER BEARING + SEAL
Supports the hub and keeps lubricant in
↓
3️⃣ HUB + LUBRICANT
Rotates around the spindle
↓
4️⃣ OUTER BEARING + ADJUSTMENT
Supports the hub and controls end play
↓
5️⃣ BRAKE / WHEEL / TIRE
Where wheel-end problems often become visible
💧 Oil on wheel or brake?
Inspect seal area, lubricant level, venting, spindle surface and bearing condition.
🔥 Hub running hot?
Check bearing adjustment, lubrication, brake drag and damaged bearings.
↔️ Wheel has movement?
Check bearing adjustment and component wear before driving.
🔊 Growl or rumble?
Inspect bearings, races, lubricant and tire condition.
⚠️ A loose, overheated, leaking or damaged wheel end can become a serious road hazard. Secure the vehicle before inspection.
