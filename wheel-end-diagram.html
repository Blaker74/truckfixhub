👇 Follow the warning

💧 LEAK: Trace where lubricant begins. Check seal surfaces, hub movement, lubricant/venting and installation. A new seal does not repair a damaged surface or loose hub.
🔥 HOT HUB: Stop safely if severe. Separate brake heat from bearing/hub heat, compare similar positions, then inspect lubrication and bearing adjustment/condition.
↔️ MOVEMENT: Properly support the vehicle, locate the actual moving joint and measure wheel-bearing end play/adjustment. On steer axles, separate bearing movement from kingpin movement.
🔊 GROWL / RUMBLE: Record road speed, turning, braking and load. Tires, brakes, bearings, differential and driveline can transmit similar noise.
⚠️ FAILED BEARING: Preserve evidence. Inspect lubrication, adjustment/retention, damage pattern, spindle and hub before replacing parts so the root cause is not rebuilt into the wheel end.

🧠 Wheel-end firewall

1️⃣ Where is the heat, leak, noise or movement actually originating?
2️⃣ Is the brake contributing? Brake drag can mimic or create wheel-end heat.
3️⃣ Is lubrication correct and contained? Verify level/condition and leakage.
4️⃣ Is the hub mechanically controlled? Measure adjustment/end play and inspect retention.
5️⃣ Are spindle, hub, bearing and seal surfaces reusable? Compare to manufacturer requirements.

🔥 HOT HUB / WHEEL END

First answer one question: is the heat coming from the brake or the bearing/hub?

1️⃣ STOP SAFELY: Severe heat, smoke, odor, looseness or lubricant loss can indicate an unsafe wheel end. Do not continue driving to “see if it clears.”
2️⃣ COMPARE: Compare similar wheel positions under similar service conditions. Temperature alone needs context.
3️⃣ BRAKE HEAT: Inspect for brake drag, incomplete release, adjustment/mechanical issues and related air-brake causes before blaming the bearing.
4️⃣ LUBRICATION: Check wheel-end lubricant level, condition and evidence of leakage or contamination using the applicable hub design procedure.
5️⃣ BEARING / ADJUSTMENT: Measure movement/end play and inspect bearing/retention condition according to manufacturer specifications.
6️⃣ VERIFY: After repair, confirm normal release, lubrication and temperature behavior under comparable operation.
STOP → COMPARE → BRAKE OR BEARING? → LUBRICATION → MEASURE / INSPECT → REPAIR → VERIFY.
⚠️ An overheated wheel end can fail catastrophically or ignite. Treat severe heat as a road-safety issue.

⚠️ WHEEL BEARING FAILURE

Replacing damaged bearings without finding why they failed risks repeating the failure.

1️⃣ PRESERVE EVIDENCE: Note heat, noise, lubricant loss, looseness and service history before cleaning/disassembly erases clues.
2️⃣ LUBRICATION: Determine whether lubricant quantity, type, contamination or leakage contributed. Look for evidence rather than assuming.
3️⃣ ADJUSTMENT / RETENTION: Inspect the applicable spindle nuts, locking system and signs of incorrect end play, preload or component movement.
4️⃣ BEARING / RACE PATTERN: Inspect damage patterns according to bearing/wheel-end manufacturer guidance. Heat discoloration, scoring, spalling and other damage can point toward different causes.
5️⃣ SPINDLE + HUB: A bearing failure can damage mounting, race or seal surfaces. Inspect all reusable parts against manufacturer limits before assembly.
6️⃣ ROOT CAUSE + VERIFY: Correct the cause, assemble/lubricate/adjust using the exact procedure, then recheck temperature, leakage and end play as required.
PRESERVE EVIDENCE → LUBRICATION → ADJUSTMENT / RETENTION → DAMAGE PATTERN → SPINDLE / HUB → ROOT CAUSE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Suspected bearing failure can lead to wheel-end separation or fire. The vehicle should not be operated until the wheel end is proven safe.

↔️ LOOSE WHEEL BEARING / HUB MOVEMENT

Movement at the wheel is a safety warning. Locate and measure it instead of diagnosing by feel alone.

1️⃣ SECURE + SUPPORT: Use the vehicle/axle manufacturer’s approved lifting and support points before checking movement.
2️⃣ LOCATE THE MOVEMENT: Watch the hub relative to the spindle/axle. On steer axles, separate wheel-bearing movement from kingpin/knuckle movement.
3️⃣ MEASURE: Measure bearing end play or adjustment by the exact wheel-end manufacturer procedure. Do not use a generic universal limit.
4️⃣ INSPECT RETENTION: If adjustment is wrong, determine why. Inspect the applicable spindle threads, nuts, locking/retention system and evidence of movement.
5️⃣ BEARING / SPINDLE / HUB: If service is required, inspect bearing/race condition and the relevant spindle/hub surfaces before simply readjusting.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Complete the specified adjustment/retention procedure and remeasure before return to service.
SUPPORT → LOCATE MOVEMENT → MEASURE → RETENTION → BEARING / SPINDLE / HUB → ADJUST / REPAIR → REMEASURE.
⚠️ Do not operate a truck with unresolved wheel-end looseness. Wheel bearing adjustment and retention are safety-critical.

🔊 WHEEL-END GROWL / RUMBLE

Noise can travel through the chassis. Do not condemn a bearing from sound alone.

1️⃣ DEFINE THE NOISE: Record road speed, acceleration/coast, braking, turning and loaded/unloaded conditions.
2️⃣ TIRES / WHEELS: Irregular tire wear can create a bearing-like roar. Inspect tread pattern, wheel condition and obvious damage first.
3️⃣ BRAKES: Check for drag, contact or heat that changes with brake application.
4️⃣ WHEEL END: Inspect lubricant condition/level as applicable, hub temperature, movement/end play and bearing condition by the manufacturer procedure.
5️⃣ AXLE / DRIVELINE: If the noise is not isolated to the hub, continue into differential and driveline diagnosis rather than forcing the wheel-bearing conclusion.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Recreate the same speed/load condition after the proven repair.
NOISE CONDITION → TIRE / WHEEL → BRAKE → WHEEL END → AXLE / DRIVELINE → PROVE SOURCE → VERIFY.
⚠️ A noisy wheel end accompanied by heat, looseness or lubricant loss requires immediate safety inspection.

💧 WHEEL SEAL KEEPS LEAKING

A repeat leak means the job is not just “replace the seal again.” Find what is defeating the seal.

1️⃣ CONFIRM THE SOURCE: Clean and trace where lubricant begins. Separate hub/seal leakage from other nearby fluids or old residue.
2️⃣ SEALING SURFACES: Inspect the applicable spindle/hub seal surfaces for wear, grooves, corrosion, damage or incorrect repair surfaces.
3️⃣ HUB MOVEMENT: Incorrect bearing adjustment or damaged bearings can allow movement that affects sealing. Measure by the exact wheel-end procedure.
4️⃣ LUBRICANT / VENTING: Verify correct lubricant, level and hub/axle venting where applicable. Overfill, contamination or pressure problems can contribute.
5️⃣ INSTALLATION: Confirm the correct seal/application, installation depth/orientation and approved tools/procedure. Inspect the hub/spindle for damage caused during prior service.
6️⃣ BRAKES + VERIFY: If lubricant reached friction components, service them per manufacturer requirements. Recheck leakage after operation.
CONFIRM SOURCE → SEAL SURFACE → HUB MOVEMENT → LUBE / VENT → INSTALLATION → BRAKES → VERIFY.
⚠️ Lubricant-contaminated brakes and low wheel-end lubricant are serious safety concerns. Follow manufacturer replacement/service requirements.
