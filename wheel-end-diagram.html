👇 PICK THE COMPLAINT

💧 Oil or grease is showing around the wheel

  1. Secure the vehicle and identify exactly where the lubricant begins.
  2. Check lubricant level and condition using the correct wheel-end procedure.
  3. Inspect the hub / axle venting arrangement where applicable.
  4. Inspect seal area, spindle / seal surfaces and bearing condition rather than replacing the seal alone.
  5. If lubricant reached the brake components, inspect and service the brake system according to manufacturer requirements before returning the vehicle to service.

🔥 One hub is much hotter than the others

  1. Stop safely if heat is severe, increasing rapidly, or accompanied by smoke, odor or looseness.
  2. Compare the affected wheel end with similar wheel ends under comparable operating conditions.
  3. Check for brake drag first because brake heat can look like a bearing problem.
  4. Inspect lubricant level / condition and wheel-bearing adjustment or end play using the manufacturer procedure.
  5. Inspect bearings, races and related components if lubrication, adjustment or condition indicates a wheel-end problem.

↔️ The wheel / hub has noticeable movement

  1. Do not keep operating the vehicle until the looseness is understood.
  2. Properly support the vehicle and verify where the movement occurs.
  3. Measure bearing end play / adjustment using the approved procedure rather than judging by feel alone.
  4. Inspect bearings, races, spindle, hub and retention hardware as required.
  5. After repair or adjustment, verify the complete wheel-end assembly meets manufacturer specifications.

📚 Read the whole wheel end—not one symptom

A wheel end is a system. Bearings support the rotating hub, lubricant protects those bearings, seals retain lubricant, adjustment controls bearing relationship, and the brake assembly adds another major heat source. A useful diagnosis separates these functions before naming a failed part.

🔥 Heat: compare before condemning

A hot hub does not automatically mean a failed bearing. Brake drag can transfer substantial heat into the hub and wheel. Compare similar wheel positions after comparable operation and look for brake evidence, lubricant condition, abnormal movement, odor and noise. Severe heat, smoke or looseness calls for immediate attention.

💧 A new seal may not fix the reason it leaked

A seal can leak because the sealing surface is damaged, bearing condition or adjustment allows unwanted movement, lubricant condition or level is wrong, or venting is not functioning as intended where applicable. Find the cause around the seal so the repair does not become a repeat repair.

↔️ Bearing adjustment should be measured

Wheel-bearing adjustment is safety-critical and procedures differ by hub, bearing and retention system. Feeling for looseness can identify a concern, but final adjustment or end-play verification should follow the applicable manufacturer procedure and specifications rather than a universal guess.

🔊 Noise can travel

A growl or rumble heard near one wheel may come from a bearing, tire, brake, differential or another rotating component. Note whether it changes with road speed, braking, turning or load. Use those changes to narrow the source, then inspect and measure before replacing components.

🛢️ Lubricant tells a story

Low lubricant can damage bearings, but simply adding lubricant does not explain why it became low. Look for leakage and the correct lubrication method for that wheel end. Discoloration, contamination or metallic debris can be evidence that deeper inspection is needed.

🛑 Brake contamination changes the repair

If wheel-end lubricant reaches friction components, stopping the leak is only part of the job. The affected brake assembly must be inspected and serviced according to the applicable manufacturer requirements before the vehicle returns to service.

TruckFixHub diagnostic chain: secure vehicle → locate heat/leak/movement/noise → compare sides → inspect brake and lubrication evidence → measure bearing condition/adjustment as required → repair proven cause → verify wheel end before service.
