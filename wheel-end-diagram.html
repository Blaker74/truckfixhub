🛞 WHEEL ENDS

Think from the axle outward.

1️⃣ SPINDLE / AXLE
The foundation
↓
2️⃣ INNER BEARING + SEAL
Supports the hub and helps keep lubricant where it belongs
↓
3️⃣ HUB + LUBRICANT
The rotating hub depends on correct lubrication
↓
4️⃣ OUTER BEARING + ADJUSTMENT
Supports the hub; correct bearing adjustment is critical
↓
5️⃣ BRAKE / WHEEL / TIRE
Where heat, leakage, looseness and wear may become visible
💧 Oil on wheel or brake?
Inspect the seal area and follow the approved procedure for lubricant level, venting, spindle/seal surfaces and bearing condition.
🔥 Hub running hot?
Compare temperatures carefully and investigate lubrication, bearing condition/adjustment and brake drag.
↔️ Wheel has movement?
Stop and inspect bearing adjustment and wheel-end components using the manufacturer’s end-play/adjustment procedure.
🔊 Growl or rumble?
Inspect bearings and races, lubricant condition, tires and nearby rotating components before condemning the hub.

Leaks, heat, looseness and noise are clues

A wheel-end complaint can come from more than one component. A leaking seal may be related to seal surfaces, lubrication, venting or bearing condition. Heat can come from bearings or brake drag. Noise can come from bearings, tires or other rotating parts. Inspect the complete wheel end and use the correct manufacturer bearing-adjustment procedure instead of guessing from one symptom.
