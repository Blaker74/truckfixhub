👇 Follow the warning

💧 HUB / WHEEL LEAKING: Locate where lubricant actually begins. Check level/condition, venting where applicable, seal surfaces, spindle/hub condition and bearing condition/adjustment. Replacing the seal alone may not correct the reason it leaked.
🔥 ONE WHEEL IS HOT: Stop safely if severe. Separate brake heat from bearing heat first. Compare similar wheel positions, inspect brake drag, lubricant and bearing adjustment/condition using the applicable procedure.
↔️ HUB / WHEEL HAS MOVEMENT: Treat unexpected looseness as a safety concern. Properly support the vehicle, locate the movement and measure bearing end play/adjustment rather than relying only on feel.
🔊 GROWL / RUMBLE: Note changes with road speed, braking, turning and load. A tire, brake, bearing, differential or other rotating component can transmit noise through the chassis.
〰️ VIBRATION: If the complaint follows road speed, include the wheel end in the inspection—but also check wheels/tires and driveline. Do not condemn a bearing from vibration alone.

🧠 Before replacing a hub seal

1️⃣ Why did lubricant escape? Trace the true source and check level/condition.
2️⃣ Is the sealing surface healthy? Inspect the applicable spindle/hub sealing surfaces.
3️⃣ Is the hub moving correctly? Bearing wear or incorrect adjustment can contribute to leakage.
4️⃣ Did lubricant reach the brakes? If so, the brake assembly also requires inspection/service according to the applicable manufacturer requirements.
