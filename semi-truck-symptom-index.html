Semi Truck Symptom Index

Drivers and technicians rarely begin with a component name. They begin with a complaint: “won’t crank,” “no power,” “losing air,” “running hot,” “shaking,” or “won’t regen.” Pick the symptom that best matches the truck and follow the diagnostic path.

Start here: A symptom is evidence—not a parts order. Confirm the exact application, reproduce the complaint when safe, measure the system, and use current manufacturer service information for final specifications and repair procedures.

01 / Won’t start, crank or charge

First determine whether the engine does not crank, cranks slowly, or cranks normally but does not start.

05 / Air pressure & brake complaints
