🛞 WHEELS & TIRES

Start where the truck touches the road, then work inward.

1️⃣ TREAD
Wear pattern can point toward pressure, alignment, suspension or operating-condition problems
↓
2️⃣ TIRE PRESSURE / CONDITION
Correct inflation, damage, load capacity, size and matching all matter
↓
3️⃣ WHEEL / RIM
Inspect condition, seating, cracks, damage and runout concerns by the proper procedure
↓
4️⃣ FASTENERS
Studs, nuts and mounting surfaces must be serviceable and secured to specification
↓
5️⃣ HUB / WHEEL END / SUSPENSION
Problems farther inward can appear at the tire as wear, heat, looseness or vibration
〰️ Vibration?
Record the road speed and whether it changes with acceleration or braking. Inspect tires and wheels first, then wheel ends, suspension and driveline as the symptom indicates.
📉 Uneven wear?
Use the wear pattern as a clue. Check inflation, alignment, ride height and worn steering/suspension or wheel-end components before assuming alignment alone is the fix.
💨 Keeps losing air?
Locate the actual leak at the tread/casing, valve, bead or wheel. Do not repeatedly reinflate a damaged or questionable assembly without proper inspection.
🔥 One tire / wheel hot?
Stop safely and investigate. Low inflation, overload, brake drag, bearing/wheel-end trouble or other mechanical problems can create dangerous heat.

The tire often shows the symptom, not the root cause

Tires react to inflation, load, alignment, steering and suspension movement, wheel-end condition and operating environment. Replacing a worn tire without correcting the cause can simply wear out the replacement. Inspect the complete path and use manufacturer specifications for pressure, wheel installation, fastener torque and alignment.

🧠 Simple rule: the wear pattern is a CLUE. Find what caused it before replacing the tire and sending the truck back out.
⚠️ Commercial truck tire and wheel assemblies can store tremendous energy. Never weld, heat or improperly service a pressurized assembly. Damaged or questionable assemblies require trained personnel and the correct procedures/equipment.
