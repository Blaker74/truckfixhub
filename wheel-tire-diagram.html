🛞 WHEELS & TIRES

Start at the road and work inward.

1️⃣ TREAD
Wear pattern tells a story
↓
2️⃣ TIRE PRESSURE / CONDITION
Pressure, damage and matching matter
↓
3️⃣ WHEEL / RIM
Check condition, seating and damage
↓
4️⃣ FASTENERS
Wheel nuts/studs must be serviceable and correctly secured
↓
5️⃣ HUB / WHEEL END / SUSPENSION
Problems farther inward can show up as tire wear or vibration
〰️ Vibration?
Note the road speed. Inspect tire condition, balance/runout concerns, wheel condition and then wheel end/driveline.
📉 Uneven wear?
Check pressure, alignment, suspension/steering looseness, wheel ends and operating conditions.
💨 Keeps losing air?
Inspect tread, valve, bead and wheel for the actual leak source.
🔥 One tire/wheel hot?
Stop and inspect for low pressure, brake drag or wheel-end trouble before continuing.
🧠 Simple rule: the wear pattern is a CLUE. Find what caused it before replacing the tire and sending the truck back out.
⚠️ Commercial truck tires and wheel assemblies can store tremendous energy. Damaged or questionable assemblies should be handled by trained personnel using proper procedures and equipment.
