CAT Sensors & Electronics

Truck-electronics identification reference organized by engine family and serial-prefix/application.

C11 · C13 · C15 ACERT

Prefixes: C11 KCA · C13 KCB · C15 BXS/MXS.

Reference includes ECM, cam/crank speed-timing sensors, coolant/fuel/inlet-air temperature, boost, atmospheric pressure, oil pressure, VVA oil pressure, coolant diverter valve, throttle-position sensors and smart-wastegate solenoid.

C7 · C9 ACERT

Prefixes: C7 KAL · C9 9DG.

Reference includes ECM 239-8277, speed/timing sensor 245-4630, temperature, boost, atmospheric and oil-pressure sensors, throttle-position sensor and smart-wastegate solenoid.

3126E · 3116/3126 HEUI

Reference includes 3126E ECM 192-7896, temperature, speed/timing, atmospheric, boost, injection-actuation pressure and pedal-position sensors, plus injector/sensor harness service parts. HEUI sections include ECM, coolant/inlet-air, boost and injection-actuation components.

3176 · 3176B · 3406E

Coverage includes 3176 prefixes 7LG/2YG, 3176B 9CK and multiple 3406E applications, with ECM, speed/timing, coolant/fuel temperature, air-inlet temperature, boost, atmospheric, oil-pressure, throttle-position and vehicle-speed components.

C10 · C12 · C15 · C16

Production/application sections distinguish serial prefixes and production periods. The source lists ECM and cam/crank speed-timing sensor changes along with temperature, boost, atmospheric, oil-pressure and throttle-position components.

PEEC Electronics

PEEC II/III references include ECMs, transducer modules, speed/rack/timing sensors, BTM, throttle-position sensors, coolant temperature, oil-pressure kits and vehicle-speed buffer information.

Common CAT sensor families in the source

Speed/timing · cam · crank · coolant temperature · fuel temperature · inlet-air/manifold temperature · boost pressure · atmospheric pressure · oil pressure · VVA oil pressure · injection-actuation pressure · throttle/pedal position · vehicle speed.

CAT verification rule

The source itself states: “Always consider SIS the best source for current information.” TruckFixHub therefore treats this document as an identification/reference aid. Verify the engine serial number, prefix, production range and current Caterpillar SIS information before ordering parts, diagnosing wiring or performing service.
