Cooling System

Semi-Truck Low Coolant Warning Keeps Coming On

First determine whether coolant is truly low.

If coolant is low

Inspect cap, tank, hoses, radiator, water pump, heater lines and pressure-test. If no external leak appears, investigate EGR cooler, oil cooler, coolant expulsion, compressor and internal engine paths.

If level is correct

Inspect the level sensor, connector, terminals, harness and module input. A warning during turns or grades can occur when a marginally low level moves away from the sensor.

Do not repeatedly top off coolant without finding why it is disappearing.
