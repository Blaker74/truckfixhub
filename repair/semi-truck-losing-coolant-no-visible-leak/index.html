Cooling System

Semi Truck Losing Coolant With No Visible Leak

If coolant keeps disappearing but there is no puddle, determine whether the loss occurs only hot or pressurized, enters another system, or leaves through the exhaust.

Diagnostic order

  1. Check surge tank, cap, hoses, clamps, radiator, water pump and heater lines cold.
  2. Pressure-test the cooling system to the OEM specification.
  3. Look for dried coolant residue.
  4. Inspect engine oil and coolant for cross-contamination.
  5. Check for abnormal cooling-system pressure.
  6. Test the EGR cooler where applicable.
  7. Evaluate the coolant-cooled air compressor and oil cooler.
  8. Investigate head gasket, cylinder head and liners only when testing supports it.

Important clues

White exhaust can point toward coolant entering the EGR/exhaust or combustion path. Rapid pressure from a cold start can justify testing for combustion leakage or another pressure source. Coolant in oil requires immediate attention.

When to stop driving

Stop if coolant loss becomes severe, temperature rises abnormally, coolant contaminates oil, or the reservoir repeatedly pushes coolant out.
