Cooling System

Semi Truck Cooling System Building Pressure

Some pressure is normal. Rapid pressure immediately after startup, repeated coolant expulsion, or excessive pressure requires diagnosis.

Diagnostic order

  1. Verify cold coolant level and cap condition.
  2. Pressure-test the system and inspect for leaks.
  3. Determine how quickly pressure develops after a cold start.
  4. Check for continuous bubbling or coolant displacement.
  5. Test the EGR cooler where applicable.
  6. Evaluate the coolant-cooled air compressor.
  7. Test for combustion gases.
  8. Investigate head gasket, cylinder head and liners only when evidence supports internal failure.

Cold-start clue

Significant pressure before coolant temperature rises can indicate pressure entering the cooling circuit from another source. Temperature expansion alone develops as coolant warms.
