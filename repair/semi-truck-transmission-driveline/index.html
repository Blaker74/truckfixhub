Diagnose before replacing

Identify whether the complaint changes with road speed, engine RPM, gear selection, clutch position, throttle load or braking. That distinction can separate engine-related vibration from transmission, driveshaft, wheel-end and tire problems.

For electronically controlled transmissions, record active fault codes and verify power, grounds, network communication and air supply where applicable before condemning a transmission control module or internal transmission component.

Safety boundary

Driveline, clutch and transmission work can involve rotating assemblies, stored energy and heavy components. Do not work beneath an unsupported vehicle or inspect a rotating driveline from an unsafe position. Use proper lifting/support equipment and OEM procedures.
