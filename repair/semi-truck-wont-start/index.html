Truck won't start?

Do not guess at batteries, starters or fuel parts. First identify exactly what happens when you try to start it.

What does the truck do?

✋

Nothing. No crank.

The engine does not turn over.
🔊

It clicks.

You hear a click, but the engine does not turn.
🔋

Cranks slow / weak.

Starting power sounds weak or batteries keep going dead.
🔄

Cranks, won't fire.

The engine turns normally but will not start.

TruckFixHub rule

Separate the symptom before replacing the part. A no-crank problem and a crank-no-start problem are two different diagnostic paths. For no-crank complaints, prove battery condition and the high-current and control circuits. For crank-no-start complaints, verify ECM power/communication, cranking RPM, fuel supply and start-enable conditions.

Safety

Secure the vehicle before testing. Do not routinely bypass neutral, clutch or other start-safety interlocks. Keep clear of moving engine components and use the vehicle manufacturer's procedures and specifications.
