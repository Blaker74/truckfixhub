Semi-Truck Starter Voltage-Drop Testing

Voltage-drop testing checks a circuit while current flows and can expose resistance that continuity testing misses.

Positive side

Test battery positive through cables, disconnects, solenoid and starter while cranking.

Ground side

Test starter/engine through ground straps and battery negative while cranking.

If excessive drop appears, divide the circuit: post-to-terminal, terminal-to-cable, cable sections, disconnect, starter connection and ground straps. Use OEM limits rather than one universal number.

Battery voltage under load asks whether the battery can maintain voltage. Voltage drop asks how much voltage is lost getting to and from the starter.
