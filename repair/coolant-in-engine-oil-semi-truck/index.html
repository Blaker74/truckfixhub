Cooling System

Coolant in Semi-Truck Engine Oil

Confirmed coolant contamination can reduce lubrication and damage bearings and other internal components. Avoid unnecessary operation until the source is found.

Confirm and isolate

  1. Check oil and coolant levels and appearance.
  2. Use oil analysis where appropriate.
  3. Pressure-test the cooling system.
  4. Test the oil cooler and sealing interfaces.
  5. Investigate head gasket and cylinder head.
  6. On wet-liner engines, evaluate liner seals and related sealing areas.

Light residue around an oil-fill cap can sometimes be condensation; do not diagnose major engine failure from appearance alone.

After repair

Drain contaminated oil, replace filters, follow OEM flushing/service instructions, refill with specified lubricant, verify pressure integrity and monitor both fluids.
