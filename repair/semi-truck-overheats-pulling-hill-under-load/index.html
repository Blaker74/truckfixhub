Cooling System

Semi Truck Overheats Pulling Hills or Under Load

Heavy load increases heat production. A marginal cooling system may handle cruising but lose control on grades.

Check

  1. Verify temperature, coolant level and fault codes.
  2. Verify full fan engagement under high-temperature conditions.
  3. Inspect the complete cooling stack and debris between CAC/radiator.
  4. Evaluate radiator heat-transfer capacity.
  5. Check thermostat, water pump and coolant circulation.
  6. Inspect charge-air boots, clamps, piping and CAC.
  7. Investigate abnormal cooling-system pressure.

Record ambient temperature, gross weight, grade, RPM, coolant temperature, engine load, fan status and vehicle speed. This pattern data can narrow diagnosis quickly.
