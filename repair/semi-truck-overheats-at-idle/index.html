Cooling System

Semi Truck Overheats at Idle but Cools While Driving

If temperature rises sitting still but falls once moving, vehicle speed is changing airflow through the cooling system. Start with airflow and fan operation.

  1. Verify actual temperature and coolant level.
  2. Check fan engagement and ECM command.
  3. Check fan clutch air/electrical controls.
  4. Inspect condenser, CAC, radiator and spaces between them for debris.
  5. Evaluate radiator condition.
  6. If airflow passes, check thermostat, water pump and circulation.
  7. If coolant is expelled, investigate abnormal pressure separately.
