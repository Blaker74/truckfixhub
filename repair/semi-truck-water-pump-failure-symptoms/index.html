Semi-Truck Water Pump Failure Symptoms

Water-pump failure can cause coolant loss, inadequate circulation and overheating, but overheating alone does not prove pump failure.

  1. Check cold coolant level and pressure-test.
  2. Inspect pump for wet or dried coolant residue.
  3. With engine safely off, inspect pulley/drive and bearing play.
  4. Verify fan operation and radiator airflow.
  5. Check thermostat operation.
  6. Evaluate coolant circulation and pump condition per OEM procedure.

Inspect belt, tensioner and pulley on belt-driven designs. Gear-driven systems require engine-specific procedures.
