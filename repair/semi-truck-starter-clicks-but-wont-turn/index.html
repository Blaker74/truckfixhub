Semi-Truck Starter Clicks but Won't Turn

A click means something in the start circuit responds; it does not prove the starter motor failed.

One heavy click

Check battery charge/capacity, positive cable voltage drop, ground-side voltage drop, starter connections, solenoid, starter and engine mechanical condition.

Rapid clicking

Start with low battery voltage, weak batteries, loose/corroded terminals, damaged cables and poor grounds.

Measure voltage at the starter during the actual crank attempt. A static no-load reading can miss high-resistance faults.
