Semi-Truck Thermostat Failure Symptoms

A thermostat that fails closed can restrict radiator flow; one that remains open can cause slow warm-up and low operating temperature.

Possible symptoms

  • Overheating or temperature rise under load
  • Slow warm-up or low coolant temperature
  • Temperature fluctuation
  • Poor cab heat

Diagnosis

Verify actual temperature, coolant level, fan operation and radiator airflow. Evaluate coolant circulation, thermostat operation and water pump according to the engine manufacturer's procedure. Some engines use multiple thermostats or specialized arrangements.
