🛞 Tires, Wheels & Alignment

Check tire condition and pressure, wheel-end condition, ride height, suspension wear and alignment evidence together. An alignment adjustment cannot repair loose or damaged hardware.

Tire-wear patterns →Wheel bearing / hub problems →

🔩 Steering Inspection

Inspect the steering path for looseness, binding, damage and mounting movement. Verify the complaint under safe conditions before condemning a steering gear or linkage component.

🛠️ Suspension & Ride Height

Inspect springs or air suspension, torque rods, bushings, shocks, mounts and ride-height control before treating a handling complaint as an alignment-only problem.

Diagnostic order
  1. Confirm the driver's complaint and when it occurs: braking, acceleration, steady cruise, loaded, empty or at a particular speed.
  2. Inspect tires, wheels and wheel ends before adjusting alignment.
  3. Check steering and suspension hardware for looseness, wear, damage or movement.
  4. Verify ride height and obvious chassis or axle-position problems.
  5. Measure alignment only after mechanical condition is known.
  6. Road-test and verify the repair under safe conditions.

Safety-critical system

Steering, suspension, wheel-end and tire faults can affect vehicle control. Do not operate a truck with unsafe looseness, damaged steering or suspension components, wheel-end problems, or tires that cannot meet applicable safety requirements. Use OEM procedures and qualified heavy-duty service personnel where appropriate.
