Semi-Truck Clogged Radiator Symptoms

A radiator can lose capacity from external airflow restriction or internal coolant-flow/heat-transfer restriction.

External

Inspect condenser, CAC, radiator and spaces between components for bugs, dirt, grass, leaves, oil, mud and bent fins.

Internal

Deposits, contamination, corrosion, incompatible coolant or stop-leak products can reduce flow or heat transfer. Temperature patterns across the radiator can provide clues when interpreted with load, fan status and ambient conditions.

Verify fan operation, thermostat and water pump before condemning the radiator.
