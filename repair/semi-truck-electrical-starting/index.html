Quick Diagnostic Paths

Nothing Happens When the Key Is Turned

Start with battery condition, main connections and grounds, then verify the start command, safety/interlock circuits, relay, solenoid and starter.

Starter Clicks but Engine Does Not Turn

Check battery voltage under load and perform positive- and ground-side voltage-drop tests before condemning the starter.

Engine Cranks Normally but Will Not Start

Check fault codes, ECM communication, ECM power and grounds, engine RPM while cranking, fuel supply and crank/cam synchronization.

Batteries Keep Going Dead

Test each battery, verify charging performance and circuit voltage drop, then check key-off draw after modules enter normal sleep state.

Scan Tool Cannot Communicate With the ECM

Verify diagnostic equipment and connector power, determine whether other modules communicate, then test ECM powers, grounds and network integrity.

TruckFixHub Diagnostic Principles

  • Test before replacing parts.
  • Measure circuits while they are loaded whenever appropriate.
  • Check both the positive and ground sides.
  • Use live data to confirm what the ECM sees.
  • Use OEM specifications instead of assuming one universal voltage, resistance or sensor value fits every truck.
  • Inspect anything recently disturbed before assuming an unrelated failure.

Safety: Heavy-truck electrical systems can deliver very high current. Use properly rated test equipment, secure the vehicle and follow manufacturer service procedures.
