Semi Truck Cranks but Won't Start

If cranking speed is normal, shift diagnosis toward engine management, fuel delivery and position signals.

  1. Scan active/stored codes and verify ECM communication.
  2. Check ECM power and grounds during cranking.
  3. Watch engine RPM while cranking.
  4. Verify fuel level, filters, restrictions and low-pressure supply.
  5. Check required high-pressure fuel values where applicable.
  6. Verify crank/cam synchronization and injector command.

If the engine physically cranks but scan data shows no RPM, investigate crankshaft-position signal, wiring, connector and trigger system. After recent repairs, inspect disturbed connectors, grounds and fuel-system connections first.
