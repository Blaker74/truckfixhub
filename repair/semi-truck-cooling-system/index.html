TruckFixHub rule

Overheating is a symptom, not a diagnosis. Prove whether the problem is coolant loss, circulation, airflow/heat rejection, abnormal system pressure, sensor information or engine load before replacing parts.

Safety

Hot coolant and steam can cause severe burns. Never remove a pressurized cooling-system cap while hot. Keep clear of electronically controlled fans, which may engage unexpectedly. Follow OEM procedures and specifications.
