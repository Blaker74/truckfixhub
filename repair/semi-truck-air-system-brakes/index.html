Semi Truck Air System & Air Brake Troubleshooting

Air-system complaints overlap. A truck that builds air slowly may have a compressor problem, a control problem, or simply a leak consuming compressor output. Start with exactly what the truck is doing and keep the evidence connected before replacing parts.

Choose the symptom

💨 Air supply & pressure

Won't build air pressure →Air pressure builds slowly →Air compressor not building pressure →Truck losing air pressure →Air dryer purging or leaking →Low-air warning / buzzer →

🛑 Brake application

Air pressure drops when braking →Air leak when brakes are applied →Brake chamber / wheel-area leak →Brakes dragging / not releasing →Hot wheel / smoking brake →

🅿️ Parking & trailer brakes

Parking brake won't release →Trailer brakes won't release →Pressure drops while parked →Low air prevents normal release →

🔎 Diagnose before replacing

Record primary and secondary pressure behavior, when the leak or pressure loss occurs, whether the symptom changes with brake application or a trailer connected, and any recent air-system work. One observation rarely proves one component.

Safety boundary

Air brakes are safety-critical

Do not operate a truck that cannot build or maintain required air pressure, has abnormal braking performance, or has an unresolved air-brake defect. Never bypass low-air warnings, spring-brake protection or other safety devices. Spring brake chambers can contain dangerous stored mechanical energy. Use current manufacturer procedures, applicable inspection requirements and qualified heavy-duty service practices.
