🛑 SAFETY: Do not operate a truck that cannot build or maintain required air pressure, has abnormal braking, or has an unresolved air-brake defect. Never bypass low-air warnings or spring-brake protection. Spring brake chambers can contain dangerous stored mechanical energy. Exact inspection limits, pressure requirements and procedures must be verified for the exact vehicle.
