Safety: Do not operate a truck that cannot build or maintain required air pressure, has abnormal braking, or has an unresolved air-brake defect. Never bypass low-air warnings or spring-brake protection. Spring brake chambers can contain dangerous stored mechanical energy. Use current manufacturer procedures and qualified heavy-duty service practices.
