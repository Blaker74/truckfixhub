Semi-Truck Head Gasket Failure Symptoms

A failed head gasket can cause serious problems, but many commonly blamed symptoms overlap with other failures.

Possible symptoms

  • Rapid cooling-system pressure
  • Coolant pushed from the surge tank
  • Persistent bubbling or coolant loss
  • Overheating
  • Coolant contamination
  • Combustion gases in coolant
  • Symptoms worsening under load

Before teardown

Document pressure behavior, coolant consumption, operating temperature, contamination, combustion-leak results, and applicable EGR cooler, air compressor and oil cooler tests. Cylinder-head removal should follow evidence, not assumption.
