Cooling System

Semi-Truck Fan Clutch Not Engaging

A fan that fails to engage can cause overheating, especially at idle, low road speed or heavy load.

Diagnostic order

  1. Confirm actual temperature.
  2. Determine whether the ECM commands the fan on.
  3. Inspect fan/clutch safely with engine off.
  4. Check air supply on pneumatic systems.
  5. Test fan-control solenoid, power, ground, wiring and connectors.
  6. Verify temperature and A/C pressure inputs where applicable.
  7. Test the clutch per manufacturer procedure.

Commanded ON but not engaged points toward clutch/control delivery. Not commanded ON despite high temperature points toward inputs or control logic.
