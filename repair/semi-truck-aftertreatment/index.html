Diagnose before replacing

Record what warning appeared first, current and inactive faults, DEF refill history, regen attempts and completion history, duty cycle, recent repairs and DPF maintenance history.

A code naming a system or sensor does not automatically condemn that component. Wiring, connectors, fluid quality, temperature and pressure data, dosing operation and other manufacturer-specific conditions may need verification.

Safety & emissions boundary

Follow critical STOP/SHUTDOWN warnings and manufacturer instructions. TruckFixHub does not recommend defeating emissions equipment, bypassing engine-protection logic or using generic reset procedures as a substitute for diagnosis. Perform parked regeneration only under the applicable OEM procedure and required safety conditions.
