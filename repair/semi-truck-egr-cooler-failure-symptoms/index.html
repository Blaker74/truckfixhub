Semi-Truck EGR Cooler Failure Symptoms

An internally leaking EGR cooler can allow coolant into the EGR/exhaust system without leaving an obvious puddle.

Possible symptoms

  • Unexplained coolant loss
  • White exhaust vapor
  • Coolant residue in EGR/exhaust passages
  • Repeated low-coolant warnings
  • Overheating or cooling-system abnormalities

Diagnosis

Pressure-test the complete cooling system, inspect for external leaks, then isolate and test the cooler using the engine manufacturer's procedure. Do not replace the cooler or condemn the head gasket solely from symptoms.
