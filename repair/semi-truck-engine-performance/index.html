Before buying a part

Record when the complaint happens: idle, acceleration, highway cruise, hill pull, cold, hot or during a derate. Save active codes and recent repair history. Look for visible leaks, loose connections and abnormal smoke. Compare commanded information with what the engine actually does. Then test the system that the evidence points toward.

SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

🛑 SAFETY: Stop safely for a red stop-engine warning, severe knocking, rapid oil or coolant loss, uncontrolled engine speed, severe overheating or another manufacturer-directed shutdown condition. Verify exact specifications and procedures for the exact engine and vehicle.
