Cooling System

Semi Truck Overheating

Overheating requires systematic diagnosis. Do not replace thermostats, pumps or radiators without proving the failure.

Diagnostic order

  1. Verify actual temperature and cold coolant level.
  2. Inspect leaks, cap and reservoir.
  3. Verify fan engagement and ECM fan command.
  4. Inspect radiator/CAC airflow and debris between components.
  5. Evaluate thermostat operation and coolant circulation.
  6. Inspect water-pump condition.
  7. Investigate abnormal pressure, EGR cooler and internal engine problems when evidence supports them.

Pattern clues

Overheats at idle but cools moving: prioritize airflow and fan. Overheats pulling grades: evaluate maximum cooling capacity, airflow, radiator condition and coolant flow.
