Semi-Truck Batteries Keep Dying

Repeated dead batteries usually indicate a battery-bank, charging, connection or key-off draw problem.

  1. Fully charge the bank and test each battery.
  2. Inspect interconnects, main cables and grounds.
  3. Verify charging voltage/output under load.
  4. Perform charging-circuit voltage-drop tests.
  5. If batteries die parked, measure key-off draw after modules enter normal sleep state.
  6. Isolate abnormal circuits systematically.

Possible key-off loads include sleeper accessories, inverter, refrigerator, lighting, telematics, trailer circuits, stuck relays and modules that fail to sleep. New batteries will not fix an unresolved charging or draw problem.
