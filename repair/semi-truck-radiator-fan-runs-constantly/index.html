Cooling System

Semi Truck Engine Fan Runs Constantly

A continuously engaged fan can consume horsepower, increase noise and reduce fuel economy.

  1. Verify the fan is engaged.
  2. Check fault codes, coolant temperature and ECM fan-command status.
  3. Determine the reason for the fan request.
  4. Check A/C pressure and other sensor inputs.
  5. Test fan-solenoid electrical and pneumatic operation.
  6. Inspect wiring, connectors and air lines.
  7. Test the clutch after the control system is proven.

If ECM commands ON, find why. If ECM commands OFF but the fan stays engaged, investigate the solenoid, air logic, wiring, fail-safe strategy and clutch.
