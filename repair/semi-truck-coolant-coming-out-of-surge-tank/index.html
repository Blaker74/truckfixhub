Cooling System

Semi Truck Pushing Coolant Out of Surge Tank

Coolant discharge can result from overfilling, a faulty cap, overheating or abnormal system pressure. It does not automatically prove a blown head gasket.

Check first

  1. Allow the engine to cool and verify the correct cold level.
  2. Inspect the tank, cap and sealing surfaces.
  3. Check for external leaks and radiator airflow.
  4. Verify fan and thermostat operation if overheating is present.
  5. Determine whether pressure develops unusually fast from cold.
  6. Pressure-test the system.

If it keeps pushing coolant

Investigate EGR cooler, coolant-cooled air compressor, combustion leakage, head gasket, cylinder head and liner sealing according to the engine design.
