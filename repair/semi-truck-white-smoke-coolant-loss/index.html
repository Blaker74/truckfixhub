Semi Truck White Smoke and Losing Coolant

White exhaust plus verified coolant loss can indicate coolant entering the intake, combustion or exhaust path, but white exhaust can also be unburned fuel or cold-start behavior.

  1. Verify coolant loss and inspect external leaks.
  2. Pressure-test the system.
  3. Check oil for contamination and scan fault codes.
  4. Determine whether white exhaust occurs cold, hot or continuously.
  5. Test the EGR cooler where applicable.
  6. Check abnormal cooling-system pressure and combustion leakage.
  7. Investigate cylinder head, gasket and liners only when supported.

If coolant remains stable, investigate fuel/combustion causes rather than forcing a cooling-system diagnosis.
