💨 AIR & BRAKES HUB📖 TECHNICAL DETAILS

What the system is telling you

No pressure increase, slow build, pressure that builds and falls, and one circuit behaving differently are different clues. Do not treat them as the same failure.

Areas to test

  • Large or continuous air leaks
  • Air-compressor output and drive
  • Governor and compressor control
  • Air dryer and purge system
  • Damaged or restricted air lines
  • Reservoir plumbing
  • Brake valves, chambers and other air-operated devices

SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

The symptom does not prove which component failed. Exact pressure requirements and procedures must be verified for the exact truck.
