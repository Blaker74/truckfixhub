Full troubleshooting explanation

When a truck will not build air pressure, first separate what the system is actually doing. No pressure increase, slow build, pressure that builds and falls, and one circuit behaving differently are different observations.

Common areas technicians investigate

  • Large or continuous air leaks in lines, fittings, valves, chambers or connected systems
  • Air-compressor output and drive-related concerns
  • Governor and compressor-control operation
  • Air dryer, purge valve and associated plumbing
  • Damaged, restricted or leaking air lines
  • Reservoir/tank plumbing and drain-related problems
  • Brake-system valves or other air-operated accessories consuming or leaking air

The symptom alone does not prove which component failed. Isolate and test the problem before replacing parts.
