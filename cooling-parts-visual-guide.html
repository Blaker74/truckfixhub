🌡️ COOLING PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Follow the coolant, heat and airflow path.

Never open a hot pressurized cooling system. These illustrations teach component recognition; exact location and shape vary by engine and chassis.

Radiator

Look for: Large finned heat exchanger at front cooling stack with upper/lower coolant connections.

Job: Transfers engine heat from coolant to airflow.

OVERHEATING →

Surge / Expansion Tank

Look for: Coolant reservoir near top of cooling system with pressure cap, level markings and hoses.

Job: Provides fill/deaeration/expansion space and maintains system coolant supply.

COOLANT PUSHING OUT →

Water Pump

Look for: Engine-mounted pump housing driven by gear/belt depending on engine, connected to coolant passages.

Job: Circulates coolant through engine and heat exchangers.

SEE CIRCULATION →

Thermostat

Look for: Small temperature-controlled valve located inside thermostat housing; often not visible until housing is opened.

Job: Regulates coolant flow based on temperature.

TEMP FLUCTUATES →

Cooling Fan

Look for: Large multi-blade fan behind radiator/cooling stack.

Job: Pulls/pushes air through heat exchangers when vehicle airflow is insufficient.

FAN WON'T TURN ON →

Fan Clutch / Fan Drive

Look for: Heavy hub/drive assembly between engine drive and cooling fan; actuation varies by application.

Job: Engages or controls fan operation when commanded.

FAN STAYS ON →

Coolant Hose

Look for: Large molded rubber hoses and smaller coolant lines connecting engine, radiator, reservoir, heater and coolers.

Job: Carries coolant between system components.

HARD UPPER HOSE →

Coolant Temperature Sensor

Look for: Small threaded electrical sensor in coolant passage/housing with connector.

Job: Reports coolant temperature to engine electronics/instrumentation.

TEMP / SENSOR CODE →

Engine Oil Cooler

Look for: Engine-specific cooler/core or housing where engine oil and coolant exchange heat without mixing.

Job: Controls oil temperature using engine coolant.

OIL IN COOLANT →

EGR Cooler

Look for: Engine-mounted exhaust-gas heat exchanger with coolant and exhaust connections.

Job: Uses engine coolant to reduce EGR gas temperature.
