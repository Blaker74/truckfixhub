DPF WARNING LIGHT

The filter warning is a clue. Find out why the system needs attention.

What the DPF warning means

A diesel particulate filter warning commonly indicates that the aftertreatment system needs attention to soot loading or regeneration. The correct response depends on the warning state, active faults, engine and aftertreatment configuration, and whether the conditions required for regeneration are present. The light alone does not prove the DPF itself has failed.

🔎 Start here

1️⃣ RECORD ACTIVE FAULTS — Save all SPN/FMI or manufacturer-specific codes before clearing them. Related temperature, pressure, dosing, NOx, DEF or engine faults can explain why regeneration is not completing.
2️⃣ CHECK REGEN HISTORY AND CONDITIONS — Determine whether the truck has been completing automatic regens, whether a parked regen has been requested or inhibited, and what operating conditions were present when the warning appeared.
3️⃣ DIAGNOSE WHY — Follow the manufacturer's diagnostic path for the active faults and exact application. Do not replace or clean the filter merely because the DPF lamp is on.

Why a regen may not happen

Regeneration depends on more than the filter. Engine operating conditions, exhaust temperature, sensors, differential-pressure information, dosing systems, wiring and other aftertreatment faults can prevent or interrupt regeneration. A fault elsewhere in the system may need correction before a regen can complete successfully.

🚫 Avoid an unnecessary DPF replacement

A loaded filter can be the result of another problem. Before condemning it, identify why soot loading became excessive or why regeneration could not occur. Depending on the application and condition, the correct repair may involve another component or system, an approved cleaning process, or filter replacement—but that decision should follow diagnosis and current manufacturer limits.

After the repair

Complete the manufacturer-required verification procedure. This may include a diagnostic routine, regeneration, reset or drive verification depending on the engine and aftertreatment system. Confirm the original warning and related faults are resolved.
