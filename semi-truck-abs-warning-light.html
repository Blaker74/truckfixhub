⚠️ ABS WARNING LIGHT

An ABS light tells you the anti-lock system detected a problem. It does not tell you which part to replace.

👇 Start here

1️⃣ TRACTOR OR TRAILER? Identify which ABS warning is active and whether the complaint belongs to the tractor, trailer, or both.
2️⃣ SAVE THE FAULTS. Record the complete ABS diagnostic code, active/inactive status, occurrence information and conditions before clearing anything.
3️⃣ VERIFY POWER AND GROUND. Module supply, grounds, connectors and harness condition can create multiple or misleading symptoms. Test the circuit as the manufacturer specifies.
4️⃣ FOLLOW THE CODE TO THE CORRECT WHEEL / CIRCUIT. A wheel-speed fault can involve the sensor, sensor position/gap where applicable, wiring/connectors, tone/exciter ring, wheel-end movement or the signal path—not automatically a bad sensor.
5️⃣ COMPARE SIGNALS. When scan data is available, compare wheel speeds under the conditions that trigger the fault. One signal dropping out, reading differently or becoming erratic is useful evidence.
6️⃣ CHECK MECHANICAL CONDITIONS. Wheel-end looseness, damaged tone rings/exciters, debris, improper sensor relationship or tire-size/application differences can affect what the controller sees.
7️⃣ VERIFY THE REPAIR. Clear faults only after recording them and correcting the proven cause. Perform the required key-on/self-test/road verification procedure and confirm the warning does not return.

🧠 Pattern clues

LIGHT COMES ON AFTER MOVING: A fault that appears only after wheel movement can point toward wheel-speed signal plausibility, intermittent wiring or a mechanical signal-generation issue.
LIGHT IS ON IMMEDIATELY: Power, ground, module communication, stored active circuit faults or another condition detected before vehicle movement may be involved.
INTERMITTENT ON BUMPS / TURNS: Harness routing, connector tension, wheel-end movement and intermittent sensor wiring deserve close attention. Reproduce before disturbing the circuit when possible.
TRAILER ABS LIGHT: Verify tractor-to-trailer electrical supply/interface first, then follow the trailer ABS power, ground, ECU, sensor and modulator paths.
ABS LIGHT + BRAKE PERFORMANCE COMPLAINT: Diagnose the foundation/pneumatic brake complaint separately as well. An ABS warning does not explain every brake pull, drag, weak brake or air-system problem.
