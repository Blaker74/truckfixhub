🔩 DRIVETRAIN PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Recognizable shapes from transmission to drive axles.

Identify the component first, then diagnose the system. Driveline vibration and repeated U-joint failure often require checking angles, phasing, runout, slip movement and supporting components—not just replacing the failed part.

U-Joint

Look for: Cross-shaped joint with four bearing caps between two driveline yokes.

Job: Transfers torque while allowing operating angle.

DIAGNOSE U-JOINT →

Carrier Bearing

Look for: Round bearing in a thick rubber cushion held by a frame/crossmember bracket.

Job: Supports the center of a multi-piece driveshaft.

DIAGNOSE →

Slip Yoke / Slip Spline

Look for: Splined telescoping section in the driveshaft.

Job: Allows driveline length to change as suspension/chassis moves.

DIAGNOSE →

Driveshaft Tube

Look for: Long round steel/aluminum tube running between transmission and axle assemblies.

Job: Carries rotational torque through the driveline.

DIAGNOSE VIBRATION →

Driveshaft Yoke

Look for: Fork-shaped end with two ears that hold opposing U-joint bearing caps.

Job: Connects the U-joint to a shaft, transmission or axle input.

SEE DRIVETRAIN →

Transmission Output / Companion Yoke Area

Look for: Rear of transmission where the first driveshaft/yoke attaches.

Job: Sends transmission output torque into the driveshaft.

SEE TRANSMISSION →

Differential Carrier

Look for: Large center gear housing in the drive axle with driveshaft input at the front.

Job: Changes drive direction and allows wheel-speed difference.

SEE REAR AXLE →

Pinion / Input Yoke Area

Look for: Front input of differential where driveshaft attaches; pinion seal sits behind the yoke.

Job: Carries driveshaft torque into the differential gearset.

DIAGNOSE PINION SEAL →

Drive Axle Housing

Look for: Heavy axle tube/housing spanning wheel end to wheel end with differential in the center.

Job: Supports axle components and carries drive torque to wheel ends.

SEE AXLE SYSTEM →

Power Divider / Inter-Axle Differential

Look for: Forward tandem differential section with mechanism controlling torque between tandem axles.

Job: Distributes torque between forward and rear drive axles.
