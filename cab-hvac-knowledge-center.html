Cab & HVAC Toolbox

You do not need to know the HVAC part name. Start with what the driver feels, hears or sees.

🌬️ SIMPLE HVAC RULE
MAKE AIR MOVE → CONTROL WHERE IT GOES → CHANGE ITS TEMPERATURE → PROVE THE RESULT
❄️ AC IS NOT COLDFirst decide whether airflow is normal but the air is warm, or airflow itself is weak.
🔥 NO HEATSeparate engine-temperature/coolant problems from airflow and temperature-control problems.
🌬️ WEAK AIRFLOWCheck blower operation, restrictions, cab filter if equipped, doors and duct delivery.
🌀 BLOWER DOESN'T WORKFollow power, command, protection, motor and ground instead of replacing the blower first.
🎚️ TEMPERATURE WON'T CHANGELook at command, actuator/door movement and the hot/cold sources available to the HVAC box.
↔️ AIR COMES FROM WRONG VENTSVerify mode control and door/actuator operation before condemning the control head.

🧰 Four HVAC Drawers

1 · AIRFLOWIs the blower moving enough air? Check every speed and every outlet before diagnosing temperature.
2 · AIR DIRECTIONDoes the air go to panel, floor and defrost when commanded? A mode problem can feel like a heating or cooling problem.
3 · TEMPERATUREIf airflow and direction are correct, determine whether the system has the hot or cold source it needs and whether the HVAC box is controlling it.
4 · CONTROLVerify the driver's command reaches the component and that power, ground, actuators, sensors and controls respond correctly.

🔧 SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT

SEE ITRecord ambient conditions, vent location, blower speed, engine temperature and exactly when the complaint occurs.
NAME ITRoute the complaint toward airflow, direction, heating, refrigeration, electrical supply or electronic control.
TEST ITUse measurements and safe service procedures instead of diagnosing by touch or guessing from one pressure/value.
PROVE ITVerify the original complaint is gone under the same conditions and all modes/speeds operate correctly.

🧠 Do Not Mix Up These Complaints

Air is warm but airflow is strong: focus on temperature generation/control rather than the blower.

Air is cold or hot but barely moving: focus on airflow and restrictions before refrigeration or heater performance.

Only one blower speed works: treat it as a speed-control/electrical clue, not automatic proof of a bad blower motor.

Heat changes with engine load or speed: consider coolant temperature/circulation evidence as well as HVAC controls.

⚠️ HVAC SAFETY & EXACT INFORMATION

Refrigerant systems can contain high pressure and require proper recovery/service equipment, training and applicable environmental procedures. Do not vent refrigerant. Exact refrigerant type, charge amount, pressures, electrical values, control logic and service procedures vary by truck and HVAC configuration. Verify current manufacturer information before service.
