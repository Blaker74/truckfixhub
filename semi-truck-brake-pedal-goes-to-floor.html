Brake feel changed or braking feels weak

“Pedal goes too far” describes what the driver feels. It does not name the failed part.

🛑 If stopping ability is questionable, stop operating the truck.

Do not keep driving just to gather more clues.

🧒 Organize the complaint

🦶 FEEL CHANGED
📟 CHECK AIR
🛞 CHECK PATTERN
🔎 PROVE CAUSE

Normal-looking air gauges do not prove the complete brake system is healthy.

📟 Air pressure appears normal

Move attention toward the application-specific brake system and foundation-brake condition. Inspection may include brake application and movement, adjustment/travel where applicable, friction material, drums or rotors, contamination, hardware and related wheel-end conditions. These are inspection areas—not a parts list.

Useful evidence

Preserve whether the change was sudden or gradual, primary and secondary gauge behavior, warnings, audible leaks, pulling or shaking, hot wheel positions, whether brakes release normally, whether the trailer changes the complaint, and what brake or wheel-end work was recently performed.

SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

Brake feel is evidence. Combine it with air-pressure behavior and wheel-position evidence before naming a cause.

🛑 SAFETY: Reduced stopping ability, abnormal brake control, low-air warnings, severe pulling, smoke or overheating can make a truck unsafe to operate. Do not bypass warning or protection devices, improvise spring-brake release, or perform brake adjustment by guesswork. Exact travel, adjustment, pressure and inspection limits must match the installed system and applicable requirements.
