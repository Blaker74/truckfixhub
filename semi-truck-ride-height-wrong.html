📏 RIDE HEIGHT IS WRONG

Ride height affects more than appearance. It can affect axle geometry and driveline operating angles.

1️⃣ GET THE RIGHT SPEC: Identify chassis/suspension configuration and use the exact manufacturer measurement points and target specification.
2️⃣ VERIFY AIR SUPPLY: Make sure the suspension has adequate air and is not being held in a dump condition.
3️⃣ INSPECT LINKAGE / VALVE: Check height-control linkage, mounting and valve operation for damage, looseness or incorrect setup.
4️⃣ AIR SPRINGS + MECHANICAL PARTS: Inspect bags, mounts, bushings, torque rods and axle-locating parts. Do not adjust a valve to hide bent, worn or broken hardware.
5️⃣ ADJUST BY PROCEDURE: Use the specified settling/cycling and measurement procedure. Small linkage changes can create large chassis-height changes.
6️⃣ VERIFY GEOMETRY: After correcting ride height, verify related alignment/driveline concerns when the original complaint included tracking, vibration or unusual tire wear.
EXACT SPEC → AIR / DUMP STATE → LINKAGE / VALVE → BAGS / MECHANICAL → ADJUST → SETTLE / REMEASURE → VERIFY RELATED GEOMETRY.
⚠️ Do not work beneath an air-supported chassis. Support it mechanically and follow current suspension manufacturer procedures.
