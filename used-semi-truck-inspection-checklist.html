Step 1 · Before you visit

Confirm identity & records

Ask for the VIN, engine identification, maintenance history and permission for an independent inspection. Record gaps instead of assuming everything was serviced.

Find manufacturer resourcesUse the truck and engine identification to find the right information.
Step 2 · At the parked truck

Observe before starting

Ask to see the truck before it has been warmed up. Record visible leaks, damage, tire condition and warning lights. Do not crawl under an unsupported vehicle.

Name the engine partsUnderstand the components you are looking at.Wheels and tiresConnect visible wear and tire concerns to the right guides.
Step 3 · Cold start & warm-up

Record what actually happens

Note cranking speed, smoke, unusual sounds, warning lights and fluid concerns. A seller explanation is useful context, but not proof of the cause.

Starting and chargingSeparate no crank, slow crank and failure to start.Cooling systemUnderstand coolant loss, overheating and fluid mixing.DPF and DEF warningsRecord the exact warning and any available codes.
Step 4 · Qualified inspection

Check the safety systems

Have a qualified inspector assess brakes, steering, suspension, wheel ends and coupling. Do not use a checklist as a roadworthiness certificate.

Air brakesUnderstand the air-building, leaking and braking routes.Steering and suspensionUnderstand steering feel and suspension concerns.Fifth-wheel connectionUnderstand locking, release and play concerns.
Step 5 · Only when safe & authorized

Road test & diagnostic review

Have a qualified driver assess the truck under suitable conditions. Ask for a diagnostic review and document codes and concerns; do not treat an empty warning display as proof of a healthy truck.

Drivetrain symptomsSeparate vibration, clunks and related complaints.Understand fault codesUse codes as evidence, not automatic parts diagnoses.
Step 6 · Before paying

Pause, price or proceed

Unresolved safety issue? Pause for inspection. Repairable concern? Obtain a written estimate. Findings acceptable? Review the complete inspection and purchase details before deciding.
