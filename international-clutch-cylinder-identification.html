Parts-counter field guide

International clutch cylinder marked “Kongsberg”

The identification request began with an International truck, an 8LL transmission description, an air line and one readable name on the cylinder: Kongsberg Automotive. Which of those details belongs in the part-number field? Leave it open.

Read the clues separately

Kongsberg Automotive
Record it under maker markings. Copy any other numbers on a separate line and note where each appeared. A number cast into the housing and a number printed on an assembly label are different pieces of evidence.
8LL
Keep the owner's transmission description. Ask for a legible transmission-tag photo so the supplier can work from the exact model and serial when looking at variants.
Visible air line
Include it in the description. Take a wide photo that shows which component the line actually reaches, then a closer view of that connection. One cropped photo can hide a bracket or make an adjacent line look attached.

The next call to the counter

Ask the supplier to look up the truck build by VIN, identify the reference shown for the clutch actuation assembly, and compare it with the installed unit. Send the VIN privately. If the catalog returns two options, ask what distinguishes those particular options before gathering more photos.

For example, if the options differ by mounting layout, two more photos of the maker name add little. A view showing the full mount answers the question. If the assemblies look alike and the supplier needs a build or serial break, ask which tag resolves that break. Take photos only from a safe, accessible position.

A request you can use

Please identify the clutch cylinder for an International described as having an 8LL. The visible marking reads “Kongsberg Automotive,” and an air line is visible. I can provide the VIN privately, transmission tag and photos of the mount and connections. Which identifier separates your catalog options, and does the quote cover the complete assembly?

The available clues leave the service part number unresolved. Record the confirmed number only after the supplier matches the installed assembly to the application.
