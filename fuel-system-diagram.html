⛽ DIESEL FUEL SYSTEM

Follow the fuel from the tank to the cylinder.

1️⃣ FUEL TANK
Stores the fuel and supplies the system
↓
2️⃣ SUPPLY LINE + FILTERS
Carry fuel forward while filters remove contamination
↓
3️⃣ LOW-PRESSURE / TRANSFER SIDE
Provides the fuel supply required by the injection system
↓
4️⃣ HIGH-PRESSURE PUMP / RAIL
On common-rail systems, builds and controls the pressure required for injection
↓
5️⃣ INJECTORS → CYLINDERS
Meter and deliver fuel into the engine
🔇 Hard start / no start?
Verify usable fuel, filter condition, restriction or air intrusion, low-side supply and commanded/actual injection pressure using the correct engine procedure.
🐌 Low power?
Check for restricted filters, inadequate supply, pressure that cannot meet demand, fuel quality problems and engine derates before replacing injection components.
🌫️ Smoke / rough running?
Fuel delivery is only one possibility. Also consider air supply, cylinder condition, injector performance and related electronic faults.
💧 Fuel leak?
Identify whether the leak is on the low-pressure or high-pressure side and follow the manufacturer’s depressurization and repair procedure.

Prove the supply side before blaming the expensive side

A high-pressure pump or injector cannot work correctly without the fuel supply it was designed to receive. Restrictions, plugged filters, air entering the low-pressure side, poor fuel quality or inadequate transfer supply can create hard-start and low-power complaints that resemble expensive injection failures. Test the system in order and compare measured values with the engine manufacturer’s specifications.

🧠 Simple rule: prove fuel can get FROM THE TANK to the injection system correctly before blaming the expensive parts.
⚠️ High-pressure diesel fuel can penetrate skin and cause severe injury. Never use your hand to search for a leak. Depressurize and service the system only by the manufacturer’s procedure.
