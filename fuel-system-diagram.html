⛽ DIESEL FUEL SYSTEM

Follow the fuel from the tank to the cylinder.

1️⃣ FUEL TANK
Stores the fuel
↓
2️⃣ SUPPLY LINE + FILTERS
Move and clean the fuel
↓
3️⃣ LOW-PRESSURE / TRANSFER SIDE
Feeds the high-pressure system
↓
4️⃣ HIGH-PRESSURE PUMP / RAIL
Builds and controls injection pressure where equipped
↓
5️⃣ INJECTORS → CYLINDERS
Meter fuel into the engine
🔇 Hard start / no start?
Check fuel level, restriction, air intrusion, supply pressure and related fault codes before condemning injectors.
🐌 Low power?
Look for restricted filters, weak supply, pressure problems, leaks and derates.
🌫️ Smoke / rough running?
Check fuel quality, injector balance concerns, air supply and engine mechanical condition.
💧 Fuel leak?
Find the exact source and repair it. Do not use your hand to search for a high-pressure leak.
🧠 Simple rule: prove fuel can get FROM THE TANK to the injection system before blaming the expensive parts.
⚠️ High-pressure diesel fuel can penetrate skin and cause severe injury. Never check a suspected high-pressure leak with your hand.
