💧 PINION SEAL KEEPS LEAKING

A repeat leak means the seal may be the victim instead of the cause.

1️⃣ CONFIRM THE SOURCE: Clean the area and trace where lubricant begins. Do not confuse engine/transmission/driveline contamination with an axle input leak.
2️⃣ LUBRICANT + VENT: Verify axle lubricant level/specification and inspect the axle vent/breather system as applicable. Pressure can encourage leakage.
3️⃣ YOKE / SEALING SURFACE: Inspect the exact sealing surface for grooves, corrosion, damage, runout concerns or incorrect parts.
4️⃣ PINION / INPUT CONDITION: Check applicable bearing movement and input condition using axle-manufacturer procedures. A moving shaft can defeat a new seal.
5️⃣ INSTALLATION HISTORY: Verify correct seal, installation depth/orientation/tools, yoke/fastener procedure and any required replacement hardware. Some axle designs have critical preload/retention procedures.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Correct the root cause, restore lubricant level and inspect after a representative road/load cycle.
CONFIRM SOURCE → LEVEL / VENT → SEALING SURFACE → INPUT / BEARINGS → INSTALLATION → ROOT CAUSE → VERIFY.
⚠️ Do not disturb pinion retention or preload without the exact axle manufacturer procedure. Incorrect service can damage the gearset.
