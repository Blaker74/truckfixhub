Do not diagnose the warning switch before proving the air pressure.

📟 Gauges appear normal but warning stays on

Do not immediately call the warning false. First verify actual air pressure and brake-system operation using the correct test procedure. Only after the air system is proven safe should diagnosis move toward pressure sensing, wiring, connectors, dash/module information or warning components.

👀 One gauge or both?

Record whether primary, secondary or both indications are low or behaving abnormally. That difference can help narrow which part of the air system needs attention.

A low-air warning is a symptom. First prove whether the pressure is truly low. Then prove why.

🛑 SAFETY: Never ignore, silence, bypass or defeat a low-air warning to keep a vehicle operating. If the truck cannot maintain safe air pressure or braking/release behavior is abnormal, do not continue operating it. Never crawl beneath an unsecured vehicle. Exact warning thresholds, pressure specifications and inspection procedures must be verified for the exact vehicle and applicable requirements.
