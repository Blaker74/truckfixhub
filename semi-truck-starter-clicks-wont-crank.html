🔊 STARTER CLICKS — WON’T CRANK

A click proves something moved or was commanded. It does not prove the battery bank, high-current path or starter motor is healthy.

1️⃣ LISTEN + OBSERVE: Single click, repeated rapid clicking or a heavy clunk? Note dash voltage behavior and whether lights collapse during the attempt.
2️⃣ BATTERY CAPABILITY: Test the battery bank. Repeated clicking often accompanies voltage collapse, but prove the condition.
3️⃣ HIGH-CURRENT PATH: Perform approved voltage-drop tests across positive and ground paths during the crank request.
4️⃣ COMMAND: Verify the starter/solenoid receives the correct command and that safety/interlock logic is satisfied for the exact vehicle.
5️⃣ STARTER: Only after supply, return and command are proven should the starter/solenoid assembly become the primary suspect.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Perform repeated starts under the original hot/cold condition.
CLICK TYPE → BATTERY → POSITIVE PATH → GROUND PATH → COMMAND → STARTER → VERIFY.
⚠️ Never bridge starter terminals or bypass neutral/clutch safety systems to force a crank.
