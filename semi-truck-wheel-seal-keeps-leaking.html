💧 WHEEL SEAL KEEPS LEAKING

A repeat leak means the job is not just “replace the seal again.” Find what is defeating the seal.

1️⃣ CONFIRM THE SOURCE: Clean and trace where lubricant begins. Separate hub/seal leakage from other nearby fluids or old residue.
2️⃣ SEALING SURFACES: Inspect the applicable spindle/hub seal surfaces for wear, grooves, corrosion, damage or incorrect repair surfaces.
3️⃣ HUB MOVEMENT: Incorrect bearing adjustment or damaged bearings can allow movement that affects sealing. Measure by the exact wheel-end procedure.
4️⃣ LUBRICANT / VENTING: Verify correct lubricant, level and hub/axle venting where applicable. Overfill, contamination or pressure problems can contribute.
5️⃣ INSTALLATION: Confirm the correct seal/application, installation depth/orientation and approved tools/procedure. Inspect the hub/spindle for damage caused during prior service.
6️⃣ BRAKES + VERIFY: If lubricant reached friction components, service them per manufacturer requirements. Recheck leakage after operation.
CONFIRM SOURCE → SEAL SURFACE → HUB MOVEMENT → LUBE / VENT → INSTALLATION → BRAKES → VERIFY.
⚠️ Lubricant-contaminated brakes and low wheel-end lubricant are serious safety concerns. Follow manufacturer replacement/service requirements.
