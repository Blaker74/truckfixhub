👇 Follow the complaint

🐌 LOW POWER / LOW BOOST: Save active faults first. Inspect inlet restriction and the complete charge-air path, then compare expected/commanded operation with actual data before moving toward turbocharger or actuator diagnosis.
🎵 WHISTLE / HISS UNDER LOAD: Inspect boots, clamps, pipes and charge-air cooler for evidence of leakage. Remember that exhaust leakage can also create a whistle or hiss. Locate and prove the leak.
🌫️ BLACK SMOKE / POOR POWER: Insufficient air is one possibility, not the only one. Prove the air path and boost delivery, then move into fuel and engine-condition diagnosis if the air system is healthy.
🐢 DERATE: A derated engine may not be allowed to produce the boost or power you expect. Identify the fault/system commanding the derate before treating low boost as the root cause.
🛢️ OIL IN CHARGE PIPING: Residue alone does not prove turbo failure. Evaluate amount, oil consumption, source and related engine/turbo symptoms using manufacturer inspection limits.

🧠 Three questions before replacing a turbo

1️⃣ Can air get IN? Check the air filter and inlet path for restriction or damage.
2️⃣ Can boosted air STAY IN? Check boots, clamps, piping and charge-air cooler using the applicable leak-test procedure.
3️⃣ Is the engine actually COMMANDING the expected boost? Consider faults, derate strategy, sensors, controls/actuator and operating conditions before judging turbo hardware.
