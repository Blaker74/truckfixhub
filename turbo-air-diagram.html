💨 TURBO & CHARGE AIR

Follow the air from the filter to the engine.

1️⃣ AIR FILTER / INLET
Clean air enters the engine system
↓
2️⃣ TURBO COMPRESSOR
Compresses the intake air
↓
3️⃣ CHARGE AIR COOLER
Cools the compressed air
↓
4️⃣ BOOTS / PIPES / CLAMPS
Carry boosted air toward the intake
↓
5️⃣ INTAKE MANIFOLD → ENGINE
Boosted air reaches the cylinders
🐌 Low boost / low power?
Check intake restriction, loose clamps, split boots, charge-air leaks and related fault codes before blaming the turbo.
🎵 Whistle / air noise?
Inspect charge-air connections, cooler, pipes and boots for leaks or damage.
🌫️ Black smoke?
Too little air can upset the air/fuel balance. Check restrictions and boost leaks along with fuel and engine conditions.
🛢️ Oil near turbo piping?
A light film and a major oil leak are not the same thing. Find the source and check the complete system before condemning the turbo.
🧠 Simple rule: BOOST LOST anywhere between the turbo and engine can feel like a bad turbo.
⚠️ Turbochargers and exhaust components can be extremely hot. Keep hands and tools away from rotating components.
