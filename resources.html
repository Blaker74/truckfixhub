COMMON SEMI-TRUCK PROBLEMS

Starting and electrical

Separate a truck that won’t crank from one that cranks but won’t start. For slow starter speed, charging complaints and intermittent power, use the starting and charging diagnostic path and heavy-duty electrical troubleshooting guide.

Low power, boost and fuel

A semi truck with low power can involve fuel delivery, turbo/charge-air problems, engine condition, aftertreatment derate or drivetrain behavior. Compare the complaint with the turbo and charge-air guide and diesel fuel-system diagnostic path.

DPF, DEF and derate

For engine derate, DPF warnings, DEF warnings or a truck that won’t regen, diagnose the complete aftertreatment system rather than replacing the component named by a fault code without testing.

Overheating and coolant complaints

Use the semi-truck overheating guide for high temperature complaints. Coolant loss, pressure, oil contamination and fan operation have separate paths in the heavy-duty cooling-system guide.

Air brakes, wheel ends and vibration

For pressure loss start with the semi-truck air leak guide. Hot hubs, leaking wheel seals and bearing complaints belong in the wheel-end diagnostic path. Road-speed vibration can be narrowed with the drivetrain and driveshaft guide.
