🛑 BRAKES FEEL WEAK / STOPPING DISTANCE LONG

Treat reduced braking performance as an out-of-service safety concern until the complete brake system is inspected and proven.

1️⃣ DEFINE: Tractor, trailer or combination? Always weak, after repeated stops, after service, or only loaded?
2️⃣ AIR SYSTEM: Verify supply pressure, application behavior and abnormal leakage using the correct vehicle procedure.
3️⃣ BRAKE STROKE / ADJUSTMENT: Inspect chamber stroke and adjustment to applicable limits. Automatic slack adjusters should not be repeatedly hand-adjusted as a substitute for finding the fault.
4️⃣ FRICTION / DRUM / ROTOR: Inspect lining/pad condition, contamination, heat damage, drum/rotor condition and mechanical movement.
5️⃣ DELIVERY / BALANCE: Verify valves, chambers and tractor/trailer delivery where braking is uneven or delayed.
6️⃣ HEAT / FADE: If performance changes after repeated braking, investigate overheating, adjustment and friction condition rather than continuing to operate.
DEFINE → AIR SUPPLY/APPLICATION → STROKE/ADJUSTMENT → FRICTION → DELIVERY/BALANCE → PROVE → FULL BRAKE VERIFICATION.
⚠️ A vehicle with inadequate braking performance must not be returned to service until the cause is corrected and the braking system passes required inspection/testing.
