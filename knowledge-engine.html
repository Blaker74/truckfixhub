How TruckFixHub Gets Smarter

The library can keep growing without turning into a junk drawer. Every useful fact has to be organized, simplified and labeled by evidence.

THE KNOWLEDGE LOOP

📥1. COLLECTQuestion, symptom, code, field fix or trusted reference.
🔎2. CHECKSeparate evidence from guesses and missing details.
🧩3. ORGANIZEConnect it to the right truck system, symptom and test path.
🧒4. SIMPLIFYExplain it so a new tech can understand what to do next.

EVERY ANSWER NEEDS A LABEL

✅ VERIFIEDSupported by dependable service information or repeatable evidence.
👨‍🔧 FIELD EXPERIENCEA real-world observation or repair lesson that may help, but still needs vehicle-specific confirmation.
⚠️ NEEDS VERIFICATIONUseful clue, incomplete claim or unresolved question. Never present it as proven fact.

THE RULES THAT KEEP IT USEFUL

  • Never invent a specification. Torque, pressure, resistance, pinout and procedure values must be verified for the exact application.
  • Never turn a fault code into a parts cannon. A code identifies a problem area or condition; testing proves the cause.
  • Reuse knowledge. One good lesson should connect to symptoms, systems, diagrams, fault codes and beginner explanations where it belongs.
  • Make complicated information smaller. Start with what the truck is doing, then reveal the next useful step instead of dumping a manual on the user.
  • Keep uncertainty visible. If TruckFixHub does not know, it says so and identifies what evidence is needed next.

WHAT THE COMMUNITY ADDS

A technician may bring the one piece a library cannot get from a generic description: what actually happened on the truck. Good community posts should include the symptom, vehicle details, codes, what was tested, what was found and what finally fixed it. Those experiences can expose missing topics and better diagnostic branches without automatically becoming verified facts.
