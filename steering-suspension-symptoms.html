You do not need to know the part name. Start with what the truck is doing and work from the tires inward.

🧰 Six Foundation Drawers

1 · TIRESPressure, size, position, wear pattern, damage and load can steer or shake the truck before any steering part is bad.
2 · WHEEL ENDSBearings, hubs, brakes and rotating condition must be stable. Wheel-end trouble can imitate steering and alignment problems.
3 · STEERING LINKAGEKing pins, tie rods, drag links, steering gear connections and related joints must transfer movement without unsafe looseness or binding.
4 · SUSPENSIONSprings, air springs, bushings, torque rods, shocks and mounting points hold the axles where they belong.
5 · RIDE HEIGHTRide height affects geometry and driveline relationships. Verify the suspension is holding the truck at the correct position.
6 · ALIGNMENTAlignment measurements mean the most after tires, wheel ends, linkage, suspension and ride height are mechanically sound.

🔍 Let The Symptom Pick The Drawer

↩️ PULLS WHILE CRUISINGNote road crown, tire position, load, ride height and whether the complaint changes after tire position changes.
🛑 PULLS WHILE BRAKINGDo not make an alignment-only diagnosis. Compare brake and wheel-end behavior side to side.
〰️ CONSTANT CORRECTIONCheck tire condition, steering free play, linkage, suspension movement and geometry.
📳 SHAKE AT ROAD SPEEDInspect tires/wheels, wheel ends and rotating components before chasing steering parts.
💥 SHIMMY AFTER A BUMPLook for looseness, damping problems and joints that allow movement to continue after the bump.
🪶 ONE-SIDED / CUPPED WEARRecord the exact pattern and tire position. Tire wear is evidence, not automatic proof of bad alignment.

🔧 The Diagnostic Drawer Never Changes

SEE ITRecord the complaint, road speed, braking, road surface, load, tire wear and when the truck changes direction.
NAME ITRoute the evidence toward tires, wheel ends, steering linkage, suspension, ride height, steering assist or alignment.
TEST ITInspect and measure the suspected area using safe support and the correct manufacturer procedure.
PROVE ITRepair the supported cause, verify mechanical integrity and reproduce the original driving condition.

✅ Alignment Comes After The Foundation

An alignment machine cannot repair a loose joint, damaged wheel end, tire problem or failed suspension component. Fix mechanical problems first so the machine is measuring a stable truck.

⚠️ STEERING & SUSPENSION ARE SAFETY-CRITICAL

Do not operate a vehicle with severe looseness, damaged steering linkage, suspected wheel-bearing failure, unsecured suspension components or another condition that makes directional control unsafe. Chock and support the vehicle correctly. Exact free-play limits, ride-height dimensions, alignment specifications and torque values vary by application and must be verified with current manufacturer information.
