What is the truck doing on the road?

Choose the strongest steering, handling or tire symptom. Inspect mechanical looseness and tire condition before assuming alignment is the cause.

↩️Pulls Left / RightTruck consistently pulls, drifts or changes direction under braking or cruise.〰️Wander / Loose SteeringConstant correction is needed, steering feels loose or the truck will not hold a line.📳Shake / ShimmySteering wheel or chassis shake appears at speed, over bumps or while braking.🛞Uneven Tire WearFeathering, cupping, shoulder wear or abnormal wear pattern is developing.💪Hard SteeringHigh steering effort, intermittent assist, noise or poor return-to-center.↕️Ride Height / SuspensionTruck leans, sits low/high, suspension will not maintain height or ride is unusually harsh.
Diagnostic rule: Tires, wheel ends, steering linkage, suspension joints, ride height and alignment interact. Inspect for looseness, damage, pressure and abnormal tire wear before making an alignment-only diagnosis.
Safety: Steering and suspension faults can cause loss of vehicle control. Do not operate the vehicle with severe looseness, damaged steering linkage, failed wheel-end components or another condition that makes directional control unsafe.
