🛡️ KEEP THE SHOP FLOOR USEFUL

This community is for practical heavy-duty truck questions, troubleshooting experience and respectful help. Community posts are user-submitted and are not manufacturer service instructions.

⚠️ Safety first: never rely on a community reply alone for a safety-critical repair, specification, torque value, brake procedure or other procedure where an error could cause injury or equipment damage. Verify critical work with current manufacturer information and applicable regulations.
👇 Community rules
  • Keep posts relevant to trucks, repair, maintenance, parts, diagnostics or the trucking industry.
  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hateful content or personal attacks.
  • No spam, scams, deceptive links or repeated advertising.
  • Do not post private information, passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal data.
  • Do not upload content you do not have the right to share.
  • Explain what you observed and what you tested. Do not present a guess as a proven repair.
  • TruckFixHub may remove content that is unsafe, unlawful, abusive, deceptive, irrelevant or harmful to the community.
