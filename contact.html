What can you contact us about?

Website SupportBroken links, account trouble, navigation problems or a feature that is not behaving correctly.
Technical CorrectionsIncorrect, outdated or unclear heavy-duty diagnostic information that should be reviewed.
General QuestionsQuestions about TruckFixHub, its tools, standards or how to use the site.

Reporting a technical correction

Useful corrections are welcome. When possible, include enough context for us to review the exact issue:

  • The TruckFixHub page or diagram involved.
  • The statement, fault-code path or diagnostic step you believe needs review.
  • Vehicle context when relevant, such as manufacturer, model/year, engine or controller.
  • The corrected information and the source or test evidence supporting it, when available.

We would rather correct questionable information than leave it published simply because it was already on the site.

Important support boundaries

Email support is not an emergency repair service and cannot determine whether a vehicle is safe to operate. For an immediate safety concern involving brakes, steering, fire, vehicle movement or another hazardous condition, stop operation as appropriate and use qualified on-site service or emergency assistance.

For your protection, please do not email passwords, payment-card information, API keys, authentication codes or other sensitive credentials.
