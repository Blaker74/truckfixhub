📸 Part-photo contributions

Send clear photos you personally took or have permission to share. A useful submission includes the part name if known, truck/application, where it is located and any identifying information that can be safely shown.

Please avoid faces, license plates, VINs, customer information, paperwork, addresses or other private information in the photo. Sending a photo does not guarantee publication; TruckFixHub may crop or label accepted images for educational identification.

🧰 Technical questions

For a truck repair question, start with TruckFixHub’s diagnostic guides or community. Include the truck, engine or component information, symptom, fault codes and what has already been tested when those details are available.

TruckFixHub support cannot replace application-specific manufacturer repair procedures or emergency assistance.
