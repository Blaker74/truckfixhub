What changed in the transmission or driveline?

Choose the strongest symptom first. Separate a shift-control complaint from a mechanical clutch, transmission, driveshaft or axle complaint.

↕️Won't Shift / Bad ShiftWon't select a gear, misses shifts, harsh shifts or transmission warning is active.🦶Clutch ComplaintSlip, drag, poor release, engagement problem, pedal or clutch-actuation concern.〰️VibrationSpeed-related shake, vibration under load or driveline vibration through cab/chassis.🔊Noise / ClunkClunk on takeoff, knock, squeal, growl or noise that changes with speed/load.⛔No DriveEngine runs but vehicle will not move or power is not reaching the drive wheels.🧰Driveshaft / U-JointLoose joint, damaged yoke, carrier-bearing concern, driveline play or visible damage.
Diagnostic rule: Diagnose by when the symptom occurs: stationary, during clutch engagement, during a shift, under torque, on coast or at a specific road speed. That timing helps separate transmission, clutch, driveshaft and axle causes.
Safety: A damaged driveshaft, U-joint, yoke or other rotating driveline component can fail violently. Do not operate the vehicle with severe vibration, visible driveline damage or an unsecured component. Chock and support the vehicle correctly before inspection.
