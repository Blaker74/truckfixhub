🛢️ How a Visual Spec Card Will Work

🚛
1. Identify the exact engineExample: Detroit DD15, Cummins X15, PACCAR MX-13.
2. Identify the configurationOil pan, model year and application can change the correct capacity.
3. Show the verified specificationCapacity, approved oil specification/viscosity and service notes appear here only after the configuration is known.

⚙️ Transmission

Visual cards will be organized by exact transmission model — not just by truck make.

🔩 Axle / Differential

Visual cards will identify axle model, lubricant specification and configuration before showing capacity.

❄️ Air Conditioning

Refrigerant charge and oil information will require the exact vehicle/HVAC configuration.

💧 Cooling System

Coolant specification and approximate system capacity will be separated from fill/bleed procedure.

🔧 Filters & Service

Related filter and service information can be connected to each verified configuration.

Accuracy rule: TruckFixHub will not guess a fluid capacity. Specifications can vary by model year, oil pan, transmission, axle, HVAC package and other equipment. Visual cards must identify the configuration and use verified service information before presenting a number.
