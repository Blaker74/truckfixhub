⚙️ Verified Transmission Cards

✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Eaton Endurant HD

Approx. 7.5–8.5 L
Approximately 16–18 pints depending on transmission angle. Fill with approved PS-386 lubricant until a small amount runs from the oil-check plug hole. PTO-equipped units require the level to be rechecked after running to fill the PTO.
Source: Eaton Cummins Endurant HD Service Manual TRSM0950.
✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Eaton Heavy-Duty Manual / Automated

PS-386
Eaton specifies PS-386 synthetic transmission lubricant for automated heavy-duty transmissions and heavy-duty mechanical transmissions where the PS-386 specification applies. Capacity depends on exact transmission model.
Source: Eaton Transmission and Clutch Lubrication Specifications / TCMT0021.
✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Allison On-Highway

TES 668®
Allison recommends TES 668 approved fluid for its 1000, 2000, 3000 and 4000 Series on-highway products. Exact refill capacity must be selected by transmission model, sump and configuration.
Source: Allison Transmission Approved Fluids.

🔩 Verified Axle / Differential Cards

✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Dana Spicer Tandem Drive Axles

75W-90 Synthetic
For Spicer-approved synthetic lubricant meeting SHAES-256. Dana's service information lists SAE 75W-90 for line-haul applications; approved synthetic lubricant intervals can reach 500,000 miles / 5 years with LMS wheel ends. Vocational interval is 120,000 miles / 1 year. Exact fill quantity varies by axle family and chassis installation.
Source: Dana Spicer Tandem Drive Axles Service Manual AXSM-0041.
✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Dana Spicer Axle Fill Rule

Fill to plug level
Dana states axle fill capacities are guidelines because installed axle angle changes actual quantity. The final reference is the filler hole: fill approved lubricant until level with the bottom of the filler hole.
Source: Dana Spicer S140 Series Service Manual.

💧 Verified Cooling-System Cards

✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Cummins X15 — Engine Only

28 qt / 26.5 L
This is the engine-only coolant capacity, not the complete truck cooling-system capacity. Cummins specifies fully formulated coolant meeting CES 14603. Complete chassis capacity depends on radiator, plumbing, heater and other OEM-installed components.
Source: Cummins X15 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, 5544396.
✓ OEM SOURCE VERIFIED

Cummins X15 Coolant Rule

50/50 Fully Formulated
Cummins recommends a 50/50 mixture of good-quality water and fully formulated antifreeze, or fully formulated premixed coolant, meeting CES 14603. Do not assume coolant color identifies compatibility.
Source: Cummins X15 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, 5544396.
