🔨 ENGINE KNOCK / ABNORMAL NOISE

Do not diagnose a sound by name alone. Find when it happens, where it is loudest and what changes it.

👇 Capture the noise

1️⃣ WHEN: Cold start, hot idle, acceleration, load, deceleration, engine brake, certain RPM or constantly.
2️⃣ WHERE: Top of engine, front gear/accessory area, turbo/air path, bellhousing area, lower engine or uncertain.
3️⃣ WHAT CHANGES IT: RPM, load, cylinder contribution, fan engagement, A/C/accessory operation or engine temperature.
4️⃣ CHECK THE BASICS: Oil pressure/level, faults, smoke, roughness, power loss and recent repairs can turn a vague sound into a directed diagnostic path.
5️⃣ RECENT TOP-END WORK? If the noise appeared immediately after cylinder-head, camshaft, injector, rocker or engine-brake work, treat the repair history as evidence. Verify what was disturbed before assuming a new unrelated failure.

🧠 Possible families—not verdicts

COMBUSTION / CYLINDER: Fueling or cylinder-condition problems can create sharp combustion-related noise. Use OEM cylinder tests rather than replacing an injector by sound.
VALVE TRAIN / TOP END: Valve lash, rocker/cam/actuation components and related conditions require inspection/testing to the exact engine procedure. A new top-end noise after repair deserves an assembly and adjustment review.
ACCESSORY / FRONT DRIVE: Fan drive, idlers, tensioners, compressor, alternator and other driven components can imitate internal engine noise.
AIR / EXHAUST: Charge-air or exhaust leaks can whistle, hiss, chirp or pulse and may change strongly under load.
INTERNAL MECHANICAL: Deep knocking, low oil pressure, metal evidence or severe vibration can indicate a serious condition. Stop operation when continued running could cause damage.

🔥 Detroit DD15 top-end noise path

If a DD15 develops abnormal top-end noise after head, camshaft, rocker, injector or engine-brake work, verify the assembly path, adjustments and timing with the exact engine service information. If the noise is paired with rough running or a weak-cylinder result, follow the mechanical diagnostic path as well.
