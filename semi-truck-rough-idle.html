🫨 DIESEL ROUGH IDLE

Rough idle is a symptom. First determine whether the engine is missing, hunting, shaking mechanically, or reacting to another system.

👇 Capture the complaint

1️⃣ COLD, HOT OR BOTH? Record coolant temperature and whether the symptom changes as the engine warms.
2️⃣ ONLY AT IDLE? Note whether the engine smooths out with RPM or remains rough under load.
3️⃣ WATCH THE EXHAUST. Record abnormal smoke color, odor, popping or uneven exhaust rhythm. Smoke is evidence, not a diagnosis by itself.
4️⃣ SAVE ALL FAULTS. Record complete OEM/SPN/FMI information, active/inactive status and operating conditions before clearing codes.
5️⃣ CHECK RECENT HISTORY. Fuel filter service, running out of fuel, injector work, wiring repairs, valve work, programming, cylinder-head work or another recent repair can be important evidence.

🧠 Follow the systems

⛽ FUEL SUPPLY: Verify usable fuel, filters, restrictions, air intrusion where applicable and low-side supply before blaming injectors or high-pressure components.
🔥 CYLINDER CONTRIBUTION: If scan-tool/OEM tests indicate one cylinder contributes differently, follow the manufacturer test path. Injector, wiring, compression, valve-train and other cylinder-specific conditions can overlap.
🌬️ AIR / EGR / INTAKE: Check abnormal air-path conditions and commanded-versus-actual data where applicable. Do not assume every rough idle is fuel.
⚡ SENSORS / WIRING: Unstable engine-speed, pressure, temperature or control inputs can change fueling and idle behavior. Prove power, ground, signal and connector integrity.
⚙️ MECHANICAL: When evidence points mechanical, move to the exact OEM compression, valve-train, timing or related mechanical test.
🧱 MOUNTS / ACCESSORIES: If engine speed and combustion appear stable but the truck shakes, inspect mounting and driven-accessory/mechanical vibration before treating the shake as a misfire.

🔎 Pattern clues

ROUGH AFTER FUEL SERVICE: Recheck filter installation, seals, priming, supply and possible air entry first.
ONE CYLINDER SUSPECTED: Use contribution/cutout or manufacturer-directed tests where supported. Do not replace an injector solely because a cylinder is named in a symptom or code.
IDLE HUNTS UP AND DOWN: Compare commanded and actual engine speed and relevant fueling/control inputs. Hunting is not the same complaint as one weak cylinder.
ROUGH + LOW POWER: Follow both complaints together. Shared fuel, air, boost, aftertreatment or control problems may explain both.
ROUGH IMMEDIATELY AFTER TOP-END WORK: Treat recent work as evidence. Verify disturbed connectors, injector installation, valvetrain assembly/adjustment and timing before assuming a new unrelated failure.

🔥 Is it a Detroit DD15?

If cylinder evidence points mechanical—or the complaint began after cylinder-head, camshaft, injector or valvetrain work—move into the focused DD15 paths.
