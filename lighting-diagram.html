💡 LIGHTING

Every light needs a command, power path, lamp and ground.

1️⃣ SWITCH / COMMAND
Driver or module requests the light
↓
2️⃣ FUSE / PROTECTION
Protects the circuit
↓
3️⃣ WIRING + CONNECTORS
Carry power to the lamp
↓
4️⃣ LAMP / LED ASSEMBLY
Turns electrical power into light
↓
5️⃣ GROUND / RETURN PATH
Completes the circuit
⬛ One light out?
Check that lamp/LED, connector, local power and local ground before chasing the whole truck.
🚫 Several lights out?
Look for what they share: fuse, power feed, ground, switch/module or connector.
✨ Dim / flickering?
Check voltage drop, corrosion, loose terminals, grounds and charging-system voltage.
💥 Fuse keeps blowing?
Do not keep installing fuses. Find the short, damaged wiring or overloaded circuit.
🧠 Simple rule: POWER IN + GOOD GROUND = the lamp should work. Prove both at the connector before replacing parts.
⚠️ Use the correct fuse rating. Never bypass circuit protection with wire, foil or an oversized fuse.
