🔩 Know The Assembly

HUBCarries the bearing cups/races and wheel mounting system.
BEARINGSSupport the rotating hub on the spindle.
WHEEL SEALKeeps lubricant in and contamination out.
SPINDLESupports the hub and provides bearing and seal running surfaces.
ADJUSTMENT HARDWAREControls bearing setting/end play for the exact system.
LUBRICANTOil or approved grease depending on wheel-end design.

💧 Leak Drawer

1 · IDENTIFY THE FLUID AND SOURCEDo not call every wet wheel a failed seal. Find where the wetness begins.
2 · CHECK LUBRICANTLow level, contamination or mixed lubricant changes the diagnostic path.
3 · INSPECT HUB, SEAL AREA & VENTINGLook for damage, looseness and installation evidence.
4 · INSPECT THE RUNNING SURFACEA new seal cannot repair a damaged spindle or wear surface.
5 · REPAIR AND VERIFYSet up and lubricate using the exact manufacturer procedure.

🌡️ Hot Wheel Drawer

1 · COMPARE SIDES SAFELYA temperature difference is evidence—not a part number.
2 · SEPARATE BRAKE HEAT FROM BEARING HEATCheck for dragging-brake evidence before condemning bearings.
3 · CHECK LUBRICANT AND PLAYLoss of lubrication or incorrect adjustment can create heat.
4 · INSPECT IF TEARDOWN IS JUSTIFIEDLook for discoloration, scoring, pitting, spalling or roughness.

↔️ Looseness Drawer

1 · FIND WHERE IT MOVESDo not confuse king-pin, suspension or steering looseness with wheel-bearing movement.
2 · MEASURE CORRECTLYUse the approved procedure and specification for the exact wheel-end system.
3 · FIND WHY ADJUSTMENT CHANGEDIf adjustment will not remain correct, find the worn, damaged or incorrectly installed component.

🔊 Noise Drawer

1 · REPRODUCE AND LOCATE ITRoad speed, turning load and braking can help separate possible sources.
2 · CHECK LOOK-ALIKESTires, brakes and driveline components can imitate wheel-bearing noise.
3 · INSPECT WHEN EVIDENCE JUSTIFIES TEARDOWNDamage patterns are evidence. Document what you find.

⚠️ SAFETY-CRITICAL ASSEMBLY

Wheel ends carry vehicle load and retain the wheel/hub assembly. Incorrect bearing adjustment, fastener procedure, seal installation or lubrication can cause serious failure. TruckFixHub intentionally does not publish one universal torque or end-play value.

✅ VERIFY THE EXACT EQUIPMENT

Before assembly, identify the axle, hub, bearing-adjustment system, seal and lubrication method. Use the current manufacturer procedure for torque sequences, end play, lubricant quantity and component reuse limits.
