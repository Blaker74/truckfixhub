🌡️ COOLING SYSTEM

Coolant has one job: carry engine heat away.

1️⃣ WATER PUMP
Moves coolant
↓
2️⃣ ENGINE
Heat moves into the coolant
↓
3️⃣ THERMOSTAT
Controls flow as temperature changes
↓
4️⃣ RADIATOR + FAN
Release heat to the air
↓
5️⃣ BACK TO WATER PUMP
The cycle starts again
🔥 Overheating?
Check coolant level, leaks, airflow, fan operation, belt/pump drive and coolant circulation.
💧 Losing coolant?
Inspect hoses, clamps, radiator, reservoir, pump area and external leak points.
🌡️ Runs cold?
Consider thermostat operation and temperature-sensor accuracy.
🫧 Pressure / bubbles?
Do not assume one cause. Verify cap, leaks, combustion-gas concerns and system condition.
🧠 Simple rule: NO COOLANT FLOW or NO AIRFLOW = heat cannot leave the engine.
⚠️ Never open a hot pressurized cooling system. Hot coolant and steam can cause severe burns.
