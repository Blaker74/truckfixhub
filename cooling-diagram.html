🌡️ COOLING SYSTEM

The cooling system moves engine heat into the air.

1️⃣ WATER PUMP
Circulates coolant through the system
↓
2️⃣ ENGINE
Engine heat transfers into the coolant
↓
3️⃣ THERMOSTAT
Helps regulate coolant flow and operating temperature
↓
4️⃣ RADIATOR + AIRFLOW / FAN
Transfer coolant heat into outside air
↓
5️⃣ RETURN TO WATER PUMP
The circulation loop continues
🔥 Overheating?
Verify coolant level and condition, leaks, airflow/fan operation, belt or pump drive, thermostat operation and circulation using the correct procedure.
💧 Losing coolant?
Inspect external leak points first, including hoses, clamps, radiator, reservoir, cap area and water-pump area. Internal loss requires further testing.
🌡️ Runs too cold?
Consider thermostat operation, fan behavior and temperature-sensor accuracy before replacing parts.
🫧 Pressure / bubbles?
Do not assume a head gasket. Check the system and cap correctly and use approved testing when combustion-gas intrusion is suspected.

Overheating comes down to heat transfer

The engine makes heat, coolant carries heat, and the radiator releases heat to moving air. A problem with coolant quantity or circulation, radiator airflow, fan operation, pressure control, or excessive engine heat can upset that process. Confirm which part of the heat-transfer path is failing before replacing components.

🧠 Simple rule: if coolant cannot carry the heat or air cannot remove the heat, engine temperature rises.
⚠️ Never open a hot pressurized cooling system. Hot coolant and steam can cause severe burns. Follow the manufacturer’s cooling-system service and pressure-testing procedures.
