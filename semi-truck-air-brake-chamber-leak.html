✅ PROVE IT

Air heard at a wheel position can come from the chamber, its hose or fitting, nearby plumbing, or another component. Sound gets you close. It does not identify the failed part.

🔓 Leak changes when parking brakes release

The system state is useful evidence. Record whether the leak begins, stops or becomes louder as the parking/spring brake is applied or released. That helps separate service-side and spring-brake-side behavior before parts are condemned.

💨 Leak is continuous

Safely verify the exact escape point. Inspect accessible external hoses, fittings and connections as well as the chamber area. Do not assume the chamber is bad simply because it is the nearest large component.

⚠️ Spring brake chamber danger

Do not open, cut, disassemble or improvise repairs on a spring brake chamber. It can contain powerful stored mechanical energy. Chamber service must follow the brake manufacturer's procedure and qualified heavy-duty repair practices.

Useful evidence to save

Record the wheel position, when the leak occurs, which air gauge changes, whether the low-air warning activates, whether the brake drags or gets hot, and whether recent brake or wheel-end work was performed.
