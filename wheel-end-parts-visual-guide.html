🛞 WHEEL END PARTS — SEE IT & NAME IT

Recognizable shapes from spindle to wheel and brake.

A hot wheel can come from brakes, bearings, lubrication, tires or installation problems. Identify the system and gather evidence before replacing parts. Wheel-end and brake work is safety-critical.

Wheel Hub

Look for: Large rotating center assembly carrying bearings, seal, wheel studs and lubricant.

Job: Supports wheel rotation on the spindle.

SEE WHEEL END →

Tapered Roller Bearing

Look for: Metal cone/race with tapered rollers in a cage.

Job: Carries radial and thrust loads while the hub rotates.

DIAGNOSE →

Wheel Seal

Look for: Large circular rubber/metal seal installed inside the inboard side of the hub.

Job: Retains wheel-end lubricant and helps keep contamination out.

DIAGNOSE LEAK →

Spindle

Look for: Heavy machined axle end the hub/bearings slide over, with threaded outer end.

Job: Supports the wheel-end bearing and hub assembly.

SEE WHEEL END →

Brake Drum

Look for: Large heavy round cast-iron drum surrounding brake shoes.

Job: Provides the friction surface the shoes press against.

DIAGNOSE HOT WHEEL →

Brake Shoes

Look for: Curved steel shoes with friction lining mounted inside the brake drum.

Job: Expand against the drum to create braking friction.

SEE AIR BRAKE PARTS →

ABS Tone Ring

Look for: Toothed/slotted ring rotating with the hub near the wheel-speed sensor.

Job: Provides the repeating target used to calculate wheel speed.

DIAGNOSE ABS →

ABS Wheel Speed Sensor

Look for: Small cylindrical sensor and cable positioned close to the tone ring.

Job: Sends wheel-speed information to ABS.

DIAGNOSE ABS →

Hubcap / Oil Cap

Look for: Outer cap on an oil-lubricated hub, often with a clear/inspection window and fill plug.

Job: Closes the hub and allows lubricant level inspection/service where designed.

SEE WHEEL END →

Axle Shaft / Drive Flange

Look for: On drive axles, shaft/flange running through hub into differential side gears.

Job: Transfers axle torque to the drive wheel hub.
