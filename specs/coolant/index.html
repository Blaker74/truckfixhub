Coolant Specs

Heavy-duty cooling-system guidance organized by engine and application.

Do not choose coolant by color alone. Verify engine/OEM coolant specification, formulation, concentration, compatibility and service procedure. Total chassis cooling capacity can vary substantially with radiator, heater, sleeper and plumbing configuration.
OEM-SPECIFIC

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017

Cummins specifies fully formulated antifreeze/coolant meeting the required heavy-duty formulation and references TMC RP 329 for ethylene-glycol coolant or TMC RP 330 for propylene-glycol coolant. Cummins guidance calls for maintaining the coolant mixture within the approved concentration range rather than identifying coolant by dye color.

Cummins X15 EPA 2017 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, Bulletin 5410641. Chassis cooling capacity is installation-dependent.
GENERATION MATTERS

Detroit DD13 / DD15 / DD16

Detroit heavy-duty engines use coolant meeting the applicable Detroit Fluids Specification for the engine generation. Current DD Platform operator literature distinguishes approved coolant chemistry and maintenance requirements; therefore TruckFixHub does not label every DD engine with one universal coolant product. Verify the engine generation and current Detroit approved-products information before mixing or changing coolant.

Detroit DD Platform operator manuals and Detroit Lubricants, Fuels & Coolants guidance.
LEGACY ENGINE

Detroit Series 60

Series 60 service literature requires a properly inhibited heavy-duty coolant and emphasizes maintaining freeze protection and supplemental coolant additive chemistry where applicable. Older conventional-coolant systems and later extended-life coolant programs must not be treated as automatically interchangeable.

Detroit Series 60 On-Highway Operator's Guide DDC-SVC-MAN-0039 and Detroit coolant guidance. Verify engine/application and coolant currently in service.
OEM-SPECIFIC

PACCAR MX-11 / MX-13

PACCAR MX operator literature specifies using PACCAR-approved coolant and warns against mixing incompatible coolant types. The correct concentration and approved chemistry are controlled by the applicable MX operator/service manual. Total system quantity remains chassis-dependent because radiator, heater and plumbing packages vary.

PACCAR MX-11 and MX-13 operator/service literature. Verify engine generation and manual revision.
OEM-SPECIFIC

Volvo D11 / D13 and Mack MP7 / MP8

Volvo and Mack heavy-duty engines require coolant meeting the applicable Volvo Group coolant specification for the engine generation. Current OEM guidance uses approved premixed/extended-life coolant programs and requires chemistry compatibility when topping off. Do not assume that two coolants are compatible because their colors look alike.

Volvo Trucks North America and Mack Trucks operator/body-builder maintenance literature. Verify engine generation and current approved coolant list.
APPLICATION-SPECIFIC

Caterpillar 3406E / C10 / C12 / C15 / C16

Caterpillar legacy on-highway literature distinguishes conventional heavy-duty coolant programs using supplemental coolant additive (SCA) maintenance from Caterpillar Extended Life Coolant (ELC) programs. These maintenance strategies are not interchangeable without following Caterpillar conversion/service procedures.

Caterpillar Operation and Maintenance literature SEBU7186 and related Caterpillar coolant/ELC guidance. Verify engine serial prefix and existing coolant chemistry.
SERVICE RULE

Coolant Concentration & Top-Off

A coolant's visible color is not its specification. Before topping off, identify what is already in the system, verify the OEM-approved chemistry and concentration, and use compatible premix or concentrate/water proportions as directed. Mixing incompatible formulations can reduce corrosion protection and shorten service life.
