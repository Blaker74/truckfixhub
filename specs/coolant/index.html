Coolant Specs

Heavy-duty cooling-system guidance organized by engine and application.

Do not choose coolant by color alone. Verify engine/OEM coolant specification, formulation, concentration, compatibility and service procedure. Total chassis cooling capacity can vary substantially with radiator, heater, sleeper and plumbing configuration.

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017

Cummins specifies fully formulated antifreeze/coolant with the required additive package and references TMC RP 329 for ethylene-glycol coolant or TMC RP 330 for propylene-glycol coolant.

Cummins X15 EPA 2017 Truck Maintenance and Operation Quick Reference Guide, Bulletin 5410641. Chassis cooling capacity is installation-dependent.

Detroit DD Platform

Detroit coolant requirements are generation-specific and must be matched to the current Detroit Fluids Specification and engine service literature. TruckFixHub will index exact coolant specification and service interval by DD13/DD15/DD16 generation rather than assigning one universal coolant to every DD engine.

OEM verification queue: Detroit Lubricants, Fuels & Coolants / DD Platform operator literature.

PACCAR MX

PACCAR MX cooling specifications are being indexed by engine generation and operator-manual revision. Cooling-system capacity is vehicle-installation dependent, so a universal MX-11/MX-13 truck capacity will not be published.

OEM verification queue: PACCAR MX operator/service literature.

Volvo / Mack

Volvo D-series and Mack MP-series coolant entries will be separated by engine generation and OEM coolant specification. Do not substitute coolant solely because another product has the same visible color.

OEM verification queue: Volvo Trucks and Mack service literature.

Caterpillar / Legacy Engines

Caterpillar and legacy engine coolant specifications will be indexed by engine family and serial-prefix/application where the OEM literature requires it. SCA/ELC requirements must be verified before service.

OEM verification queue: Caterpillar operation and maintenance literature.
TruckFixHub standard: no guessed capacities and no color-based coolant matching. Exact verified specifications are added only when supported by manufacturer/service literature.
