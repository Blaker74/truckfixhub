Engine Oil Specs

Class 8 diesel engines — exact component first.

Before filling: Verify engine model, serial number, model year/emissions generation, oil-pan/sump and filter configuration. Published quantities are starting specifications; the correct dipstick level and current OEM procedure control the final fill.

Detroit DD13 — Gen 5

Service fill oil + filter: 40 qt (38.0 L). Pan high: 37 qt. Pan low: 32 qt.

Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0217.

Detroit DD15 — Gen 5

Service fill oil + filter: 45 qt (43.0 L). Pan high: 42 qt. Pan low: 37 qt.

Detroit DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0217.

Detroit DD16 — GHG17

Service fill oil + filter: 45 qt (43.0 L). Pan high: 42 qt. Pan low: 37 qt.

Detroit DD Heavy Duty Platform Operator Manual DDC-SVC-MAN-0189.

Detroit Series 60 — On-Highway

LOW mark without filters: 26 qt. FULL mark without filters: 32 qt. Total at FULL with filters and galleries charged: 40 qt.

Detroit Series 60 On-Highway Operator's Guide DDC-SVC-MAN-0039.

Cummins X15 — EPA 2017

15W-40 or 10W-30 meeting Cummins CES 20086 / API CK-4. Stamped-steel pan oil-change fill: 43.5 L / 11.5 gal. Aluminum wedge pan: 45.4 L / 12 gal. Aluminum rear-center sump: 41.6 L / 11 gal. Standard filter: 2.2 L.

Cummins X15 EPA 2017 Quick Reference Guide, Bulletin 5410641.

PACCAR MX-13

Published total system capacity, oil pan plus new filters: 42 U.S. qt (40.0 L).

PACCAR MX-13 Operator's Manual Y53-1100-1F1. Confirm generation/manual revision.

Mack MP7 / MP8

Published engine oil capacity: 44 qt (42 L). MP7 steel-pan oil-change volume: 38 qt (36 L). MP8 service quantity depends on filter configuration.

Mack Trucks Heavy-Duty Body Builder, Section 1 — Oil and Filters.

Mack MP10

Published engine oil capacity: 55 qt (52 L).

Mack Trucks Heavy-Duty Body Builder, Section 1 — Oil and Filters.

Volvo D13 — EPA 2027

Volvo VDS-5.0, SAE 5W-30. Published total lube capacity: 35 qt (33 L).

Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications.

Caterpillar C15 — On-Highway

Deep-standard sump approximate refill including standard filters: 40 qt (38 L). Optional CAT bypass filter adds approximately 2.6 qt (2.5 L).

Caterpillar Operation and Maintenance literature SEBU7695. Confirm serial prefix and sump.

Caterpillar C11 / C13 — On-Highway

Deep-standard sump: 42 qt (40 L). Shallow sump: 36 qt (34 L). Includes standard factory-installed filters.

Caterpillar Operation and Maintenance literature SEBU7695. Confirm sump and serial prefix.
More engines are being verified: Cummins ISX/ISM/N14, CAT 3406E/C12 and additional C15 families, PACCAR MX-11 and older MX generations, International/Navistar, older Mack/Volvo and additional Detroit generations. TruckFixHub will not publish a guessed capacity merely to fill a blank.
