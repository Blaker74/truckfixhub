Drive Axle Lubricant Specs

Differentials and tandem drive axles — exact axle model first.

Critical rule: Axle housing, model, installation angle and configuration affect lubricant quantity. Do not use one generic “Freightliner,” “Peterbilt” or “Kenworth” rear-end capacity. Fill level and current axle-manufacturer procedure are the final reference.

Dana / Spicer Commercial Vehicle Drive Axles

Identify the axle from its tag before choosing lubricant or quantity. Dana specifies filling the axle to the proper level at the filler hole. Published capacities are approximate and change with axle angle and housing configuration.

Dana Spicer Drive Axles Service Manual AXSM0057 and Dana commercial vehicle lubrication guidance.

Dana AdvanTEK 40 — D40-155 at 4° Installation Angle

Published approximate lubricant quantities for the D40-155 tandem family at a 4° installation angle:

Forward axle
27.8 pt
13.2 L
Rear axle
23.2 pt
11.0 L

Use an approved Dana lubricant and fill to the bottom of the filler hole after the axle is in its normal installed position.

Dana Spicer AdvanTEK 40 service/lubrication information. Capacity changes with installation angle.

Dana AdvanTEK 40 — D40-156 at 4° Installation Angle

Published approximate lubricant quantities for the D40-156 tandem family at a 4° installation angle:

Forward axle
27.8 pt
13.2 L
Rear axle
20.0 pt
9.5 L

The D40-156 rear quantity differs from the D40-155 rear axle, which is exactly why TruckFixHub does not publish one generic “AdvanTEK 40” capacity.

Dana Spicer AdvanTEK 40 service/lubrication information. Verify axle tag and installation angle.

Dana Fill-Level Procedure

Approximate quantity gets the lubricant into the housing; the bottom of the filler hole establishes the final service level unless the exact Dana procedure for the identified axle specifies otherwise. Recheck after servicing and inspect for leaks.

Dana Spicer commercial vehicle drive-axle service guidance.

Meritor / Rockwell

Model-specific entries are being built for common Class 8 single and tandem drive axles. Exact model identification is required before a lubricant quantity is published.

Detroit / Mack / Volvo / PACCAR

OEM-branded and integrated axle families are being separated by model and configuration. TruckFixHub will display lubricant specification, approximate service quantity when substantiated, fill-level procedure and source.

Why we are strict here: Rear axle overfill and underfill can both create failures. A “close enough” capacity copied from another housing is not TruckFixHub-quality information.
