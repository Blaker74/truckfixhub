Drive Axle Lubricant Specs

Differentials and tandem drive axles — exact axle model first.

Critical rule: Axle housing, model, installation angle and configuration affect lubricant quantity. Do not use one generic “Freightliner,” “Peterbilt” or “Kenworth” rear-end capacity. Fill level and current axle-manufacturer procedure are the final reference.
Before using a capacity, read the axle tag.
  • Manufacturer — Dana/Spicer, Meritor or other OEM.
  • Exact axle model — similar-looking housings can have different capacities.
  • Forward vs rear tandem axle — the two housings frequently do not hold the same amount.
  • Installation/pinion angle — published guide quantities can change with installed angle.
  • Options and configuration — pumps, filters, coolers and special housings can change service requirements.

Dana / Spicer Commercial Vehicle Drive Axles

Identify the axle from its tag before choosing lubricant or quantity. Dana specifies filling the axle to the proper level at the filler hole. Published capacities are approximate and change with axle angle and housing configuration.

Dana Spicer Drive Axles Service Manual AXSM0057 and Dana commercial vehicle lubrication guidance.

Dana Fill-Level Procedure

Approximate quantity gets the lubricant into the housing; the bottom of the filler hole establishes the final service level unless the exact Dana procedure for the identified axle specifies otherwise. Recheck after servicing and inspect for leaks.

Dana Spicer commercial vehicle drive-axle service guidance.

Meritor MT-40-143MA-N Tandem

Meritor publishes the following guide capacities with the drive pinion horizontal:

Forward axle
30.2 pt
14.3 L
Rear axle
25.8 pt
12.2 L

These are guide quantities only. Meritor specifically states that changing the drive-pinion angle changes axle lubricant capacity.

Meritor Maintenance Manual MM-0250, Section 6 Lubrication, Table G.

Meritor MT-40-14X Family — Verify Exact Suffix

The MT-40-14X designation covers a family with options and variations. Meritor documentation lists MT-40-14X/P, MT-40-143DA-N, MT-40-143MA-N and MT-40-144/P among tandem applications. Do not transfer the MT-40-143MA-N capacity above to another MT-40-14X-family axle unless its own service table confirms it.

Meritor published axle-family/warranty documentation and model-specific maintenance literature.

Meritor Drive Axle Lubricant

Meritor Maintenance Manual MM-1 specifies multigrade hypoid gear oil meeting API GL-5 requirements for drive axles, with viscosity selected for the expected ambient-temperature range. Meritor warns against reducing lubricant viscosity with thinning agents.

Meritor Maintenance Manual MM-1, Lubrication.

Meritor Fill-Level Procedure

With the vehicle on a level surface, add the specified axle lubricant through the fill opening until the lubricant reaches the bottom of the fill-plug hole. Meritor notes that pinion angle changes capacity. After service, road-test as directed by the applicable axle manual and recheck lubricant level and leaks.

Meritor drive axle maintenance literature.
