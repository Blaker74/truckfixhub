Drive Axle Lubricant Specs

Differentials and tandem drive axles — exact axle model first.

Critical rule: Axle housing, model, installation angle and configuration affect lubricant quantity. Do not use one generic “Freightliner,” “Peterbilt” or “Kenworth” rear-end capacity. Fill level and current axle-manufacturer procedure are the final reference.

Dana / Spicer Commercial Vehicle Drive Axles

TruckFixHub indexes these by the identification tag and exact axle model rather than chassis badge. Dana service literature instructs technicians to use the specified lubricant and establish the proper level at the axle fill opening; published quantities can vary with installation angle and housing.

Manufacturer basis: Dana/Spicer commercial vehicle drive-axle lubrication/service literature.

Dana AdvanTEK 40 — D40-155 / D40-156 family

Indexed as a separate axle family because housing and installation details matter. Verify the axle tag, approved lubricant and fill-level procedure before service rather than relying on a universal tandem quantity.

Manufacturer basis: Dana Spicer AdvanTEK 40 service/lubrication information.

Meritor / Rockwell

Model-specific entries are being built for common Class 8 single and tandem drive axles. Exact model identification is required before a lubricant quantity is published.

Detroit / Mack / Volvo / PACCAR

OEM-branded and integrated axle families are being separated by model and configuration. TruckFixHub will display lubricant specification, approximate service quantity when substantiated, fill-level procedure and source.

Why we are strict here: Rear axle overfill and underfill can both create failures. A “close enough” capacity copied from another housing is not TruckFixHub-quality information.
