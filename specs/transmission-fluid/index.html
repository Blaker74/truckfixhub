Transmission Fluid Specs

Identify the transmission model before choosing lubricant or quantity.

Do not fill by truck model alone. Transmission family, exact model, cooler/PTO arrangement, installation angle and auxiliary equipment can change service quantity and lubricant requirements. Follow the current OEM level-check procedure for final fill.

Eaton Cummins Endurant HD

Recommended lubricant: Eaton PS-386. Nominal oil capacity: 16 pints (7.57 L). Actual fill can vary with installation angle.

Eaton Cummins Endurant HD official service/product literature.

Eaton Cummins Endurant XD / XD Pro

Recommended lubricant: Eaton PS-386. Published nominal capacity: 25.4 pints (12 L). Published lubricant service guidance includes on-highway 500,000 miles/5 years and vocational 250,000 miles/3 years, subject to current Eaton requirements.

Eaton Cummins Endurant XD/XD Pro manufacturer literature.

Eaton Fuller — Common 9-Speed Models

Published approximate capacities vary by exact model. Examples: 9509/11509/12509 25 pints (12 L); 11609/12609/13609/14609 and 11709/12709/13709/14709/16709 27 pints (13 L). PTOs and oil coolers increase capacity.

Eaton Fuller Transmission Oil Capacities / Super Parts Book TRIP0916. Fill hole is the final level reference.

Eaton Fuller — Common 10-Speed Models

11610/12610/14610 and 11710/12710/13710/14710/15710/16710: 26 pints (12 L). RTLO-11610/12610/13610/14610/15610/16610: 28 pints (13 L). Exact model matters.

Eaton Fuller Transmission Oil Capacities / Super Parts Book TRIP0916.

Eaton Fuller RTLO — Common 13-Speed Models

RTLO-14613, RTLO-12713, RTLO-14713 and RTLO-16713: approximate capacity 28 pints (13 L). Other Fuller 13-speed models can differ.

Eaton Fuller Transmission Oil Capacities / Super Parts Book TRIP0916.

Eaton Fuller — Common 15-Speed Models

915, 12515 and 11615: approximately 28 pints (13 L). 14615 and 15615: approximately 30 pints (14 L). 11715/14715/15715: approximately 28 pints (13 L).

Eaton Fuller Transmission Oil Capacities / Super Parts Book TRIP0916.

Eaton Fuller RTLO — Common 18-Speed Models

RTLO-14618, RTLO-16618, RTLO-14718, RTLO-16718 and RTLO-18718: approximate capacity 28 pints (13 L). Eaton states these are approximate; the fill hole is the final reference.

Eaton Fuller Transmission Oil Capacities / Super Parts Book TRIP0916.

Eaton Heavy-Duty Lubricant Guidance

PS-386 synthetic transmission fluid is Eaton-approved for UltraShift PLUS, Fuller Advantage, FR and RT Series transmissions. Eaton's current heavy-duty synthetic guidance lists 500,000 miles/5 years linehaul and 180,000 miles/3 years vocational for applicable mechanical and automated transmissions.

Eaton Transmission and Clutch Lubrication Specifications and PS-386 Technical Data Sheet TCSL0022.

Detroit DT12 — MY 2020 C-Box DC/OC

Approximate refill capacity: 8.9 qt (8.4 L). Detroit specifies filling until lubricant is level with the bottom of the fill hole with the vehicle in normal operating position.

Detroit Transmission Fluid Maintenance Information DDC-SVC-BRO-0118, Table 10.

Detroit DT12 — MY 2017 B-Box DB/OB

Approximate refill capacity: 8.5 qt (8.0 L). MY 2016-and-older B-Box refill capacity is approximately 11.6 qt (11.0 L).

Detroit DDC-SVC-BRO-0118, Table 10. Final level is bottom of fill hole.

Detroit DT12 — A-Box DA/OA

MY 2017 approximate refill: 12.7 qt (12.0 L). MY 2016-and-older approximate refill: 15.9 qt (15.0 L). Detroit lists approved 75W-85 MB 235.16 products for MY 2017+ and approved 75W-90 MB 235.11 products for all model years.

Detroit DDC-SVC-BRO-0118 and Detroit approved transmission-oil information.

PACCAR TX-12 / TX-12 PRO

Published transmission oil capacity: 16 pints. Identify the exact TX-12 variant and follow the current PACCAR operator/service procedure for lubricant specification and final level.

PACCAR Powertrain TX-12 official product specifications.

PACCAR TX-18 / TX-18 PRO

Published lubricant capacity: 25.4 pints (12 L). PACCAR approves PACCAR Genuine PS-386 / Eaton-approved PS-386. The published capacity does not cover every cooler, PTO or hose arrangement; use the transmission check hole as the final reference.

PACCAR TX-18 Operator's Manual Y53-1349-1A1 and 2025 TX-18 official specification sheet.

Mack mDRIVE — TmD12

Published lubrication capacity including cooler: 17.9 qt. Mack directs technicians to approved-oils list SB 175-61 and uses the transmission sight glass for final level.

Mack Body Builder Instructions, Transmission and Oil/Filters.

Mack mDRIVE HD — TmD13 / TmD14

Published lubrication capacity including cooler: 20.5 qt. Approved oil depends on installation; radiator-mounted oil-cooler applications require the specified lower-viscosity lubricant.

Mack Body Builder Instructions, Transmission and Oil/Filters.

Volvo I-Shift — AT2612 / ATO2612

Current published service fill: 16 L (16.9 qt). Additional oil is required for certain PTO and oil-cooler installations. Use only Volvo-approved synthetic transmission oil.

Volvo Trucks North America Body Builder Instructions — I-Shift Transmission, USA191997144.

Volvo I-Shift — EPA 2027 published configurations

Approximate published oil capacities: 18 qt (17 L) or 20.8 qt (19.68 L), depending on transmission configuration.

Volvo Trucks North America EPA 2027 Powertrain specifications.

Allison 3000 Series — Mack Published Installations

Capacity excluding external circuits with PTO: 26.4 qt (25 L) shallow sump or 29.5 qt (28 L) deep sump. These values are installation-specific, not universal.

Mack Body Builder Instructions. Verify exact Allison model, sump, PTO and external circuits.

Allison 4500 RDS — Mack Published Installations

Capacity excluding external circuits with shallow sump: 47.5 qt (45 L) with PTO or 40 qt (38 L) without PTO.

Mack Body Builder Instructions. Verify exact model and installation.

Allison 3000 / 4000 Series — Current Fluid Guidance

Allison recommends TES 668 approved fluid for 3000 and 4000 Series on-highway products. TES 295 and TES 468 approved fluids remain approved, with Allison recommending consideration of TES 668 at the next service. Exact refill quantity is configuration-dependent.

Allison Transmission official Approved Fluids guidance.
