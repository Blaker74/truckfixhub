👇 Follow the evidence

🎵 WHINE / HOWL: Write down acceleration, coast, steady throttle and turning behavior. Verify lubricant, then separate tires, wheel ends and driveline before internal gearset diagnosis.
🔨 CLUNK: Inspect U-joints, yokes, slip splines, mounts and axle-location components. Total driveline lash can stack across multiple parts.
💧 PINION LEAK: Confirm source, level/specification, venting, yoke sealing surface and input/bearing condition. A new seal does not repair a moving shaft or damaged sealing surface.
🚫 NO DRIVE: Follow torque from transmission output through driveshaft, axle input, differential/power divider, axle shafts and hubs. Find the first point where torque stops.
🚛 TANDEM / POWER DIVIDER: Identify the exact tandem configuration. Separate switch/air/electrical command from actuator engagement and internal torque transfer.

🧠 Noise map

UNDER PULL: Record noise while torque loads the drive side of the system.
ON COAST: Torque reversal can change gear/bearing noise and driveline lash.
STEADY SPEED: Helps distinguish load-sensitive noise from road-speed rotating noise.
TURNING: Changing wheel speed/load can expose tire, wheel-end or differential behavior.
