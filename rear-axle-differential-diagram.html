⚙️ REAR AXLE & DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSTICS

Before opening the differential, prove the complaint is actually inside the axle.

1️⃣ DRIVESHAFT
Delivers torque to the drive axle
↓
2️⃣ PINION / INPUT
Transfers torque into the axle gearset
↓
3️⃣ RING GEAR / DIFFERENTIAL
Changes torque direction and distributes it
↓
4️⃣ AXLE SHAFTS
Carry torque outward
↓
5️⃣ HUBS / DRIVE TIRES
Transfer axle torque to the road

👇 Follow the condition

🎵 WHINE / HOWL: Record whether it occurs on acceleration, coast, steady throttle or turning. Verify lubricant, then separate tires, wheel ends and driveline from the axle before internal diagnosis.
🔨 CLUNK: Inspect U-joints, yokes, splines and upstream driveline movement first. Driveline lash can add together; do not assume a takeoff clunk means ring-and-pinion failure.
💧 GEAR-OIL LEAK: Trace the true source. Check lubricant level, venting and relevant bearing/input condition. A visible pinion seal leak does not explain why it began leaking.
🚫 NO DRIVE: Follow torque. Determine whether it reaches the driveshaft, axle input, differential and axle shafts. Find the first point where torque stops before major disassembly.
🚛 TANDEM DRIVE: Identify the exact axle configuration. Power-divider and inter-axle components create additional torque paths and can change no-drive and noise diagnosis.

🧠 The four conditions worth writing down

1️⃣ ACCELERATION: Does the noise appear under pull?
2️⃣ COAST: Does it change when torque reverses direction?
3️⃣ STEADY SPEED: Does it remain with stable load?
4️⃣ TURNING: Does changing wheel speed/load alter the complaint?
