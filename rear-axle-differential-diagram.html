⚙️ REAR AXLE & DIFFERENTIAL

Follow the power from the driveshaft to the drive tires.

1️⃣ DRIVESHAFT
Brings power to the axle
↓
2️⃣ INPUT / PINION
Turns driveshaft rotation into the gearset
↓
3️⃣ RING GEAR + DIFFERENTIAL
Changes direction and divides torque
↓
4️⃣ AXLE SHAFTS
Carry torque outward
↓
5️⃣ HUBS → DRIVE TIRES
Put the torque onto the road
🎵 Whine / howl?
Note whether it happens on acceleration, coast or both. Check lubricant level/condition and isolate road-speed-related noise.
🔨 Clunk?
Check driveline free play, U-joints, yokes, axle shafts and differential-related movement.
💧 Gear oil leak?
Find the exact source: input/pinion area, housing, cover, axle ends or venting concerns.
🚫 No drive at a wheel/axle?
Work inward from the wheel: axle shaft, differential, input and upstream driveline.
🧠 Simple rule: ACCELERATION, COAST and ROAD SPEED are clues. Write down exactly when the noise or vibration happens.
⚠️ Secure and support the vehicle correctly before inspecting underneath it. Do not work around a rotating driveline.
