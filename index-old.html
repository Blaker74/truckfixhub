Start with your truck and symptoms

These are starter topic guides, not member posts. Search to find what information to gather for a useful troubleshooting conversation.

Engine & starting

Explain what happens at startup

Gather the model year, engine model, whether the engine cranks, dash messages, and any exact fault codes.

Electrical

Describe the electrical symptoms

Record which systems are affected, when the problem occurs, battery age, and recent repairs or accessory changes.

Aftertreatment

Keep the exact warning messages

Record DEF or DPF warnings, fault codes, engine model, and when a derate or regeneration message first appeared.

Air & brakes

Document the air system concern

Note dash warnings, the situation in which they appear, and recent brake or air system service. Use qualified help for safety-critical faults.

Drivetrain

Describe the driving conditions

Include the transmission model, load conditions, affected gears, warning messages, and when a shift or vibration issue happens.

Parts identification

Identify the part accurately

Gather the truck and engine model, existing part number, and clear photos. Keep personal details out of public photos and posts.
