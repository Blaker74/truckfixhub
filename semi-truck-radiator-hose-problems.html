Radiator Hose Leaking, Swollen or Collapsing?

Hose condition can reveal a leak or restriction, but determine why the hose failed instead of treating every hose symptom as an isolated part problem.

Cooling systems can remain pressurized

Inspect only when safe and follow OEM procedures. Never remove a hot pressure cap.

What to look for

  • Wetness or dried coolant near hose ends
  • Chafing or contact with surrounding components
  • Bulging, cracking or unusual softness
  • Collapse under certain operating conditions
  • Clamp or connection issues

A collapsed or repeatedly damaged hose can justify checking the broader cooling system for restriction, pressure or routing problems.
