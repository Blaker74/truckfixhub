🚚 TRAILER

When a trailer acts up, put the problem into one of four boxes.

💨 AIR
Supply, service lines, valves, tanks and chambers
💡 ELECTRICAL
Connector, power, wiring, grounds and lights
🛞 RUNNING GEAR
Wheel ends, tires, suspension and axles
🔒 COUPLING / BODY
Kingpin, landing gear, doors and structure
🛑 Trailer brakes not working right?
Start with tractor-to-trailer air supply/service connections, pressure, leaks and trailer brake components.
💡 Trailer lights dead or strange?
Check the tractor/trailer connector first, then shared power and ground before chasing individual lamps.
↔️ Trailer pulls / dog-tracks / wears tires?
Inspect tire pressure/condition, suspension, axle alignment, wheel ends and structural concerns.
⬆️ Landing gear hard to crank?
Check load, alignment, lubrication, crank/gear condition and mounting before forcing it.
🧠 Simple rule: decide whether the problem is AIR, ELECTRICAL, RUNNING GEAR or COUPLING/BODY. Then diagnose only that section.
⚠️ Chock and secure the trailer before inspection. Never work beneath an unsupported trailer or rely on air suspension alone.
