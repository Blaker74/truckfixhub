🎲 STARTER WORKS INTERMITTENTLY

Intermittent no-crank complaints are easiest to solve when the failure is captured before disturbing connections or cycling controls repeatedly.

1️⃣ DEFINE THE TRIGGER: Hot only? Cold only? After rain? After a bump? Certain shifter position? Long drive? Record the pattern.
2️⃣ WHEN IT FAILS, TEST COMMAND: Determine whether the starter command reaches the appropriate relay/solenoid path while the complaint is present.
3️⃣ WATCH VOLTAGE: Capture battery and circuit voltage during the failed attempt. A connection may pass a light load and fail at starter current.
4️⃣ INTERLOCKS / RELAYS: Use wiring information and scan data where applicable to verify neutral/clutch/start-permission logic rather than bypassing it.
5️⃣ HEAT / VIBRATION: Inspect connections, harnesses and starter area for heat damage, looseness, corrosion or movement-sensitive faults.
6️⃣ VERIFY: Repeat enough hot/cold cycles to prove the intermittent condition is gone.
TRIGGER → CAPTURE FAILURE → COMMAND → LOADED VOLTAGE → INTERLOCK / RELAY → STARTER → REPEAT-CYCLE VERIFY.
⚠️ Do not bypass safety interlocks during diagnosis. Secure the vehicle against unintended movement.
