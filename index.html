Independent, experience-built heavy-duty resource

TruckFixHub was founded by Blake Pierce after more than 30 years around heavy-duty truck parts and logistics. The public library is organized to help people move from a symptom to a system, from a system to a test, and from a test to a better next step.

TruckFixHub is independent of the manufacturers referenced in educational material. We separate troubleshooting guidance from confirmed facts, encourage vehicle-specific verification, and do not treat parts swapping as diagnosis.
