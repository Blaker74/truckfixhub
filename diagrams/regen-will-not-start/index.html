Parked Regen Will Not Start: Visual Diagnostic Path

A regeneration request can be refused because the ECM does not see the required conditions. Before forcing a regen or replacing aftertreatment parts, identify which prerequisite or fault is blocking the request.

park · brake · switches · engine state
Active faults

DPF evidence

Temperature system

Perform the approved regeneration procedureMonitor temperatures, pressure and fault status; stop if the manufacturer procedure indicates an unsafe or abnormal condition.
↓
VERIFY THE FIXConfirm the regen completes when appropriate, DPF pressure/load responds normally, warning/derate status clears as designed and no blocking faults return.
Key visual rule: “Regen will not start” often means the ECM is intentionally refusing the request. Find the missing prerequisite or blocking fault before trying to force regeneration.
Safety: Regeneration creates extremely high exhaust temperatures. Follow OEM procedures, keep combustible materials away from the exhaust and never perform a parked regeneration in an unsafe location.
