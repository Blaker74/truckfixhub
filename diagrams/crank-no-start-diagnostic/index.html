Crank No Start: Visual Diagnostic Path

If the engine cranks but will not start, prove the basics in order. A diesel needs adequate cranking speed, ECM power and inputs, a valid engine-speed/position signal, correct fuel supply and the required injection pressure.

START — Engine cranks but will not startRecord all active/inactive codes, recent repairs, fuel history, shutdown event and whether the failure was sudden or gradual.
↓
1. Is cranking speed strong and system voltage stable?Use OEM specifications. Slow cranking or voltage collapse can prevent starting even though the starter turns the engine.
↓
NO
Correct the cranking/voltage problem firstTest batteries, cables, grounds, starter circuit and mechanical drag before chasing fuel or sensors.
YES
Confirm ECM operation and engine dataMove to power, communications and speed/position evidence.
↓
2. Does the ECM show valid engine RPM while cranking?
↓
NO
Diagnose speed/position inputFollow OEM tests for crank/cam sensors, wiring, connectors, tone/trigger components and ECM power/ground as applicable.
YES
Move to fuel and start-enable evidenceDo not replace speed sensors when the ECM already sees a valid cranking signal.
↓
3. Is low-pressure fuel supply reaching the engine correctly?Check actual fuel level/quality and use the manufacturer procedure for restriction, air intrusion, priming and supply pressure.
↓
NO
Repair the supply-side faultCorrect the proven restriction, air leak, empty/incorrect fuel condition, filter, line or supply-system failure.
YES
Check required injection/rail pressureCompare commanded and actual data using OEM specifications and test conditions.
↓
4. Does injection pressure reach the minimum required to start?
↓
NO
Diagnose why pressure cannot buildFollow the engine-specific path for pumping, leakage, control valves/regulators, injectors and pressure sensing. Do not guess from one symptom.
YES
Check start enable / shutdown causesReview immobilizer/security where equipped, shutdown inputs, engine protection, timing/synchronization and companion faults using OEM diagnostics.
↓
5. Has a specific failure been proven?
↓
NO
Return to the evidenceCompare what is missing during cranking: voltage, RPM/sync, fuel supply, injection pressure or start enable. Do not shotgun parts.
YES
Repair the proven causeCorrect only the failure supported by testing, then clear/resolve faults as the OEM procedure requires.
↓
VERIFY THE FIXConfirm normal starting, stable cranking voltage, correct engine data and no returning active faults under the original operating condition.
Key visual rule: “Cranks but will not start” is not automatically a fuel-pump problem. First identify which required condition is missing, then test that system.
