🧠 THE RULE

Overheating is a symptom—not a diagnosis. A code, hot gauge, hard hose, white exhaust or coolant loss is a clue. Follow the branch, test it, and make the evidence prove the cause.

Exact fan logic, pressure limits, coolant-fill procedures, thermostat specifications and test procedures vary by engine and chassis. Verify exact application information.

↓
SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

After repair, recreate the original idle/load/ambient condition and verify stable temperature, correct fan behavior, stable coolant level and no related faults.

🛑 Safety

Never remove a hot pressurized cooling-system cap. Keep clear of fans and rotating components; electronically controlled fans can engage unexpectedly.
