SPN 4364: Visual Diagnostic Path

Low SCR conversion efficiency does not automatically condemn the SCR catalyst. Prove the inputs and operating conditions that the catalyst depends on before replacing expensive aftertreatment parts.

Key visual rule: SPN 4364 describes an SCR-efficiency problem. It does not by itself prove the SCR catalyst is bad. Verify upstream faults, DEF delivery, exhaust integrity and NOx feedback first.
