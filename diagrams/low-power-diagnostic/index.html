Low Power / Derate: Visual Diagnostic Path

Low power is a symptom, not a diagnosis. First determine whether the ECM is intentionally limiting torque or the engine simply cannot make the commanded power.

Derate logic
active faults · protection · aftertreatment
Air & boost
filter · CAC · turbo · leaks
Fuel
restriction · supply · pressure · quality
Exhaust & engine
restriction · EGR · mechanical
START — Truck has low powerRecord all codes, warning/derate messages, when the complaint occurs, recent repairs and whether the problem appeared suddenly or gradually.
↓
1. Is an active derate or engine-protection condition present?
↓
YES
Diagnose the reason for the commanded derateUse the complete fault set and OEM logic. Aftertreatment, temperature, pressure or protection faults may intentionally limit torque.
NO
Treat it as a performance complaintCompare commanded versus actual engine data under the condition where power is missing.
↓
2. Is air/boost performance correct?Inspect the air path and compare boost-related commanded/actual data using OEM specifications.
↓
NO
Find the air-side causeCheck intake restriction, charge-air leaks, clamps/boots, CAC, turbo control and related sensors. Repair what testing proves.
YES
Move to fuel deliveryDo not condemn the turbo when boost evidence is normal.
↓
3. Can the fuel system maintain required supply/injection pressure under load?
↓
NO
Diagnose fuel-side restriction or pressure lossUse OEM tests for filters, restriction, air intrusion, supply system, commanded/actual pressure and control components.
YES
Check exhaust/aftertreatment and EGR evidenceLook for restriction, regeneration history, differential pressure, EGR behavior and companion faults.
↓
4. Are air, fuel and exhaust systems performing correctly?
↓
NO
Repair the proven system faultCorrect the restriction, leak, control, sensor or delivery problem supported by testing.
YES
Move toward engine/mechanical causesFollow OEM procedures for injector contribution, valve train, compression and other engine-specific mechanical checks.
↓
5. Has the cause been proven?
↓
NO
Recreate the complaint with dataCompare requested torque/boost/fuel values with actual values. Avoid replacing expensive parts because they are merely possible causes.
YES
Repair the proven causeCorrect only the failure supported by evidence and follow manufacturer reset/learn procedures where required.
↓
VERIFY THE FIXRoad-test or load-test under the original complaint conditions and confirm expected power with no returning active faults or derate.
Key visual rule: Separate commanded low power from inability to make power. That single decision prevents a lot of unnecessary turbo, injector and aftertreatment parts replacement.
