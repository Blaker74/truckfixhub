Low power is a symptom. It does not automatically mean bad turbo, bad injectors or plugged aftertreatment. Follow the clue to the system, then prove the cause.

🛑 SAFETY: Stop safely for a red stop-engine warning, severe knocking, uncontrolled engine speed, severe overheating, rapid oil or coolant loss, or another manufacturer-directed shutdown condition. Verify exact specifications and test procedures for the exact engine and vehicle.
