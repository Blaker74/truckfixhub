SPN 3226 identifies an outlet NOx-related measurement. Separate a sensor/circuit problem from exhaust, DEF dosing or SCR-system performance before replacing parts.

Key visual rule: The outlet NOx sensor reports what happened after the SCR system. A bad reading can come from the sensor/circuit, but it can also reflect a real upstream aftertreatment problem. Prove which one before replacing the sensor.
