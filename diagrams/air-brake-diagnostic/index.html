🔎 Deeper clues

SEE IT → NAME IT → TEST IT → PROVE IT.

The symptom chooses the next test. It does not choose the replacement part.

🛑 SAFETY: Air brakes, spring brakes and wheel-end heat are safety-critical. Do not operate a vehicle that cannot maintain safe air pressure or has unresolved braking problems. Chock and secure the vehicle before inspection. Never disassemble a spring brake chamber. Verify exact procedures and specifications for the vehicle and components.
