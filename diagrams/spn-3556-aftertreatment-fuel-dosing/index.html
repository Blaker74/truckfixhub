SPN 3556 — Aftertreatment Fuel Dosing

Use the exact FMI and OEM definition first. Aftertreatment fuel-dosing faults can involve supply, leakage/restriction, electrical control, the dosing device or a system condition that prevents commanded heat generation. Prove the failed function before replacing parts.

Key visual rule: A fuel-dosing code identifies a dosing-system problem, not automatically a failed doser. Prove supply, circuit, commanded delivery and temperature response in order.
Safety / OEM information: Fuel and aftertreatment service involve fire hazards and very high exhaust temperatures. Follow OEM depressurization, leak-testing, regeneration and service procedures. Exact SPN 3556 definitions and component layouts vary by engine and calibration.
