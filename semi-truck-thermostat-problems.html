Semi Truck Thermostat Problems

A thermostat can affect warm-up and coolant-temperature control, but temperature symptoms should be diagnosed as a complete cooling-system problem.

Overheating requires attention

Follow the manufacturer's shutdown guidance for high coolant temperature. Never open a hot pressurized cooling system.

Patterns worth recording

  • Engine runs hot under load but cools at light load
  • Engine takes unusually long to warm up
  • Coolant temperature stays lower than expected
  • Temperature rises or falls unexpectedly
  • Cab heat changes with engine temperature

Fan control, coolant level, radiator airflow, sensors, water pump performance and other faults can overlap these symptoms.
