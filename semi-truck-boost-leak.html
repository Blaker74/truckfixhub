💨 BOOST / CHARGE-AIR LEAK

A charge-air leak can cause low power, low boost, smoke or a hiss under load. Find the leak before condemning the turbo.

1️⃣ DEFINE THE COMPLAINT: Does it happen only under load? Note boost data, smoke, noise and related faults.
2️⃣ VISUAL PATH: Inspect compressor outlet through charge-air cooler to intake manifold. Look for split boots, loose/misaligned clamps, rubbed pipes, oil tracks and damaged cooler connections.
3️⃣ PRESSURE / LEAK TEST: Use the engine or vehicle manufacturer’s approved charge-air test method and pressure limits. Listen and inspect the entire contained path.
4️⃣ DO NOT IGNORE EXHAUST: An exhaust leak upstream of the turbo can reduce available turbine energy and may sound similar to an intake-side leak.
5️⃣ VERIFY: Repair the proven leak and repeat the original loaded condition while comparing expected and actual boost.
COMPLAINT → COMPLETE AIR PATH → APPROVED LEAK TEST → EXHAUST ENERGY → REPAIR → LOADED VERIFY.
⚠️ Do not exceed manufacturer charge-air test pressure. Secure test equipment and stay clear of hot or rotating components.
