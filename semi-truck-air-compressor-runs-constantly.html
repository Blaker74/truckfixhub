Semi Truck Air Compressor Runs Constantly?

If the compressor appears to stay loaded or the system cycles excessively, determine whether the truck is consuming air, failing to reach its governed range or having a control problem.

Safety first

Abnormal air-system behavior can indicate leakage or a control fault. Verify pressure and warning-system operation using manufacturer-approved procedures before continued operation.

Clues that matter

  • Does system pressure reach its normal governed range?
  • Does the air dryer purge normally?
  • How quickly does pressure fall after unloading?
  • Are there audible leaks?
  • Does the behavior change when service brakes are applied?
  • Is air demand unusually high from another system?

Potential diagnostic areas

Depending on the system, excessive compressor duty can be related to air leakage, governor/control issues, dryer faults, plumbing problems, high accessory air demand or compressor/unloader problems. Confirm the actual duty cycle and system specifications before condemning a component.

Do not defeat the controls

Do not bypass the governor, dryer or warning devices to keep the truck operating. These systems require correct diagnosis and repair.
