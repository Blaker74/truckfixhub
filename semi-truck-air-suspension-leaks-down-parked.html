📉 AIR SUSPENSION LEAKS DOWN PARKED

The pattern of what drops is a map to the leak.

1️⃣ RECORD THE PATTERN: One corner, one axle/group or the whole suspension? Record starting height/pressure condition and how long the drop takes.
2️⃣ LOCAL VS SHARED: One corner suggests a local branch first. Multiple bags dropping together points toward plumbing or controls shared by those bags.
3️⃣ LEAK TEST: Use an approved leak-detection method on bags, fittings, lines, valves and connections. Flex/movement-related leaks may require inspecting the suspension through its normal travel.
4️⃣ CONTROL VALVES: A height-control or dump circuit can leak internally or exhaust when it should not. Follow the exact system’s isolation/test procedure before replacing a valve.
5️⃣ REPAIR THE PROVEN LEAK: Do not replace every bag or valve because the truck sits low by morning.
6️⃣ REPEAT THE PARKED TEST: Restore the same starting condition and confirm the previous leak-down pattern is gone.
MAP WHAT DROPS → LOCAL OR SHARED → LEAK TEST → CONTROL / DUMP CIRCUIT → REPAIR PROVEN SOURCE → REPEAT TEST.
⚠️ A leaking suspension can lower the chassis without warning. Support the vehicle mechanically before working beneath it.
