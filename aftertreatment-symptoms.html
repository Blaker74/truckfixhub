What is the aftertreatment system doing?

Start with the warning or behavior you actually have. Save active fault codes before clearing them.

⛔Regen Won't StartParked regen request is denied, inhibited or never begins.🔁Regens Too OftenRegeneration completes but returns unusually soon or soot loading rises quickly.💧DEF WarningDEF level, quality, dosing, pressure, temperature or related warning is active.🧪SCR / NOx FaultNOx conversion, inlet/outlet sensor or SCR efficiency-related complaint.🐢Derate / Speed LimitPower or vehicle speed is electronically limited. Read the active SPN/FMI trail first.🧱DPF RestrictionHigh soot/load warning, restriction concern or regen cannot recover the filter.
Diagnostic rule: A failed regen is often the result of another condition, not the root failure. Check active faults, enable conditions, sensor plausibility, temperatures, soot/load information and the upstream engine complaint before replacing aftertreatment parts.
Safety: Exhaust and aftertreatment components can reach extremely high temperatures during regeneration. Keep combustible material clear and follow OEM procedures for parked regeneration, testing and service.
