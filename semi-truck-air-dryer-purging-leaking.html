Hearing the dryer does not prove the dryer caused the problem.

💥 One purge after pressure builds

A purge event can be part of normal air-dryer operation when the compressor/control system reaches the appropriate state. Do not replace the dryer just because it makes a purge sound.

🔁 It purges again and again

Ask why the compressor is cycling again. A leak somewhere else in the truck or trailer can make the system reload repeatedly, which can make the dryer purge repeatedly too.

💨 Air leaks continuously near the dryer

First prove the exact leak location. Then separate the purge mechanism, dryer/control plumbing and other connected air circuits. A sound near the dryer is not enough evidence to replace the complete dryer assembly.

💧 Water or oil is showing up

Record what was found, where it was found, how much, weather conditions and dryer/compressor service history. Moisture or contamination is useful evidence, but the exact cause must still be proven.

🔧 Started right after service?

If the problem began after dryer, cartridge, compressor, governor/control or air-line work, inspect the changed connections and disturbed components first. Timing after a repair is evidence.

Normal purge, repeated purge and continuous leakage are three different observations. Start there before naming a failed part.

🛑 SAFETY: Air-pressure loss can affect braking. Do not operate a truck that cannot maintain safe air pressure or has an unresolved air-brake warning. Never crawl beneath an unsecured vehicle or bypass an air-system safety device. Exact pressure settings, control tests and service procedures must be verified for the exact truck and air-system components.
