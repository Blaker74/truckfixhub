TRUCKFIXHUB AIR SYSTEM GUIDE

Air Dryer Keeps Purging or Leaking Air?

A normal purge event and an air-system problem are not the same thing. If the dryer purges repeatedly, leaks continuously or the truck struggles to maintain air pressure, record exactly what the system is doing before replacing parts.

Air-brake safety comes first

Loss of air pressure can affect braking and vehicle safety. Do not operate a truck that cannot maintain the required safe air pressure or has an unresolved air-brake warning. Never crawl beneath an unsecured vehicle or bypass an air-system safety device. Follow the truck and brake-system manufacturer's procedures.

Which symptom do you have?

  • One purge after the system reaches pressure
  • Purges repeatedly every short interval
  • Continuous air leaking from the dryer or purge area
  • Air pressure drops after each purge and recovers slowly
  • Compressor seems to cycle frequently
  • Truck will not build full air pressure
  • Moisture or contamination is noticed in the air system
  • Problem began after dryer, compressor, governor or air-line service

Common areas technicians investigate

The exact air system varies by truck and component manufacturer, but diagnosis may include:

  • Air-dryer purge valve and sealing condition
  • Governor and compressor cut-in/cut-out control behavior
  • Control and signal lines
  • Leaks elsewhere in the truck or trailer air system that cause frequent compressor cycling
  • Compressor performance when the truck also builds air slowly
  • Check valves and related air-system components where applicable
  • Air-dryer cartridge/service condition according to the component manufacturer's procedure
  • Recent repairs, incorrect connections or damaged lines

Dryer purges too often

Frequent purging can be related to frequent compressor cycling. An air leak elsewhere in the system may cause the compressor to reload repeatedly, so the dryer should not automatically be blamed simply because the purge is what the driver hears.

Air leaks continuously at the dryer

Continuous leakage at or near the purge area deserves diagnosis of the dryer, purge mechanism, control system and related air circuits. Confirm the actual leak location before replacing the dryer assembly.

Truck will not build air pressure

If the truck also builds pressure slowly or cannot reach normal operating pressure, the diagnostic path becomes broader. Compressor output, major leaks, dryer/control components, lines, valves and other air-system concerns may need to be separated with proper testing.

Problem began after service

If the symptom started immediately after an air-dryer cartridge, dryer assembly, compressor, governor or air-line repair, tell the technician exactly what was changed. Connections and disturbed components should be checked before unrelated parts are condemned.

Do not adjust or bypass the system by guesswork

TruckFixHub provides educational troubleshooting information. Air-brake and compressor-control systems are safety-critical and vary by application. Use the correct manufacturer procedure and qualified heavy-duty service practices for pressure testing, adjustment and repair.
