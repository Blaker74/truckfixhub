🦶 AIR LEAK WHEN BRAKE IS APPLIED

If the leak appears only during service-brake application, focus on circuits that become pressurized or change state during that application.

1️⃣ SECURE VEHICLE: Chock/support correctly before stationary brake testing.
2️⃣ CONFIRM CONDITION: Released, light application, full application, held application? Record when leakage begins.
3️⃣ LOCALIZE SOUND / FLOW: Treadle/foot valve area, service lines, relay valves, chambers, fittings, tractor protection/trailer circuits can all require inspection depending on system design.
4️⃣ TRACTOR VS TRAILER: If connected to a trailer, isolate the complaint using approved procedures. Do not assume a tractor valve when the leak is downstream.
5️⃣ MEASURE: Use the applicable air-loss and component test specifications rather than deciding from sound alone.
RELEASED OR APPLIED? → LOCALIZE → TRACTOR/TRAILER → COMPONENT TEST → PROVE → REPAIR → AIR-LOSS VERIFICATION.
⚠️ Never crawl beneath an unsecured air-suspended vehicle. Air-brake repairs require complete functional verification before road use.
