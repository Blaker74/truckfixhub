🧰 Five Power-Path Drawers

1 · CLUTCH CONNECTS POWERA clutch can slip, fail to release, engage poorly or make noise. Pedal feel alone does not identify the failed component.
2 · TRANSMISSION CHOOSES THE RATIOShift complaints can involve mechanical parts, clutch operation, controls, air, electrical systems or programming.
3 · DRIVESHAFT CARRIES ROTATING POWERU-joints, slip joints, yokes, center bearings, phasing, angles and balance can all affect vibration and durability.
4 · DIFFERENTIAL DIVIDES TORQUENoise patterns under pull, coast and steady load can help locate carrier, bearing and gear problems.
5 · AXLES DELIVER TORQUE TO THE WHEELSAxle shafts, splines and wheel-end interfaces finish the path. Tire and wheel-end problems can imitate driveline trouble.

🔍 Let The Symptom Pick The Drawer

📳 VIBRATES AT ROAD SPEEDRecord speed range and whether vibration changes with throttle, coast or gear.
💥 CLUNKS TAKING OFFInspect the whole torque path for looseness or excessive movement instead of replacing the first U-joint you see.
⚙️ HARD TO SHIFTSeparate clutch-release trouble from transmission, linkage/control, air or electronic causes.
🦶 CLUTCH SLIPSConfirm engine speed rises without matching vehicle response. Check control, adjustment and installation evidence.
🔊 HOWLS / WHINESNote whether noise occurs under pull, coast or all the time. Location, lubricant, bearings and gears matter.
🛑 BANG / SNAP / NO MOVEMENTStop and identify where torque transfer was lost. Inspect safely for mechanical damage.

🔧 The Diagnostic Drawer Never Changes

SEE ITRecord when it happens: speed, gear, engine load, acceleration, coast, turn or takeoff.
NAME ITPlace the evidence along the power path: clutch, transmission, driveshaft, differential, axle, wheel end or tire.
TEST ITInspect movement, play, mounting, lubricant, joint condition, angles and commanded/actual operation using the correct procedure.
PROVE ITCorrect the supported cause, reproduce the original condition and verify the symptom is gone.

⚠️ ROTATING DRIVELINE COMPONENTS ARE DANGEROUS

Never work beneath or near an unsupported vehicle or exposed rotating driveline. Exact driveline angles, fastener torque, clutch adjustment, lubricant, transmission procedures and axle setup are application-specific. Verify current manufacturer information before repair or adjustment.
