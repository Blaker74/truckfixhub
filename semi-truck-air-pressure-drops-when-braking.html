Some air is used to apply the brakes. The important question is what the pressure does after the application begins.

The gauge tells you what happened. It does not tell you which part failed.

⏸️ Pressure drops and then stabilizes

A pressure change can occur as the brake system fills during an application. Do not use gauge movement alone to condemn a component. Compare the behavior with the exact vehicle's approved inspection procedure and requirements.

1️⃣ One gauge behaves differently

Record whether primary, secondary or both indications change and how they recover after the pedal is released. A difference between circuits is useful evidence for narrowing the affected air path.

🚛 Trailer changes the symptom

If the pressure-loss pattern changes with a particular trailer connected, preserve that comparison. Tractor-to-trailer control plumbing, couplings and trailer brake components become part of the diagnostic path.

📏 Do not guess the pass/fail number

TruckFixHub does not use one generic internet air-loss number to certify every commercial vehicle. Exact allowable leakage, pressure and test procedures must come from the applicable current inspection criteria and manufacturer information for the vehicle configuration.
