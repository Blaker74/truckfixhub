What does FMI mean?

The SPN identifies the parameter or condition being reported; the FMI further describes how the electronic system says that condition is failing or abnormal. The exact diagnostic procedure depends on the engine, controller, calibration and vehicle configuration. Record the complete SPN/FMI combination before troubleshooting.

Before replacing a sensor or part

Record all active and inactive faults, warning lamps, derate status, operating conditions and recent repairs. Look for related codes and verify power, ground, wiring, connectors, fluid condition and system-specific measurements where applicable. Follow the correct OEM diagnostic procedure for the exact vehicle.
